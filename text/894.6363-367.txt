**PETROLEUM IMMUNITY LAW**

Informed that Department's telegram #347, Aug. 21, 1934, was communicated to Foreign Office. Gives remarks of Chief of the Far Eastern Division, who stated that after talking not to over with interested Departments he would again get in touch with the Embassy. Nothing further received.

Japanese Petroleum Monopoly and related developments and problems: Call of Mr. B. Kleijn Molekamp, Commercial Counselor of Netherland Legation and discussion of this subject with Dr. Hornbeck and Mr. Hamilton. Mr. Molekamp stated that Netherland interests that would be affected by Japanese regulations are greater than American interests.

**DIETO; Mr. Walter C. Teagle, President, Standard Oil Co. of New Jersey, called Dr. Hornbeck on telephone from New York. He inquired as to protests made by the British and Netherlands Governments. Substance of information given him; this Govt. expects initiative by way of proposals to come from British or Netherlands Governments.**

**DIETO: Telephone conversation between Mr. Teagle and Dr. Hornbeck. Mr. Teagle inquired again whether we had information of protests made by British and Netherlands Governments. He was told that we had not been informed. Later, he called again and was given briefly, the information given Dr. Hornbeck in the morning by the Commercial Attache of the Netherlands Legation. Mr. Teagle hoped the Dept. would protest. This phase of the matter was discussed.**

Aide Memoire left with Minister for Foreign Affairs by the Netherlands Minister: Gives main points of -, and states text will be sent by mail.

**Japanese Petroleum Industry Law:** Remarks made by Acting Chief Far Eastern Dept. British Foreign Office, in which he told of action taken by British Ambassador in Tokyo, and stated he was understood the Dutch Government would authorize their Minister. At Tokyo to take similar action. Foreign Office suggests that in view of our similar interests the State Dept. might desire to instruct American Ambassador at Tokyo to similar action.
