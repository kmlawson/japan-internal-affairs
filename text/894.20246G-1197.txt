AMERICAN CONSULATE GENERAL
Hong Kong, June 24, 1940

CONFIDENTIAL.

SUBJECT: Japanese Propaganda in Hong Kong - Anonymous Letter Listing Chinese Notables to be Expelled.

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to enclose herewith, as of possible interest to the Department, copy of an anonymous letter addressed to various newspapers in Hong Kong and listing eighteen well-known Chinese who should be expelled from this Colony because they are "anti-Japanese elements". Reliable opinion here considers the letter of Japanese or Wang Ching-wei origin.

This letter has been received by the few leading newspapers published in English in Hong Kong but none of them have so far mentioned it in their columns. The enclosed copy has come to me from the Editor of the SOUTH CHINA MORNING POST. The names as written in the enclosure herewith are believed to be sufficiently plain to enable the Department's experts to identify them. Number 10 should, it is thought, be Soong Ai-ling, who is Madame H. H. Kung. Number 7, K. S. Wu, is understood not to spend as much time in Hong Kong as do some of the others. T. V. Soong is, as the Department already knows, now either in the United States or en route there by Clipper.

Very respectfully,

Addison E. Southard
American Consul General

Post Office Box No. 14
June 21st, 1940

To the Editors of all the English Press in Hong Kong

Sirs,

For the safety of Hongkong, it is the necessary step to start a drive of chasing out all the anti-Japanese elements in the colony. The following is a list of those Chungking agents in town:

1. T. V. Soong --- Financial work
2. Doo Yu-wen --- guerilla warfare
3. We Teh Chen --- Obtaining HK Government protection for Chungking agent's illegal acts
4. C. T. Wang --- Raising fund for war chest under name of Red Cross Association
5. Wang Shao-lai --- In secret service, masked as a philanthropist
6. Andrew Lee --- Agent of CEC, in charge of Intelligence
7. K. S. Wu --- has headquarter here for anti-Japanese work in Shanghai, Swatow, Canton and Macao
8. Li Kong-cho --- financial work
9. O. K. Yui --- financial and secret service work
10. Soong Eh-lin --- giving orders and instructions to all local women clubs for anti-Japanese work
11. Pei Tsui-yee --- financial work
12. Chieh Shing-tse -- financial work
13. Yih Tsong-tong --- financial work
14. Kuo Chia-tong --- guerilla warfare
15. Tehang Yih-chu --- Intelligence
16. T. A. Soong --- War chest
17. Chu Chi-kong --- propaganda
18. Yang Yao-kuen --- propaganda

Yours truly,

A Native Born of Hongkong
