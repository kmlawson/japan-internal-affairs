Tokyo's despatch No. 1707 of March 5
gives a detailed account of the trial of
Lieutenant-Colonel Aizawa for the murder
of Major-General Nagata on August 12, last.

As FE is acquainted with the major
facts in the case and with the part which
the trial played in bringing on the recent
military outbreak in Tokyo, the despatch
is chiefly useful for purposes of reference,
and need not be read.

The despatch shows clearly that the
trial of Colonel Aizawa was of the nature
of a "propaganda trial". There was never
any question of Aizawa's guilt, since this
was admitted by him from the beginning.
The important aspect of the case was the
revelation of the political ideas enter-
tained by a section of the Army. The
testimony given is evidence of the in-
credible patriotic ideology of a certain
section of the Japanese army.

The Embassy comments that perhaps the
most significant development of the trial
is the revelation of a desire held by
various army officers and persons outside
the army for a "renovation of the nation",
otherwise
