# TRANSLATION

(News item - EXCALSION - February 26, 1932)

JAPANESE AGENTS AT MAZATLAN, SINALOA
---
They propose to acquire foodstuffs to supply the (fighting) forces of their country
---

Very encouraging private reports were received yesterday in this Capital from Mazatlán to the effect that at that port on the Pacific and other points in the States of Sinaloa and Sonora have arrived commercial agents with a view to making contracts for enormous quantities of foodstuffs, especially shrimp, dried tomatoes, salt fish, chick-pea, etc., to supply the forces of the Japanese Army engaged in the campaign in China.

These reports, coming from reliable sources, for they were sent by merchants to houses with whom they deal in this City, state that no less than twenty-five agents have arrived in those regions from the United States, for the business is being done through business houses (almacenistas) established in the United States for the purpose stated.

The report in question and the presence in Mazatlán of one of these agents will cause the farming and fishing industries on the Pacific coast to rejoice, for it will not be long before those regions of Mexico will be flooded with money received for their products sent to supply the Japanese Army, a great part of the supplies for which will be secured from our territory.
