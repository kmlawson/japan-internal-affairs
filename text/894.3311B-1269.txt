The Secretary of State.
Sir:
Reference is made to State Department letter
PR 894.3311B/136 dated May 27, 1941 regarding a
proposed visit of the Japanese merchant marine
training vessel Kaio Maru to Davao, P.I. from June
11 to June 29,1941.
From a strictly naval point of view there would
appear to be no objection to the proposed visit as
such; however, it is considered that the matter
should be decided upon its merit with regard to
higher policy. If a visit should be approved it is
considered that a period of not over one week should
be stipulated. The Navy Department can visualize
no legitimate occasion for such a visit to remain
for 18 days. Permitting a short visit of a ship is
a matter considerably different from prmitting her
to utilize a port for training activities during a
period of nearly 3 weeks.
Should the Japanese Government pursue, in their
request for an 18 day visit, the Navy Department
recommends that the U.S. Government inform them that
the visit is inconvenient at the present time.
The question of the visa crew list is not a matter
under the cognizance of the Navy Department.
(s) J. Forrestal
