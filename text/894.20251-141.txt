Subject: Kokushi Kishida
Rate Translated: Time
Source and Degree of Reliability:
Anatole Kolpakoff, born in Petrograd, Russia, 1917. Moved
to Harbin, Manchuria, with parents in 1920. Grew up there
and became concert pianist. Lt. Gen. Ando Rinzo befriended
Communication of Report: him, wanted to adopt him, and introduced
him intimately in the General's home to Japanese officials.
He lived in Tokio between December, 1937 and January, 1941.
Arrived in San Francisco February 6, 1941. He has his first
citizenship papers and expresses deep desire to become a
US citizen. Parents are still living in Harbin, but he is
first-string radio. In Comments on Current Events Enter Item Headings Now announcer for OWI
short-wave broadcasts to Japan from San Francisco. Speaks
fluent Japanese without accent, has good command of English.
Tells a good story, but doesn't seem to exaggerate. OWI
says reliable. Complete security check reveals nothing
detrimental about him.
Subject stated as follows:
"Kokushi Kishida is an outstanding Japanese liberal
who specializes on France. He studied in France, has a
perfect knowledge of the French language and literature,
and has done a great deal of translating of French books
into Japanese.
"He is about 45, has a wife and two daughters of
about 10 and 12. He expressed very liberal ideas for
Jap, and is considered an outstanding personality in
Japanese cultural and scientific circles."
