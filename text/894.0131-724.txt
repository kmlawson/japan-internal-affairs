The Woman's Civil Rights Bill, intended to give municipal electoral rights to women over twenty years of age, was defeated in the House of Peers on March 24th, after being passed by the House of Representatives. The bill was satisfactory neither to the ardent supporters of the woman suffrage movement nor to the opponents of this movement. The defeat of the bill, therefore, was a cause for rejoicing among both factions. The suffragists claim that the bill was merely a half-hearted measure granting women certain rights in municipal election and they will not be satisfied until they obtain for women the complete prefectural franchise that they are working for.
