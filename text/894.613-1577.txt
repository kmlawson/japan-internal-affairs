My dear Mr. Hankel:

This is in reply to your letter of recent date in which you inquire about securing seed from the Yokohama Nursery Co. Ltd.

At present it is not possible to permit private trading relations between Japanese firms or individuals and those of other countries. All exports from Japan will be handled by some official government agency acting for the country of destination. For goods exported to the United States the U.S. Commercial Company, with offices at 811 Vermont Avenue, N.W., Washington D. C., has been designated to handle all transactions.

If you could supply the U. S. Commercial Company with a detailed list of the seeds you desire to purchase I think it would be possible for that agency to ascertain from the Supreme Commander's Headquarters in Japan whether they are available for export.

I am sending an information copy of this letter to the U.S. Commercial Company.

Sincerely yours,

Roswell H. Whitman
Acting Chief
Japanese and Korean Affairs
Division

Mr. Allen J. Hankel, Prop.,
Alanwold Nursery,
Nashaminy, Bucks Co., Pa.
