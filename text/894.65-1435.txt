3951, June 20, 2 p.m.

Legation has just received March 1944 issue TOKIO ORIENTAL ECONOMIST containing list of Japanese companies designated on February 10 as munitions companies within the meaning of the Japanese law of January 17, 1944.

List gives full name in English of each company, its authorized and paid-up capitalization and name of official responsible for production but does not give location any plants. ~~Japanese Charge d'Affaires~~ whether list should be cabled.

HARRISON.
