Office of the President
Tokyo, Japan.

January, 1936.

Main. Civilian,
Munshion,
Tokyo.

My dear Munshion Daville:

Our University is going to present to Harvard University a collection of archaeological specimens of Japan, which are being carefully selected and packed in four wooden boxes, as to come a note that distinguished occasion of our contemporary celebration in this coming fall. We are naturally very anxious to have the reason to the destination in perfect safety, may be asked for soon office a special favour to assist us in obtaining from your Government a permit for the examination of the custom in section on the goods above mentioned. For your reference, I enclosed herein a copy of the list of the said specimens. They will be shipped by the freighter Hokkai-Meru of Osaka Shosen Kaisha which is scheduled to sail from Yokohama on July 11, and arrive in Boston on August 13, via Los Angeles, Panama, and New York. Dimensions and weights of the boxes are described in the following page.

We feel exceedingly grateful for your kind attention in this matter and wait for your favourable reply,

Yours very sincerely,

(?) S. Eguchi.

Secretary.
