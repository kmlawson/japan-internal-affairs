Tokyo, June 14, 1931.
No. 245.

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

With reference to the Department's instruction No. 95 dated April 9, 1931, I have the honor to state that I am informed by the secretary to Viscount Kikujiro Ishii that the "Diplomatic Memoirs" (Gaike Yoroku) of the latter are now being translated, but that no date can be set for the completion of the work nor have arrangements been made for publishing the translated work.

Respectfully yours,

Edwin L. Neville,
Charge d'Affaires ad interim.

WRH/cr
