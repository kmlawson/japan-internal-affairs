IN ALMOST EVERY DUTCH CLASSROOM you will find brown-faced, black-eyed children of mixed Dutch-Indonesian origin. These thousands of children, all over Holland, are a living proof of the solid ties between the peoples of the Netherlands and Indonesia.

There was a time when the Dutch East Indies were merely a colony. No honest Dutchman will deny the Dutch East Indies have in times past been a source of wealth to the „motherland". On the other hand the peoples of Indonesia have equally profited from Dutch enterprise, Dutch medicine, Dutch administration (not so long ago still highly praised in the world as „exemplary"). Dutch doctors dedicated their lives to an unending battle against malicious tropical disease; Dutch engineers built proud waterworks, railroads, cities and factories. Dutch scientists had their share in bettering the crops, Dutch teachers brought knowledge to thousands of analphabetic Indonesians. The population steadily increased under Dutch rule.
