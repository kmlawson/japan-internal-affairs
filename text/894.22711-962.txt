Department of State,

Sirs:
All America Cables, Inc. is proposing to purchase some submarine cable in Japan and I propose to proceed immediately to Japan to inspect the manufacture of this cable. I am informed that it will be necessary for the Consulate in Osaka to provide a certificate showing the object of my visit in order for me to obtain permission from the Japanese Government to enter the factory for the necessary inspection.
My passport is No. 75477. It was issued on January 19, 1934 and renewed on January 30, 1936.
Please wire the Consul at Osaka, at our expense, requesting him to supply the necessary consular certificate to Mr. T. Obata of the Sumitomo Electric Wire and Cable Works, Ltd. in Osaka, so that he can proceed at once to obtain the necessary governmental permit which will allow me to commence work on my arrival in Japan, without delay.
We would appreciate your advising us when you have taken the action requested.
Very truly yours,
ALL AMERICA CABLES, INC.
John K. Roosevelt
Vice President.

ANEMBASSY,
TOKYO (JAPAN).

John K. Roosevelt, Vice President of All America Cables, Incorporated, New York, informs the Department that he expects to proceed shortly to Osaka to inspect the manufacture of submarine cable to be purchased by his company from the Sumitomo Electric Company Wire and Cable Works. He states that it will be necessary for the Consulate to supply to Mr. T. Obata of Sumitomo a QUOTE certificate UNQUOTE showing the object of his proposed inspection in order to obtain permission from the authorities to enter the factory without delay upon his arrival in Japan.

Please ask Osaka to render appropriate assistance and if no objection is perceived to furnish Obata an appropriate letter setting forth the data required by the authorities.
