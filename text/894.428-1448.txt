# EXPLANATION

The following is a bibliography of economic, military and scientific material involving Japan available at certain libraries throughout the United States.

The bibliography was compiled from lists submitted by the various libraries. The items included were arbitrarily picked from the lists submitted. The selection of items was based solely upon the information given by the libraries and the criteria for determining selections were topic and date of publication. No opportunity was afforded to check any of the items.

Most of the materials listed are in English. Wherever possible titles in Japanese were translated and both English and romaji are given. In all cases of Japanese titles romaji is used.

Individual cards were made for each item giving all of the information available from the lists. In the upper right-hand corner or along the right margin are listed numbers which indicate the library or libraries at which each item is available. For the more common references which are obtainable at many or most institutions the words "and others" appear after a library number. The "Numerical List of Libraries" is, therefore, the key to the entire bibliography.

The bibliography included many Japanese journals, bulletins, memoirs, etc. It was not possible to indicate the issues of each periodical available at each library, so that the library number on such items merely indicates that unspecified issues are available.

No United States Government Printing Office publications are included in this bibliography.

The cards have been arranged by topic and/or title, and in alphabetical order. There is no cross referencing.

R. L. Tuthill

Many items are available in many of the other libraries on this list though that is not indicated in the cards.
