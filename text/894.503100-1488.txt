=== Page 3 === (confidence 0.99)
May 23

Contains little row or interesting except for the estimate of former president Takasaki of the Manchuria Heavy Industry Development Co. that only 10 percent of the original value of Japanese investments in Manchuria remains, impliedly more due to mob looting than Soviet removals. His statements are somewhat confused but he (Takasaki) seems to envision the retention of considerable numbers of Japanese to help rebuild Manchurian industry.
