Mar 31, 1943

In reply refer to
FE

My dear Mr. Kornely:

Your letter of May 23, 1943 to Senator Alexander Wiley of Wisconsin has been referred to this Department for reply.

The only information available to the Department in regard to the death and burial of Admiral Isoroku Yamamoto is that obtained from reports of radio broadcasts from Tokyo. A brief summary of such information is as follows: Admiral Yamamoto's death occurred in an airplane; following his death numerous honors were bestowed upon him, including several conferred by the Emperor of Japan; Admiral Yamamoto's ashes arrived in Tokyo on May 23 and are lying in state at the Navy Club in Tokyo, where they will remain until the day of the funeral, June 5, which is the anniversary of the funeral of Fleet Admiral Togo, naval hero of the Russo-Japanese War; the ashes of the late Admiral Yamamoto will be buried next to the grave of Admiral Togo; and Admiral Mineichi Koga was appointed to succeed Admiral Yamamoto as Commander in Chief of the Japanese Combined Imperial Fleets.

The Department can, of course, assume no responsibility for the accuracy of the foregoing information.

Sincerely yours,

For the Secretary of State:
