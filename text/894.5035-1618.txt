My dear Mr. Bibb:

This is in answer to your letter of 19 February asking for a copy of FEC-230. This document, which refers to anti-trust policy rather than economic policy as a whole in Japan, is still under consideration by the Far Eastern Commission. Until that consideration is concluded, the document cannot be released to the public.

If and when the Far Eastern Commission does approve this or some other anti-trust policy for Japan, it will be made public and a copy will be sent to you promptly.

Sincerely yours,

Roswell H. Whitman
Acting Chief
Division of Occupied Areas
Economic Affairs

Mr. Eugene Sharp Bibb
Counsellor at Law
60 East 42nd Street
New York 17, N. Y.
