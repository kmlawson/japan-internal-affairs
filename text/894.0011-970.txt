3) Japanese Prince arrives in Canada.

Prince Chichibu, brother of the Emperor of Japan, and his consort arrived in Quebec on September 23 after a five months' visit in Europe. They are returning to Japan.

The Royal couple were greeted upon their arrival by the Minister of Justice, Mr. Nageinta and Mr. M. K. Keenley side, representing the Department of External Affairs, as well as other officials, including the Japanese Charge d'Affaires.

Originally the Prince's plans called for a visit to Ottawa but a last minute plan presented his carrying out this project.

On September 25 the Royal party left by train for Montreal, Montreal and Niagara Falls, at each of which "informal stops" were made as route to the Pacific coast.
