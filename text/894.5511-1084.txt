1939 MAR 1 PM 3 27

SUBJECT: REPATRIATION OF AMERICANS OF JAPANESE RACE

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

As the Department is aware, this Consulate has been interested in observing the problems of young native-born Americans of Japanese race who have been living in Japan. Since the outbreak of the "Incident" in July 1937, I have been trying to observe its effects upon this group of Americans. Reports have recently occurred in the newspapers to the effect that increasing numbers of Americans of Japanese race are coming to Japan from the United States - due either to anti-Japanese feeling in the United States, which is directed against them or their families, or to their wish to serve in the Japanese military forces.

If the Department sees no objection, it is requested that the Bureau of Immigration at Washington be asked to send me available statistics concerning the entries
