# AMERICAN CONSULATE
Saigon, French Indochina, October 20, 1938

## Confidential

**SUBJECT:** Raid on Large Japanese Firm

**THE HONORABLE**
**THE SECRETARY OF STATE,**
**WASHINGTON.**

SIR:

I have the honor to inform the Department that under the direction of the military authorities the premises of Dainan Koosi, the largest Japanese firm in Saigon except Mitsui, were raided on the afternoon of October 18, 1938. No notices that the raid took place have appeared in the newspapers, but it is known that the premises were searched and that a large number of documents were seized. These latter are in the process of being translated.

Although the firm and its present manager have been at Saigon for about twenty years, the cause of the seizure of its archives is believed to be nothing other than espionage. This case follows immediately the arrest of the editor of the newspaper "L'Alerte" on other charges, but with a sure indication that the matter of his alleged assistance of Japanese agents will be brought out. The editor of "L'Alerte" is a French citizen of mixed blood.

Respectfully yours,

Peter H.A. Flood
American Consul
