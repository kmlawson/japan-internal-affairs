MEMORANDUM.
Japanese propaganda in the Netherlands Indies
has been for some time directed at discouragement and
at convincing the public - whenever possible by state-
ments of Allied statesmen or military leaders, quoted
in fragments or distorted - that there is not the
slightest possibility of Japan's position in the
Archipelago being challenged by the Allies and that
the Allies have acknowledged this.
This form of propaganda, coupled with brutal
intimidation of all those who offer resistance and
with careful consideration of the personal feelings of
the masses and of leaders in Java (in contrast to many
of the other islands), seems to obtain results.
Up till the beginning of this year there were
