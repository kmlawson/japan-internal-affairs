EMBASSY OF INDIA
Washington, D.C.
11th January, 1947

My dear Whitman,

Subject: Quinine Taken from Japanese Army
and Navy --Required by the Government
of India

Will you kindly refer to Baig's letter F.166/46
dated 31st July, 1946, on the above subject?

As the Government of India are anxious to have
the quinine as early as possible, I shall be grateful if you
would let me know how the matter stands.

Yours sincerely,
P. A. Menon

Mr. Roswell Whitman,
State Department,
Division of Japanese and Korean Economic Affairs,
1818 "H" Street, N.W.,
Washington, D.C.

In reply refer to
JK
JAN 23 1947

My dear Mr. Menon:

I have your letter of January 11, 1947 renewing your request for information on the availability of quinine in Japan.

A cable was sent to the Supreme Commander in August 1946 and a reply was received in September, in which it was stated that there was no information at the time on the availability of quinine, but that a survey was being made and that we would be further advised. Unfortunately, no word has come since that time. In my opinion the original report by the Economic and Scientific Section was erroneous, though perhaps there is some quinine in other forms. A follow-up message will be sent to Tokyo.

I am sorry that this matter has taken so long, but will try to obtain an answer shortly.

Sincerely yours,

Roswell H. Whitman
Associate Chief
Division of Japanese and Korean
Economic Affairs

Mr. P. A. Menon,
Embassy of India,
Washington, D. C.

MAR 21 1947

In reply refer to
JK

My dear Mr. Menon:

With reference to Mr. Whitman's letter to you of January 23, 1947 concerning quinine stocks in Japan I have just received the reply to our inquiry.

It is stated that the Supreme Commander considers the quinine stocks on hand to be looted property and not available for export under the terms of FEC 011/12, Restitution of Looted Property. I believe that you will agree with this interpretation, though I am sorry to have to give you such unsatisfactory news after you have waited so long for a reply.

Sincerely yours,

Edwin M. Martin
Chief
Division of Japanese and Korean Economic Affairs

Mr. P. A. Menon,
Embassy of India,
Washington, D.C.
