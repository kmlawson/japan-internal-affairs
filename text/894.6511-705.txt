The problem of selecting Anshan, in Manchuria, or Shin Gishu, in Chosen, as the site for the proposed Showa Steel Works seemed at the close of the month approaching solution". Doctor Songshu, President of the South Manchuria Railway Company, returned to Tokyo near the end of June and on the 26th had a conversation in this regard with the Minister for Overseas Affairs. The following day the press reported that they had practically arrived at a decision to establish the works at Anshan, due in part to the favorable treatment which the works would receive at this site with respect to Government subsidies and tariff charges. The Showa Steel Works, the reports concluded, will be established as a collateral company of the South Manchuria Railway Company with a capital of Yen 100,000,000 and a capacity of 500,000 tons. Any definite developments in this matter will be immediately reported to the Department by despatch.
