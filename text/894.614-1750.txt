FROM: Tokyo

TO: Secretary of State

NO: 78, March 30

Reference Department's telegram 134, March 25, 1948.

Japanese Ministry of Agriculture estimated 1948 production of Houtaka (Hontonka) red peppers 800,000 pounds, approximately 50% of which will be available for export at f.o.b. Japan price approximately 17 cents US per pound.

1948 production of Yatsufusa (Tatsufea) red pepper estimated 2,200,000 pounds, approximately 50% of which will be available for export at f.o.b. Japan price approximately 14 cents US per pound.

Above listed export prices reported to be maximum prices and may be subject to future reduction.

Ministry of Agriculture estimates 1948 production of other varieties red pepper 440 metric tons; however, no production or export price information available regarding individual types.

FROM: USPOLAD, Tokyo.
Date of mailing: July 30, 1949.
Rec'd: August 1, 1949, 4:24 PM

Reference letter of May 18, 1949, from Associate Director, Office of Foreign Agricultural Relations, Department of Agriculture (transmitted to this Mission by Division of Foreign Reporting Services under date of June 13, 1949), regarding report on potatoes and sweet potatoes, following recently received from Natural Resources Section, General Headquarters, SCAP

Information requested by Office of Foreign Agricultural Relations, U.S. Dept. of Agriculture, relative to sweet potato and white potato production for 1949 will be forwarded to the Department of Army via radio as soon as these estimates are released by the Japanese Government. Radio messages will include instructions for passing all crop statistics to Office of Foreign Agricultural Relations.

Production date on the 1949 crop of white potatoes will be forwarded before 1 October as requested. Estimated production of sweet potatoes in 1949 will not be available until 10 October, however, and will be forwarded to Washington via radio immediately thereafter.

SEBALD
