April 12, 4 p.m.

Japanese consular representative has confirmed press reports that the Bank of Taiwan is making arrangements to hand over to the Central Bank of China its silver holdings in Canton and Swatow totaling about $500,000.

Sent to the Department; Peicing, Nanking.

DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS

October 14, 1935.

The Taiwan Government General is sponsoring a plan to organize a semi-official corporation to be known as the Taiwan Colonization Company, which will encourage and assist colonization of lands in Taiwan, and will engage in other activities.

Another measure contemplated by the Government is the re-establishment of the Foreign Affairs Section of the Government General after a lapse of ten years.
