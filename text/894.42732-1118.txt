SAo Paulo, Brazil, July 24, 1939.

Subject: Japanese Propaganda

The Honorable
Jefferson Caffery,
American Ambassador,
Rio de Janeiro.

My dear Mr. Ambassador:

The papers of Saturday carried an announcement of the Japanese Consul General, Mr. Junzo Sakané, that the Department of Tourism of the Ministry of Railway Transportation of Japan has decided to invite five University professors from Brazil - including one representative of the Faculty of Law and one of the Faculty of Medicine of the University of São Paulo - to visit Japan.

Apparently the free courses in Japanese offered in the Law School of the University of São Paulo are not meeting with success, if one may judge by the efforts being made to attract students.

In this connection it is perhaps of interest to note that according to the Folha da Noite and the section chief in charge of private instruction in the Directory of Teaching there 47 Japanese teachers registered in São Paulo.

Respectfully yours,

Carol H. Foster
American Consul General

Sao Paulo, Brazil, August 9, 1939.

Subject: Paulista Professors Invited to Japan.

The Honorable
Jefferson Caffery,
American Ambassador,
Rio de Janeiro.

My dear Mr. Ambassador:

The professors referred to in my letter of July 24, 1939, have been selected. They are Dr. Geraldo de Paula Souza of the Faculty of Medicine and Dr. Soares de Mello of the Faculty of Law, both of the University of Sao Paulo. In Rio they will join other university professors going to Japan as guests of the Japanese government.

Professor de Paula Souza as head of the Institute of Hygiene has had a great deal to do with the Rockefeller Foundation.

Professor Soares de Mello represented Brazil last year at the Congress of Criminology in Rome.

Very truly yours,

Carol H. Foster
American Consul General
