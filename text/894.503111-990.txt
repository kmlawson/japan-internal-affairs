# DEPARTMENT OF STATE
## ADVISER ON
## INTERNATIONAL ECONOMIC AFFAIRS

December 17, 1937.

FE - Mr. Hamilton:

In response to the inquiry of the Secretary, transmitted through you to Mr. Feis, I am submitting the following information regarding liquid resources held by Japanese institutions and nationals in this country under this date.

1. **Earmarked Gold.** The Federal Reserve Board reports that no earmarked gold is held to the account of the Bank of Japan in this country.

2. **Bank Balances.** Mr. Lochhead of the Treasury Department reports that a check of Japanese bank balances in New York reveals an aggregate balance of $45,200,000 as of December 8. Approximately four fifths of this amount is held by the New York branch of the Yokohama Specie Bank. This indicates a marked change from the situation in mid-September, when the Yokohama Specie Bank had an overdraft of $7,400,000. At the same date other Japanese banks had credits in New York amounting to $6,400,000, leaving a net overdraft of $1,000,000. Since March, when the movement of gold from Japan to this country commenced, there has been received

3. Japanese Holdings of Dollar Securities. The Japanese Ministry of Finance reported in August that Japanese holdings of dollar securities at the end of July amounted to 361 million yen, which, at the current rate of exchange, would be equivalent to slightly over $100 million. There is reason to believe that this amount is considerably overestimated, bond holdings being presumably carried at par value. Moreover, there has probably been an appreciable liquidation of these holdings since the end of July.

The Treasury Department is at present engaged in estimating the total Japanese assets in the United States but is unable at this time to furnish definitive figures. Preliminary estimates as reported by Mr. White indicate that Japanese investments in the United States, including
