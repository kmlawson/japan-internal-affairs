December 3, 1941.

Memorandum for State Department.

894.8592

Information received from British Admiralty sources states that Japanese representatives of the Osaka Shoshen Kaisha and Yamashita Steamship Lines in Montevideo and Buenos Aires have been recalled and are returning to Japan as early as possible.
