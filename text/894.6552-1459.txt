The following is in response to Mr. Rencharu's request
for information concerning the need of the Army and Navy for
silk.
1. The Army and Navy have informed OPM that there
is more than sufficient silk in the United States to supply
all the needs of the Army and Navy for the next two years and
that they do not request that any silk be acquired from Japan.
2. In addition to the Army and Navy requirements
there is some silk for civilian purposes. OPM has a division
at work for supplying substitutes for silk and rationing the
existing silk supply. I do not believe that there is any need
for importing silk for civilian purposes.
3. Just after the freezing order the President ap-
proved instructions, one of which was that no licenses should
