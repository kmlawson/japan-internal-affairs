Warsaw, Poland.
June 26, 1934.

No. 323.

Subject: Alleged Central Japanese New Bureau for Europe in Warsaw.

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

With reference to the informal comment dated May 8, 1934, of the Chief of the Division of Eastern European Division with regard to the work of the Embassy, I have the honor to report that very careful investigation has failed to disclose the existence in Warsaw or Poland of a "Central Japanese News Bureau for Europe", or any comparable organization.

A member of the staff of the semi-official ISKRA news agency recently commented to a member of my staff

=== Page 3 === (confidence 0.97)
-2-

staff that whenever he has encountered at the Japanese Legation in Warsaw representatives of the RIENT news agency the latter invariably have appeared to be very much at home in that mission. From this it appears possible that the RIENT agency has been assumed by some uninformed observer to be a Japanese news bureau. As a matter of fact, the RIENT agency is without prestige or importance. It is operated by a certain Wassan-Girej Dzabagi who is reported to be a native of the Caucasus. Associated with him are two or three Russian emigrees. It is not listed by name in the Warsaw telephone directory, nor does it have quarters of its own. Its "office" is the apartment at Vienna 20 that is occupied by Mr. Dzabagi. The agency is reported to collect and to distribute to a limited number of purchasers, news and articles of a strongly anti-Soviet, and pro-Japanese, character. It has been in existence for several years, but the Embassy has been unable to ascertain the date on which it was founded.

In this connection it may be pertinent to remark that I have been informed by the American Military Attache in Warsaw that the office of the Japanese Military Attache in this capital is an evaluation center for information gathered by the Japanese military attachés in the Baltic States and Poland.

Respectfully yours,

John Cudahy.
