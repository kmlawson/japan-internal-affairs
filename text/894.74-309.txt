The advisers of the Temporary Industrial
Rationalization Bureau of the Department of Com-
merce and Industry met on the 23rd to consider the
rationalization of the steel industry. Certain of
them advocated the establishment of a company, a
joint undertaking of the Government and the people
resembling the South Manchuria Railway Company. It
was anticipated that a committee would be appointed
