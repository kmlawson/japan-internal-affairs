SUBJECT: Establishment of Fumigation Service, Night Quarantine Inspection, Dairen and Port Arthur, Manchuria.
PAGE 1 OF 12
AUG 19 1931
THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to inform the Department that the Marine Bureau of the Imperial Japanese Kwantung Government on July 1, 1931, established its own service for the fumigation of vessels calling at Dairen and Port Arthur, fumigation heretofore having been performed by the ships themselves under the supervision of the port authorities, and that it now issues fumigation certificates.

The Marine Bureau for the time being will perform quarantine inspection of ships arriving in port within one hour after sunset.

The following fees are charged at present for fumigating vessels in Dairen and for passing ships through quarantine after sundown:
