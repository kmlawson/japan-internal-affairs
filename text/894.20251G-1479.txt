November 20, 1944

Memorandum for: Mr. Jack D. Neal, Dept. of State

Subject: Location of Japanese Espionage Headquarters in French Indo-China

1. A source, evaluated A-2, on September 22, 1944 advised that he had received information from a reliable source to the effect that the Japanese Liaison Headquarters, with the Vichy French in French Indo-China, had been moved from Hue to Turone. This Japanese Liaison Headquarters is actually a cover for their espionage operations.

2. This information has not been transmitted to any Embassies of the United States.

Bernard A. Towell
I-2 Branch

JAN 9 1945

Lord Halifax called upon me this afternoon at his request.

Lord Halifax stated he had raised with me on November 23, the matter of Lord Mountbatten having a small number of Frenchmen at his headquarters in connection with sabotage in Indo China. I told Lord Halifax that I was under the impression that we had already given an answer to his Embassy. I then called Mr. Matthews on the telephone and asked him to determine the status of the matter, and he called me back and stated that the matter had been referred to the President and was now in his hands. I advised Lord Halifax of this. He asked if I would be good enough to raise the questions with the President the next time I saw him as they were very anxious for the answer and Mountbatten attached great importance to the matter.
