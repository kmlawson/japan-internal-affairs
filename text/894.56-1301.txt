WITH REFERENCE TO THE ATTACHED DOCUMENT FROM THE BRITISH IN REGARD TO JAPANESE REGULATIONS FOR THE DEPARTURE OF FOREIGNERS, A COMPARISON WITH THE AMERICAN REQUIREMENTS AS ASCERTAINED FROM VD AND MR. HISS OF MR. ACHESON'S OFFICE IS GIVEN SERIATIM AS FOLLOWS:

1. (a) EXIT PERMITS ARE NOT REQUIRED OF JAPANESE DEPARTING FROM THE UNITED STATES. (Regulations requiring permits for all aliens to depart have not yet been promulgated.)
(b) FOREIGN EXCHANGE PERMITS ARE REQUIRED FOR MONEYS NEEDED, BUT ARE NOT NECESSARY TO DEPART FROM THE UNITED STATES.
(c) CERTIFICATE OF COMPLIANCE WITH PAYMENT OF INCOME TAX REQUIRED, BUT NOT FOR OTHER TAXES.

There are no regulations with respect to paying of rent, staff, gratuities and retiring allowances at a specified rate.

2. (a) Permit to buy ticket not required, being covered by single permit of exchange for travel, living, etc.
(b) Only single permit required for exchange.

3. No permit to liquidate business or holdings as such required. In the majority of cases "do business" licenses which permit liquidation and carrying on business in the United States are issued.

(1) Any designated foreigner has to obtain (a) exit permit from Governor Prefect (b) foreign exchange permit, (c) if, he is a person of any substance or income he will find it almost impossible to obtain permit (1) without getting receipt in payment of taxes including income tax (for current financial year not normally assessed until April of following year) and in respect of paying of rent, staff gratuities and retiring allowance at Japanese rate etc.
