I have the honor respectfully to report to the
Department that it has come to the attention of this
Consulate that British agents, located along the west
coast of South America, have instituted a program of
slowing down the loading of Japanese merchantmen of
which there is said to be forty enroute to or now on
the Pacific coast of this continent for the purpose
of loading minerals.
The Shipping Adviser of the British Consulate at
Antofagasta, who is a reserve Lieutenant Commander in
the British Navy, has told me that he just completed
a journey to the port of Chañaral and Caldera, which
export large quantities of mineral ores, for the purpoce
of instructing stevedoring agencies in the slow down 
technique, and he appeared to be extremely satisfied
of a British national and that the delay in loading 6
Japanese vessels there may be expected to reach fifty
percent more than the normal time necessary. In
Chañaral it is reported that all the stevedoring is done
by an American firm, the manager of which is an American
born in the United States of British parentage and
registered at birth in the British Consulate at
Charleston, South Carolina. This person is reported
Confidential File
by the Shipping Adviser to have been overjoyed with the
project under discussion. He reported that Chañaral
has the worst loading record on the west coast of South
America, and it would be no trick at all to make it
worse, as far as Japanese Wessels are concerned. He
said that he could easily apread one day's loading
over two days thereby effecting a one hundred percent
slow down.
