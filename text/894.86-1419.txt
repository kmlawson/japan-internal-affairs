FROM FRODated March 20, 1944
Rec'd 1 p.m. 21st

Secretary of State,
Washington.

947, March 20, midnight

Moscow papers, for March 19 publish the following Tass despatch from Tokyo entitled "Extraordinary Measures in Japan":

"The Japanese paper ASAHI announces that the Japanese Government on March 10 approved extraordinary measures to mobilize the seamen of the merchant fleet. All seamen up to the age of 60 are subject to registration. They will be placed in service in the merchant fleet even if they are now performing other important work. It is also intended to utilize fully students attending the schools or courses of the merchant fleet. The paper states that the period of instructions in these institutions of learning should be sharply curtailed."

HARRIMAN

DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
September 11, 1940

Tokyo's despatch no. 4890, August 5, 1940, Transmitting Three Newspaper Cartoons from The Japan Times of July 19, July 30, and August 2, 1940, Devoted to the United States".

Attached are three cartoons from The Japan Times.

Comment: So far as these cartoons may have influenced such Japanese as The Japan Times reaches, their influence has probably been flutary. One cartoon represents the United States as awakening to the need of armament; other suggests that we do not want to sell online to Japan. To impress both these hints seems to me to be in our interest.

.91/8
