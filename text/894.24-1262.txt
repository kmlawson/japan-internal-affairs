Tokyo's despatch no. 5472, March 26, 1941: "British Despatch Regarding Activities of the Japanese Nanyo Development Company".

There is enclosed with this despatch a copy of a despatch from the British Consul General at Batavia, January 13, 1941, concerning the activities and scope of the Japanese Nanyo Development Company (Nanyo Kohatsu Kaisha). The Netherlands East Indian Government seized the archives of the Japanese company's head office at Manokwari, New Guinea. The evidence obtained from those files confirms information from other sources in regard to the company's close relationship with Japanese Ministries of Foreign Affairs and Overseas Affairs and the Japanese navy and in regard to Japanese construction of fortifications on the mandated islands. It is reported that in a letter dated June 6, 1939, the manager of this company in New Guinea wrote: "... In Japan it was decided by the Government that we should commence military works on the islands in the Inner-Nanyo to cost some tens of millions of yen. The Nanyo Kohatsu Kaisha have accepted the contract for this work and they will also work on Hainan Island..."
