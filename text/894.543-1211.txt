Demandant: Greenfield Tap and Die Corporation

In the matter of the invalidation proceeding against
Reg1stration No. 212580, the decision has been handed down as follows:
TEXT: Registration No. 2l2580 has been invalidated.
GIST: Omitted
REASON FOR DECISION: The Trade Mark No. 2l258O consists of the
letters "G.T.D." and designates, Class 17, "Drivers for Rubber
Tires, Heaters for Water and all other goods belonging to this
Class". The application was filed on April 6, 1929 and was register-
ed on January 15, 1930. The Trade Mark No. 132,212 c1ted by the
Demandant consists of the two mountains, in the left of which is
insoribed the letter "G" and in the right of which the letter "D",
while the letter "T" appears between the two, and it was registered
on July 18th, 1921.
The letters "G.T.D." appear very distinct in the cited
trade mark, so it is quite natural to say that the same has the sound
and idea "G.T.D.". Therefore it cannot avoid that the trademark
in question is similar to it, ha ving the same sound and idea. They
are used on the same goods.
The defendant says that the cited trade mark is called
"Three Mountains" in trade. Even granting this is true, but such
appellation and idea are not natural, so that the same does not
constitute a bar to the cited trademark being called "G.T.D.".
Further, the fact that the cited trade mark is famous in trade
can be admitted from Evidence A-4 and A-5, so that if the trade
mark in question be used on the goods designated, such goods
would be considered to have been produced by the demandant, that
1s to say, therewould be fear of confusion raised as to the
origin of goods.
In view of the foregoing, the registration of the trade
mark in question should be said to have been made in contradiction
to Art. ¿, Par. 1, No. 11 of the Trade Mark Law and should there-
fore be invalidated by Art. 16, Par. 1, No. 1 of the said law.
