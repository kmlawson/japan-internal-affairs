DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS

September 6, 1932.

MEMO.

Major Witsell, of the Far Eastern Section of the War Department, stated orally to Mr. Salisbury, on August 29, that the War Department would like to have the information given by Mr. Sanchez to the Minister at Lima, as reported in the attached despatch, substantiated or disproved, if possible. Major Witsell was informed that, at the request of O.N.I., the Minister had been instructed to submit such other information regarding Japanese activities in Peru as might come to his knowledge.
