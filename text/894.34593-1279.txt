235, July 8, 3 p. m.

Confidential. Following summarizes the main points of a private conversation with Everitt Groff-Smith, American, Commissioner of Chinese Maritime Customs on Hainan Island, now in Hong Kong on one of his infrequent visits.

The most important developments on the island since the Japanese occupation are the rapid progress on a naval base being built at the southern end at an estimated cost of 100,000,000 yen to serve as a counterpoise to American base in the Philippines and the practical completion of mile square air base partly concrete surfaced believed to be one of the best in the Far East.

The Japanese Army and Navy originally exercised equal joint supervision but the Army is being gradually withdrawn. He believes that by August 1st army withdrawal will be completed thus leaving the Navy in complete control.
