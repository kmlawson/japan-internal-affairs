EMBASSY OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
Panama, July 22, 1946

No. 2005

CONFIDENTIAL

SUBJECT: Humberto BALLESTAS Designated to Represent Panamanian Government in New Orleans on the Anniversary of Founding of that City.

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to report that according to the Panama American, July 18, 1946, Humberto BALLESTAS will represent the Government of Panama in New Orleans on the anniversary of the founding of that city.

Ballestas, who was appointed a collector of back taxes by the Government of Panama (see Embassy's despatch No. 2801, September 24, 1945, Subject: "Raul R. ACEVEDO and Humberto Ballestas Become Collectors of Back Taxes"), is reported by both the Civil Intelligence Section of The Panama Canal and G-2, Panama Canal Department, who have been intimate with the Japanese colony in Panama City and to have been an employee and confidant of Yoshitaro AMANO, known to be the head of Japanese espionage activities in Central and South America, who was deported from Panama and repatriated to Japan at the outbreak of the war.

A letter dated November 27, 1941 from Walter KIRCHNER, who was interned and repatriated to Germany at the outbreak of the war, to a Mexican motor company concerning certain automobile equipment indicated that Kirchner wanted the documents regarding the transaction sent to Humberto Ballestas in Panama in the event that war broke out between the United States and Japan.

Respectfully yours,

For the Ambassador:

V. Lansing Collins, Jr.
Second Secretary of Embassy

841.5
MATERS; mw
