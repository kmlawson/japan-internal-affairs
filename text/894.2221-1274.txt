# TELEGRAM RECEIVED

![img-0.jpeg](img-0.jpeg)

FROM

Tokyo via Shanghai &amp; N.R.

Dated June 21, 1941

Rec'd. 8:18 a. m. 23rd

Secretary of State,

Washington

857, June 21, 7 p. m.

![img-1.jpeg](img-1.jpeg)

Today's Press reports a statement issued yesterday by the War Minister to the effect that the cabinet had decided to establish next year a system of voluntary military service in Taiwan similar to that set up in Chosen in 1938. The move is called a step toward amalgamation of the natives of Taiwan into the Japanese race.

Send to Department. Via airmail to Shanghai.

GREW

MG

PS/DM
