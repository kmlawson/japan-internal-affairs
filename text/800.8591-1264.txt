Information regarding ship movements other than those of American and Japanese vessels is not published nor otherwise available except by exceptional official confidential release which would not (repeat not) be made to the Consulate General or Naval Observer except under guarantee that transmission be by absolutely safe means. Telegram no. 86
Dated: 4/19/41, 2 p.m.
From: Consul Singapore.
Rec'd: 4/21/41, 5:25 a.m.

Department's telegram of March 17, 7 p.m., #177 repeated from Manila and was received March 18.

Instruction in regard to movements of vessels other than Japanese and American ships is not published or otherwise available except by rare official confidential release, which would not be made to the Naval Observer or the Consulate General except upon a guarantee that it be sent by absolutely confidential means. The Consulate General does not have a code sufficiently secret for this purpose, and suggests Navy cipher be used. This office will make no official request for such information pending further instructions from the Department.

While telegraphed instructions under consideration do not supersede instructions previously received in regard to ships of Japan of 4,000 or more tons modified subsequently to 3,500 or more tons, it is not clear if movements of shippon ships of 2,000 to 3,500 tons are to be reported
