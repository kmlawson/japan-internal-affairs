DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
January 8, 1934.

This morning, by means of a long distance telephone call, Mr. Mackay imparted to Mr. Dundas the substance of paragraph one of the Tokyo Embassy's telegram No. 2 of January 7, 6 p.m. Mr. Mackay also stated that the Department is giving consideration to the suggestion made in the last sentence of the paragraph under reference and that it would communicate its views to Mr. Dundas at an early moment. Mr. Dundas stated that if the Department is able to assist in the manner suggested, such facility would be greatly appreciated, but if the Department's decision is in the negative, the Company would understand the situation.
