# CROSS-REFERENCE FILE

## NOTE

### SUBJECT
Efforts of Japanese to establish themselves in Mexican fisheries.

Discussion of past -, in providing experts to assist Mexicans, and furnishing of scholarships for three Mexican students, two of whom have been in employ of Mexican Fisheries Department. Offer pending before Mexican Government, to take at least one Mexican student to Japan from Mexican fisheries, without cost to Mexico. Japanese efforts likely to increase with more critical conditions in Far East.
