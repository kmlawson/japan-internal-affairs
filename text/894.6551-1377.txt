Spinning Industry Reorganization
(Tokyo 20th Domei)
In connection with the reorganization of our Nation's
spinning industry, in the second reorganization of 1940
some 77 textile manufacturers were reformed and unified
into some 40 companies which together with remaining compa-
nies formed a unit of 50 spinners which were formed into
14 blocks. Each block was managed rationally on a basis of
50% working, 30% reserve and 20% idle. With the progress
of the war and the necessity for the highest degree of
control, the Ministry of Commerce and Industry has decided
to put into effect from February 1, 1943 the following
further reorganization:
1. 30% working, 30% reserve and 40% idle equipment,
which shall be available for disposal.
2.
Exercise of control under the block system in
changing the besis of the textile industry,
each spinner to be organized and unified on a
basis of 1,000,000 or more spindles.
Translation from: "Diario Nan-A", "El Argentin Djijo",

Planting of Cotton in Southern Regions
Direct Advance of the Spinning Companies
(Osaka 23rd Domei)
As a result of the conference of those persons and
organizations concerned with the cotton industry in the
Cotton and Staple Fiber Control Association, the Spinning
Industrialists of China, and the East Asia Fiber Industry
Association, in connection with the recent policy of in-
creasing production of Chinese cotton, complete agreement
was reached on the view that "In the captured places in
the southern regions, the progress of every spinning company
in cotton planting achieved better results than anticipated.
In view of this fact, rather than have only the cotton com-
panies and already existing agencies concern themselves with
the increased production of cotton on the continent of Asia,
the spinning companies will make direct endeavors to advance
into this field as they have done in the southern regions."
Concrete steps will now be taken to establish the requisite
organizations and companies to increase and round out cotton
production.
