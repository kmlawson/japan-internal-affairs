May 17 1935

Dear Mr. Secretary:

I am enclosing herewith a letter that I have received from Mr. Charles W. Palomba, P. O. Box 341, Waterbury, Connecticut, in reference to a report on the Japanese Post Office Insurance System. I shall appreciate your kindness if you can obtain a copy of this report for Mr. Palomba or advise him where it may be readily obtained.

Cordially yours,
Royal S. Copeland

Honorable Cordell Hull
The Secretary of State

THE CHARLES WILLIAM PALOMBA CO.
P. O. BOX 341
WATERBURY, CONNECTICUT

May 9, 1935.

Hon. Royal S. Copeland,
United States Senate,
Washington, D.C.

My dear Sir:

I have expressed my interest in matters concerning public interest and I have found a reference to a matter of Life Insurance in the Post Offices in the country of Japan and referred to as the "Japanese Post Office Insurance System" and appearing in the issue of "The Spectator" (An American Weekly Review of Insurance) for Jan 18, 1934 and carrying quite an interesting article on the subject.

The article explained that the Government of Japan had prepared a report in the English language and it is this matter in which I am particularly interested and enlist your good offices to procure for me.

I would appreciate your kind efforts to obtain through governmental or diplomatic channels a copy of the Report on Japanese Post Office Insurance and and explanation of its features and results.

Thanking you very kindly, and with kindest personal regards, I am

Respectfully yours,

Charles W. Palomba,

Palomba

May 17 1935

In reply refer to
A-C/C 894.5064/14

My dear Senator Copeland:

The receipt is acknowledged of your letter dated May 13, 1935, with which was transmitted a letter from Mr. Charles W. Palomba, Waterbury, Connecticut, dated May 9, 1935. Mr. Palomba requested a copy of a report on Japanese Post Office Insurance, published by the Japanese Government, together with an explanation of the features and benefits of such insurance.

The American Consul General at Tokyo, under date of February 27, 1935, forwarded to the Department one copy of this report, which was transmitted to the Department of Labor. A copy of the Consul General's despatch accompanying the report, giving certain salient features thereof, is enclosed herewith.

It

The Honorable
Royal S. Copeland,
United States Senate,
Washington, D. C.

It is suggested that Mr. Palomba may be able to obtain a loan of the report from the Department of Labor. If he is unable to obtain such a loan and will authorize the payment of any charges which may be incurred in obtaining an additional copy thereof, the Department will be glad to instruct the American Consul General at Tokyo to procure a copy for him.

Sincerely yours,
Cordell Hull
