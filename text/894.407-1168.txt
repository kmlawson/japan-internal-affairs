# TELEGRAM RECEIVED

JS
1-1896

FROM
Gray
TOKYO
Dated December 27, 1937
Rec'd 12:21 a.m.

Secretary of State,
Washington, D.C.

684, December 27, 11 a.m.

The period January 1st to 6th is in Japan a continuous holiday. Government offices are normally closed and most of the cabinet ministers and higher officials leave Tokyo. Owing to present circumstances a skeleton staff will be on duty at the Foreign Office but official business requiring the consideration of cabinet ministers may be difficult or impossible to transact.

GREW

JS
