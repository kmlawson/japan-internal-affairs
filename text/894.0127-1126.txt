SUBJECT: JAPANESE GOVERNMENT'S SPECIAL REGISTRATION OF FOREIGNERS OF JAPANESE RACE
1-1055 GPO

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to report that the Japanese authorities have recently registered all residents of Japanese race who have foreign nationality. Nearly all of the persons affected are those who were born in the United States, although there are a few persons of Japanese race who were born in Canada or South America. I enclose herewith a copy of the questionnaire which is used for registering these individuals. It will be noted that each registrant is required to indicate whether he has Japanese as well as foreign nationality and whether he is subject to military service.

It is believed that the authorities are making this special registration for two reasons. The first reason is presumably to locate every foreign-born individual of Japanese race who also has Japanese nationality and
