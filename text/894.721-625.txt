FROM
65NY X Svc.
SD, Washington, D. C.
Bern SCE Burinterna to Secstate, Washington.

Japanese Administration draws attention of administrations concerned to the following fact: numerous telegrams with insufficient address (for instance, address including only name of addressee or one in which supplementary indication regarding domicile is lacking) are transmitted to Japan as a result of restriction in use of abbreviated addresses. This administration finds itself faced with difficulty of forwarding these telegrams. Administrations concerned are accordingly invited to review particularly provisions No. 71 of telegraph regulations. They are further requested to accept only at the risk of sender telegrams with insufficient addresses the forwarding of which cannot be guaranteed in accordance with the provisions No. 93.

(No signature)
From Cable Cable (sic)
New York, September 14, 1939.
2:44 p.m.

TR: AVA: JP
