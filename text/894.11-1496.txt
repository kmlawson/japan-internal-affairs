UNRESTRICTED

No.

To the

American Consular Officer in Charge,
Shanghai.

The Secretary of State transmits herewith copy
of a letter of November 6, 1945, from Dungan, Hood
&amp; Co., Inc., of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, regarding
a consignment of leather shipped on the SS President
Van Buren on September 17, 1945, to Vienna Shoes
Company at Shanghai.

The Consulate General is directed to make
inquiries in an endeavor to ascertain the whereabouts
or disposition of the shipment in question and the
procedure which may be followed by Dungan, Hood &amp;
Co., Inc., to regain possession of merchandise or
compensation therefor.

Enclosure:
From Mr. Penrose
November 6, 1945.
