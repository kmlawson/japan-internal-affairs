CIRCULAIRE.

Le Ministère Impérial des Affaires étrangères a l'honneur de porter à la connaissance des Ambassades et Légations que, par suite d'une modification récente de la réglementation concernant les installations radioélectriques à ondes courtes et à grande distance, le Ministère des Communications vient de décider de reconnaître l'établissement de telles installations à l'usage des Ambassades et Légations, sous les conditions suivantes:

1). Il est permis d'établir seulement des installations qui seront exclusivement destinées à la réception d'émissions radioélectriques publiques, c'est-à-dire d'émissions non destinées à une personne particulière, et dont la puissance ne dépassera pas la limite nécessaire pour les recevoir, mais sous condition de réciprocité avec le Pays du chef de la mission diplomatique intéressée.

2). Le chef de la mission diplomatique qui désire obtenir l'autorisation d'établir une telle installation est prié de présenter par écrit, par l'intermédiaire du Ministère des Affaires étrangères,

au

Translation.

No. GO. 13/T2

Circular.

The Imperial Minister of Foreign Affairs has the honor to advise the Embassies and Legations that, as the result of a recent amendment to the regulations concerning short wave and long distance radio installations, the Ministry of Communications has just decided to permit the establishment of such installations for the use of embassies and legations, on the following conditions:

(1) It is permitted to establish only installations which will be intended exclusively for receiving public radio broadcasts, i. e., broadcasts not intended for a private individual, and the power of which shall not exceed the limit necessary to receive them, but on the condition of reciprocity with the country of the chief of the diplomatic mission concerned.

(2) The chief of the diplomatic mission which desires to obtain authorization to establish such an installation is requested to submit in writing, through the

=== Page 6 === (confidence 0.99)
- 2 -

the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, to the Ministry of Communications, an application giving the following data:

(a) Name of the person who is to establish the radio installations.

(b) Place where the apparatus will be installed.

(c) Class and number of receiving apparatus, as well as audible frequency.

(d) Names of broadcasting stations and names of the countries where they operate.

(e) Statement as to the broadcasts which they wish to receive.

(3) It is not permissible to reproduce in the newspapers, periodicals, etc., anything received by means of radio installations thus authorized.

Tokio, October 19, 1931.

Ordinance No. 40 of the Ministry of Communications

Wireless telegram regulations shall be revised as follows:

Article 19, No. 2: Wireless telegram sent by the captain
in order to obtain instructions in regard to the medical treatment
of sick or wounded on a ship at sea to hospitals specified elsewhere
or to ships having doctors on board, and replies thereto may be
taken as medical treatment wireless telegrams (hereafter called
medical treatment telegrams).

Article 19, No. 3: The sender of a medical treatment
telegram shall mention words "medical treatment" in the space for
postage stamps on a telegram form.

The paragraph on "official telegrams and private telegrams"
in Article 42 shall be revised as follows:

16 sen for each word

No. 2. Article 14. Wireless telegrams which are sent and received between captains of Japanese ships and hospitals or stations on land authorized by the government offices of foreign countries or by private enterprises, or foreign ships with doctors on board, and between the captains of foreign ships and hospitals which are specified elsewhere or Japanese ships with doctors on board, shall be regarded as medical treatment messages.

No. 3, Article 14. The sender of medical treatment messages shall mention the words "medical treatment" in the space for postage stamps on a telegram form.
