4014, Second, (SECTION ONE)

Berlin official DAD states European countries particularly Germany greatly interested Japan's vast prospects for expansion cultivation cotton in Philippines Celebes and Continental Asia. While realizing Tokio in replacing sugar culture by cotton plans create raw material base for Asiatic textile industry new production will offer possibility Japan supply Europe with cotton over amounts obtained for Egypt Asia Minor Irak and Iran. Finally European countries will at least for transition period assist Japan supply eastern Asia with finished textiles to meet shortages inevitably caused by elimination Britain and America as furnishers vital necessities Eastern Asia.

4014, September 2, (SECTION TWO).

This statement as to planned elimination of American cotton from both Asiatic and European markets is typical example German economic propaganda. Special efforts are being made in Germany to convince populace that raw materials for European economy can be obtained in postwar period without calling on the Americans for supplies formerly obtained there.

(END OF MESSAGE)

A-44, January 16, 10 a.m.
For Hamilton SBW

Following items have been obtained from a weekly
"Intelligence report for Far East propaganda" issued
by the Far East Section of the British Ministry of Information

ONE: "As was mentioned in the last report, Domei
on January 2 spoke of 'Experimental' cultivation of cotton
in the Philippines. An article in a recent 'Oriental
Economist' quoted by 'Machrichten Fur Den Aussenhandel'
December 2, full; confirms this description: 'Expansion
of Cotton' says 'the Japanese writer, 'has hardly even
begun in the Southern Region.' He admits that cotton
growing in Occupied China 'is only making slow progress'.

TWO: "The 'Deutsche Bergwerkzeitung' of September 2
1942 quotes Shimoda, editor of 'Mainichi', who after obvious
remarks on shortage and surplus in the 'Sphere' says that
twelve million tons of cargo are waiting to be shipped."

JHA:BE:ORS
