260, April 18, 9 p.m.
Department's 114, April 13, 3 p.m. Maritime Commission's ship WEST CUSSETA.

Foreign Office states that dry dock facilities are fully occupied but authorities will endeavor vouch temporary release of dry dock when precise information concerning necessary repairs is available. Ship arrival at Yokohama today in tow sister ship COLESTREAM. Survey will probably be made tomorrow. Agents are considering the advisability of sending the vessel in tow to Shanghai for repairs in view of local shortage of facilities and materials. Embassy will report further developments as they occur.

GREW
CSB
