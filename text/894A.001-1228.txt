November 29, 1940.

Mr. Hornbeck:

With regard to your question as to whether Admiral Kiyoshi Hasegawa was in command of the Japanese Naval Landing forces which attempted to take Shunji in 1932:

Admiral Hasegawa was the Japanese Naval Commander in Chief at Shanghai in 1937 and continued in that command until relieved in April 1938 by Admiral Oikawa.

The person who had to do particularly with the landing of Japanese naval forces at Shanghai in 1932 was Vice Admiral Koichi Shiozawa, who was chief of staff of the First Japanese Fleet at the time. He was recalled and Admiral Nomura was sent at that time to Shanghai to take the situation in hand.

1206, November 26, 8 p.m.

Press announces this evening that Admiral Kobayashi has resigned as Governor General of Formosa, and that Admiral Kiyoshi Hasegawa, member of the Supreme War Council has been selected as the new Governor General. It was reported that Admiral Hasegawa will retain his active duty status which in some circles is believed to indicate a still further emphasis on Japan's southward advance, since the Governor General of Formosa is directly in charge of the organization for the advance southward.

Naval Attaché requests Navy Department be informed.
Sent to the Department via Shanghai.

Note
740.0011 Pacific War

28 November 0805 consider appointment Admiral Kiyoshi Hasigawa as Governor General Taiwan and his retention on active list significant. Marking increased influence navy in Japanese Government. Admiral Hasegawa while experienced naval administrator has no experience in colonial Administration. His appointment as Governor General therefore not connected with internal administration of island but with coordination southward advance policy. Active naval officer necessary for effective cooperation navy and south seas administration especially in view probability that in near future mandate islands administration will be combined with Taiwan. Believe navy will assume same position in Taiwan and mandate that army has long held in Manchuria and that Admiral Hasegawa's position will be similar that General Umezu.

GES
