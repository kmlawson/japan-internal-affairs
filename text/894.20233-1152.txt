DEPARTMENT OF STATE

URUGUAY - Population &amp; Social Conditions.

Subject: Foreign Propaganda;
Japan Justifying Chinese Adventure.

Forwarded herewith are two pamphlets of Japanese propaganda that is being distributed in Montevideo. An investigation in Buenos Aires fails to reveal such pamphlets in public reading rooms, but recently there were several articles in The Standard, oldest English-language newspaper in Argentina, now owned by an Argentine family, which resemble the arguments put forth in these pamphlets and may have come from this source.

These pamphlets are published in English by the Foreign Affairs Association of Japan - four editions - from September to November 1937.

The copies found in Montevideo were distributed by no less a person than the present Mayor of that city, a Mr. Zanni, a Uruguayan who, it is said, visited Japan about three years ago, and upon his return established in Montevideo the Nakoto Susaki Commercial Interchange Company of Uruguay. This company exports Uruguay products such as wool, hides, canned meats, and bones to Japan, and imports such Japanese products as bicycles, colth goods, electric fixtures, lamps, etc.

REMARKS:
The pamphlets are good attempts to make Japan's case in China seem altruistic. They say with regard to their future treatment of China that: "The Japanese people are fighting for a better and happier future for both nations - for co-operation, and territory".

Source: Personal contacts.

Lester Baker, Col. Inf.,
Military Attaché.

Original &amp; four copies (with two pamphlets) airmailed Jan.29th.
Confirmation by steamer Jan 29th.

Form M/A Uruguay.
Report No. 5712.
January 24, 1938.
