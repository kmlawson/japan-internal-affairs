October 17, 1940
October 23, 1940

Mr. Pink, Commissioner of Insurance of New York State, called upon me with a letter of introduction from Governor Lehman. He explained that two Japanese insurance companies operating in New York State had definitely affirmed their intention of going out of business. They plan to turn over existing business to an American company. Mr. Pink explained that the insurance authorities insist upon the retention of sufficient funds to guaranty payment to all present creditors of the two Japanese companies. However, he explained that the reserves of these Japanese companies are so large that over and above the amounts the Japanese could be asked to retain here, there would be an amount of five and one-half millions dollars which the Japs wish now to send back to Japan. Mr. Pink stated that he was taking this occasion to ascertain whether this or other departments of government wished to express any observations regarding the matter. I said that offhand I had none. There is no law or regulation in force whereby the return of these funds to Japan was subject to control or restriction.
