This message consists of 4 parts.

Part 1. Reurad WSVC 5767 (States serial 481) and ourad CX 64431, request RFC confirm allocation, advise quantities each grade available and arrange shipping schedule to Japan from Philippines 685,000 pounds spinnable abaca fiber cordage grades J and below, delivery 4th quarter 1946. Higher grades preferred.

Part 2. Abaca tow grades (OTYW) in 100 ton monthly quantities desired soonest. Higher grades preferred. Advise quantities each grade available and shipping schedule.

Part 3. Values will be determined by taking cost of same grades for shipment to United States plus freight and insurance to prepresent prices FOB Japanese Port.

Part 4. Payment to RFC from SCAP export fund account is authorized for quantities of abaca set forth above.
