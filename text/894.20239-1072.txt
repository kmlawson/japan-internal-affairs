LEGATION OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
Ciudad Trujillo, D. C.
1939 FEB 8 PM 12 43 February 6, 1939.

No. 615

Subject: Expulsion of Japanese Commercial Mission from Dominican Republic.

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington, D. C.

Sir:

I have the honor to inform the Department that a so-called Japanese "commercial delegation" which has been in Ciudad Trujillo for the past week was given an ultimatum by the Dominican authorities to leave the Republic within ten days or be forcibly expelled on the presumption of espionage.

The members of the mission, one of whom was reported to have been a former secretary of the Japanese legation in Mexico City, were Messrs. Noboru Fukuchi, Atan Nakagawa, Takashi Naka, Kozo Murasi and Tukuitiro (Takutaro?) Oroui. The spelling of the last name is doubtful as the list was taken from press notices. These gentlemen, burdened with cameras
