# DOCUMENT FILE

## NOTE

SEE 894.7961/7 FOR #69

FROM (Neville) DATED Feb.26, 1931.
TO Japan NAME 1-1127

REGARDING: the distinction always maintained by the Department between the legal status of the Kwantung Leased Territory and the South Manchuria Railway Zone. Question has arisen out of the Japanese attempt to impose restrictions on flights by foreign aviators over the South Manchuria Railway Zone.
