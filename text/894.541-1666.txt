OCT 13 1948

In reply refer to
IR

My dear Mr. Bradley:

In accordance with your request to Mr. Dixon of my office during the conference of September 24, I am forwarding herewith copies of the Japanese Wartime Laws of Industrial Property of 1917 and 1943.

I regret the delay in sending these documents, which has been necessary in order to check on the authenticity of the copies we had at hand. The translations leave something to be desired, but I believe the meaning is clear enough.

Sincerely yours,

Raymond Vernon
Assistant Chief, International Resources Division

Enclosures:
1. War-Time Law of Industrial Property.
2. War-Time Special Regulations of Industrial Property Law.

OCT 13 1948

Mr. W.E.F. Bradley, Chairman,
Committee on Industrial Property,
National Foreign Trade Council, Inc.,
111 Broadway,
New York 6, New York.
