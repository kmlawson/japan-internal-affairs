Between December 1, 1942 and January 31, 1943 this
Consulate will handle draft deferment business for the
1943 draft year. It is requested that those concerned
immediately take necessary steps in compliance with the
following instructions. Regardless of the reason, ap-
plications will not be accepted after January 31, 1943.
Japanese Consulate
Buenos Aires,
November 18, 1942.
1) Draft deferment application for those abroad.
Those attaining conscription age in the l943
draft year, that is, those born between Decem-
ber 2, 1922 and December 1, 1923, who apply
for draft deferment, shall fill in Form A and
submit it in duplicate to this Consulate.
2) Declaration of residence abroad.
Among those who nave already had their draft
deferments approved and are continuing to
reside abroad, persons born after December 2,
1905 should fill in Forn B and submit it in
duplicate to this Consulate.
3) Military (or conscription) age report.
In addition to the requirement that those of
military age living outside of Japan submit
the application mentioned in paragraph 1, the
head of the family shall fill in Form C, con-
scription age report, and submit it in dupli-
cate to this Consulate on behalf of the con-
scriptee. When the conscriptee himself be-
comes head of the household he shall submit
his military age report himself.

directly a conscription age report about a
conscript so that it reaches the head of the
