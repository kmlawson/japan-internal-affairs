TOKYO
Dated March 3, 1937
Rec'd 12:50 a.m.

Secretary of State,
Washington, D.C.

URGENT.
65, March 3, noon.

In order to help me determine how to vote in a meeting of the local diplomatic corps to decide certain questions of protocol can the Department inform me as to the present practice in Washington concerning the relative precedence of foreign ministers plenipotentiary with (one) the Under Secretary of State, and (two) American Ambassadors and Ministers at home on leave or in retirement in (A) diplomatic, and (B) American Houses.

GREW
JS
