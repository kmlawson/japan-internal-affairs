There are no laws, ordinances or regulations restricting the employment of aliens in Taiwan.

The only restriction relates to the entry of aliens and has to do entirely with Chinese nationals. The entry of Chinese immigrants into Taiwan is controlled by the Government General under the "Regulations Governing Control of Chinese Laborers". Only those Chinese laborers who have obtained licenses from the immigration agents appointed by the Taiwan Government General are allowed entry into Taiwan. However, once lawfully in the country there is no restriction on their employment.
