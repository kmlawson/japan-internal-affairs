JAPANESE DIET MEMBERS: RESULTS OF APRIL 30, GENERAL ELECTION OF STATE
894.05/9
JUN 4 1942
PS/LIC

This list includes the names of 427 of the 466 Diet members, plus the names of 91 unsuccessful runners-up. According to the domestic Japanese-language broadcasts, 381 of the successful candidates were recommended by the Imperial Rule Assistance Association, and 85 were not recommended. There were 20 successful candidates who had been members of earlier Diets, 199 candidates elected to the Diet for the first time, and 477 incumbents returned to office.

Where names appeared in several broadcast programs, and were permitted differently by different monitors or translators, the variations are given variability in both reception conditions and the amount of information given by the Tokyo announcers account for the fact that some of the prefectures and districts are more complete than others.
