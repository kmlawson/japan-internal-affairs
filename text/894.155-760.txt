Tokyo, November 14, 1932.

No. 192.

Subject: Data on national recreational areas in Japan.

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

I refer to the Department's circular instruction (Diplomatic Serial No. 2226) of September 16, 1932, concerning the desire of the American Association of Museums to obtain complete data on National recreational areas in Japan.

There are herewith transmitted 29 issues of a semi-official publication entitled "The National Park"; an official

official copy of the Law and Regulations Governing
National Parks; two copies of a map showing the sites

Concerning the National Park Law." All of these publications
are issued or sponsored by the Health Bureau

the Embassy to extend the necessary money.

Respectfully yours,
