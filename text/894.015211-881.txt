Secretary of State,
Washington.

October 25, noon.

One. Because an intoxicated American sailor attached to submarine squadron five showed disrespect to Japanese flag on the night of October 17th, which happened to be Japanese memorial day for soldiers who (#) in Tsingtao in 1914, Japanese Vice Consul called at this Consulate yesterday and requested (a) apology at the Japanese Consulate General by the Commander of the USS CANOPUS, (b) punishment of the sailor who insulted flag, (c) assurances by the Commander of non-recurrence of incident.

Two. Having at the time of Japanese Vice Consul's call some knowledge of incident, this office immediately expressed official and personal regrets that disrespect was shown to Japanese flag. Consulate also assured Japanese Vice Consul that an investigation had been commenced in the morning by senior patrol officer which fact was communicated from this office to the chief

Five. So far as Consulate is informed sailor wrested (?) pole from Chinese boy who retained Japanese flag and subsequently sailor manipulated with this flag pole another small Japanese flag from its mounting on outside of building.

Six. In the afternoon officer in charge of the Japanese Consulate General called and went over same ground. This Consulate replied that he must accept sincerity of my expression made in this Consulate.

94, October 26, 6 p.m.
Reference Tsingtao's October 25 noon.

Marine guard has just furnished the Embassy the following paraphrase of a summary received by it from the commander submarine squadron at Tsingtau "0026 request marine detachment notify American Embassy Peiping following information. Following meeting with Japanese naval intelligence officer at his own request and at which meeting commander Picking expressed regret over flag incident commander submarine squadron five and the American consul (?) civilian commandment in company with the Japanese intelligence officer met at the home of the Japanese Consul General as a token of settlement of case. No request was made at latter meeting for any expression of regret nor was any given other than cordial relations. State Department has not received above information". Have repeated above paraphrase to Nanking and to Shanghai.

For the Ambassador.

LCCKHART
CSB
