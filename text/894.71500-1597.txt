Reference is your airgram A-742 (September 22, 1947, re sending packers to Japan.

A 117 parcels of 1000-1000 post, only one per week by one sender to one warehouse. Machine, 1000 (no dimensions specified). Contents limited to noncommercial goods, available medicines in noncommercial quantities, some clothing, other relief items in amounts reasonably to be used by addressee and his immediate family. Saccharine tablets are of great value limited to two hundred, pipe tobacco 1/2 pound, cigars 50, but only one tobacco item per package. If not deliverable will not be returned but turned over to authorized relief agencies for distribution. Relief parcels admitted duty free, but small customs clearance charge not exceeding $1000 to be collected from addressee. Such items as pens, watches, cameras, etc., prohibited.

Packages containing samples listed to 500 grams (18 oz.) and packets containing merchandise limited to 1 kilo ram (2 lbs, 2 oz) may be mailed also subject to small customs clearance charges but not by parcel post, hence no gifts, souvenirs, and other personal articles permitted. Commercial papers, except as following, permissible: Scores or sheets of music in manuscript, manuscripts of works, or newspapers sent separately, all papers of legal procedure, documents of all kinds drawn up by ministerial officers. Cigarettes, photo raphs, drawings, plans, maps, and patterns may be mailed, but not newspapers, books, magazines, or pamphlets. Mail matter addressed in Chinese or Korean must have interlined addresses in Roman characters.
