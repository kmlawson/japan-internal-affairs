A-394, December 27, 1949

Following news item released December 24, 1949, by Public Information Office, General Headquarters, U.S. Air East Command, Tokyo, under heading "To Establish Radiotelephone Circuit Between Japan and Formosa:"

"The Ministry of Telecommunications has been authorized to establish radiotelephone service between Japan and Formosa, (C-1) Communications Section announced today. Service is expected to be available about Jan. 1.

"The Japanese government has established the following rates, expressed in Japanese yen, from Japan to Formosa: 2,160 yen for the initial period of first three minutes, or fraction thereof: 720 yen for each additional minute of overtime, or fraction thereof, and 216 yen for report charges."
