894.242
28 1935

Dear Dr. Hornbeck:

I enclose copy of a report received via Germany that I think will interest you. The possibility of Japan becoming a great armament producer is worthy of consideration.

I am endeavoring to check the various statements made, particularly the one concerning Brazil.

Please return report when it has served your purpose.

With best wishes, I am

Very sincerely,

W. D. Puleston,
Captain, U. S. Navy,
Director of Naval Intelligence.

Dr. Stanley K. Hornbeck,
Division of Far Eastern Affairs,
Department of State,
Washington, D. C.

December 28, 1935.

CONFIDENTIAL.

My dear Captain Puleston:

The receipt is acknowledged with thanks of your letter of December 27 enclosing a copy of a report received via Germany relating to the possibility that Japan may become a large producer of armament.

I note that you are endeavoring to check various of the statements made in the report. In the event that your investigation discloses information of interest, I should appreciate it if you would be so good as to bring it to my attention.

The report is returned herewith as requested.

Sincerely yours,

Stanley K. Hornbeck,
Chief,
Division of Far Eastern Affairs.

Enclosure:
As described.

Captain J. D. Puleston, U.S.N.,
Director of Naval Intelligence,
Navy Department.
