All foreign cigarettes and other forms of tobacco average about 20% higher. The three best known brands of American cigarettes now sell for ¥0.76 per package of twenty and two of these brands available in sealed tins of fifty now sell for ¥1.90 per tin. "Three Castles" cost ¥0.72 for a package of 10 and ¥3.60 for a tin of fifty. "Navy Cut" smoking tobacco costs ¥3.30 for the mild and ¥2.90 for the medium and full strengths.

Number 7 grade makes up the majority of the Japanese variety of leaf purchases, while number 3 and 4 grades are the commonest in the Virginia variety.

The table given below shows a gradual but steady increase in the average price per kilogram paid by the monopoly for Japanese-grown tobacco during the past few years. This is a reflection of the efforts toward improvement in the quality of tobacco produced which have resulted in a higher average grading and, presumably, a higher average quality of manufactured products. While the total crop harvested has remained nearly stationary during recent years, there is visible a progressive trend toward increase in the proportion of Virginia type leaf and a corresponding
