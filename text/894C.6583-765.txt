I. Methods of Bean Oil Extraction
The oil extraction methods used in Manchuria may be clas-
sified into two general types, pressure system and abstraction
system. The pressure system may be further divided into wedge
system, screw system, and hydraulic system, and the abstrac-
tion system into benzine-benzol system and alcohol system
according to the chemical solvents used.
(1) Wedge System (Pound Cake). This is a system of pres-
sing beans by means of wedges, operated by either human or ani-
mal power. It is the most primitive and decidedly small-scale
method but stilj extensively used in the remote interior dis-
triots even to-dey.
(2) Screw System (Round Cake). Sorews are used instead
of wedges, and thc oil extraction process is exeouted by
human power but the rest of the work is done by machinery.
Most of thc bean cil milis in Manchuria use this system.
(3) Hydreu System. This system may be conveniently
divided into twc classes acccrding to the type of bean cakes
obtained, namely, round cake system and plate cake system.
(A) The Round-Cake System uses the hydraulic power in place
of screws. Large-scale bean-oil mills at Dairen and other

III. Varieties of Bean Cake
(1) Round Cake. The bean cake may be conveniently divid-
ed into three general types according to its shape - the Round
Cake, the Pla e Cake, and the Flake Cake. The Round Cake is
the original Chinese bean cake and is so named from its round,
drum-like shape. The soya beans are first flattened out and
then successively steamed, wrapped in special oil grasses,
encased in iron hoops of uniform size, and pressed for oil.
This last extraction process may be as already described, ei-
ther the wedge system, the screw system, or the hydraulic sys-

(A) Special Round Cake (Fodder Bean Cake). The Special
Round Cake is used, not as fertilizer, but as feed for domestic

It is used after dissolving the cake in the weter and the or-
dinary bean cake is both too hard for easy dissolution and
