CONFIDENTIAL REPORT,

By Warrington Dawson,
Special Assistant.

SUBJECT: A French Report Concerning
a New Japanese Arm

I venture to forward as enclosure a brief paragraph which appeared in LA TRIBUNE DES NATIONS of December 20, 1934, reporting that Mr. Yoshitaro Shimizu, Director of Physico-Chemical Researches at the Military Laboratory in Tokyo, has invented a noiseless machinegun firing 60,000 shots a minute.

LA TRIBUNE asks significantly why similar discoveries are being pushed so actively in Japan.

Encl. 1 to Confidential Report WD 1490 of Jan. 7, 1935.
From the Embassy, Paris.
Extract from La TRIBUNE DES NATIONS of December 20, 1934.

# La machine qui tuera la guerre

Ou, sinon, beaucoup d'hommes.

M. Yoshitaro Shimizu, directeur des recherches physico-chimiques au laboratoire militaire de Tokio, vient de découvrir une mitrailleuse à tir ultra-rapide, n'employant pas de poudre et ne produisant nulle détonation. Elle permet de tirer jusqu'à 60.000 coups à la minute. Son mécanisme est commandé électriquement au moyen de disques superposés.

Mais pourquoi activent-on ainsi de pareilles découvertes au Japon?

DEPARTMENT OF STATE
PENSION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS

April 24, 1939.

It is believed that the attached telegram from Singapore requires no action by the Department, as Singapore has repeated its message by airmail to Bangkok, Tokyo and nearby consulates, which may be expected to report any information obtainable in regard to delivery of material for or the construction of a Japanese airfield in south Siam.
