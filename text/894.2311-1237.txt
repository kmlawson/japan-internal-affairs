PARAPHRASE OF RADIOGRAM NO. 439, DATED
DECEMBER 30, 1940, FROM TOKYO, JAPAN

The Chief of the American Section, Japanese General Staff,
Major Sugita, sailed December 28th on the SS Nitta Maru for
San Francisco. He will be in the United States about three months
and his itinerary will include Los Angeles, Atlanta, Chicago,
Washington and Mexico City.

A letter was sent in the December 13th pouch concerning this
Subject.

January 14, 1941

No. 3414

To the American Chargé d'Affaires ad interim,
México, D. F.

The Secretary of State informs the Chargé d'Affaires ad interim of the receipt of a report to the effect that Major Sugita, who is Chief of the American Section, Japanese General Staff, sailed December 28 on the SS Hitta Maru for San Francisco. He is expected to remain about three months in the United States and his itinerary includes various cities in this country as well as Mexico City.

A true copy of the signed original.
