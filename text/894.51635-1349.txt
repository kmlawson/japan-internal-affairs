EMBASSY OF THE
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, June 1, 1942

No. 7491
Subject: Yokohama Specie Bank, Rio de Janeiro

CONFIDENTIAL

2 Copies

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington, D. C.

Sir:

I have the honor to refer to the secret memorandum of April 7, 1942, from the Chief of the Military Attache's Section to the Liaison Officer of the Department of State, relative to the Japanese staff of the Rio de Janeiro branch of the Yokohama Specie Bank.

The Director of Exchange of the Bank of Brazil informs me that the Brazilian Government has no intention of protecting this bank nor its employees. For the Department's confidential information, plans now being drafted envisage the gradual liquidation of the bank's branches in Brazil.

Respectfully yours,
For the Ambassador:

John F. Simmons,
Counselor of Embassy
