FROM France (Dawson) DATED Sept.28,1933
TO NAME 1-1127

REGARDING: Japanese anti-American activities in Morocco - Conversation with Prince Mehdi, concerning -

I am privately informed by Mehdi, Prince El Glasui, son of Pacha El Glasui of Morocco and now completing his military education in France, that Japanese commercial agents in Morocco are very active at present in efforts to cut out American trade, replacing it by Japanese trade, and also in creating anti-American sentiment amid the native populations, more especially the half-sastes.
