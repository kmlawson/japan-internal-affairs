|  1 | State Dept. Far Eastern Division (Dooman) | Mar. 2 MEMO. | Ea n-M |  | Proposal made by Mrs. M. Hosken, of the Hosken Trading Company, New York, to develop in cooperation with the Hoshi Pharmaceutical Company of Tokyo, a cinchona plantation in Formosa. She is to deposit a sum of money in Bank of Taiwan, and is to receive in return the sole agency outside of Japan of quinine produced by Hoshi. She desires to borrow from the First Import-Export Bank and hoped the Department would recommend that credit its be made available to her. Mr. Dooman called Major Hall of the Bank and asked him to receive Mrs. Hosken. Note: 811.503194a; 811.516-Export-Import Bank.  |
