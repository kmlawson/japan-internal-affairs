Office Memorandum
UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

DATE: February 17, 1949

TO: The Secretary
Through: S/S
FROM: FE - Mr. Butterworth

SUBJECT: Education of (Japanese Crown Prince) in the United States and England

The accompanying personal letter to me from Mr. Sebald (Acting Political Adviser to General MacArthur) recounts a suggestion informally made by the Japanese Empress regarding the possibility of the 15 year-old Crown Prince receiving preparatory school education in the United States, followed by higher education in England. Mr. Sebald regards the suggestion as a good one, both in the interests of United States-Japanese relations and in the interests of weaning the young Prince away from the enervating atmosphere of a royal court in which feudal customs may gradually reappear. General MacArthur agrees but feels that the boy should first learn to speak English and that the aim should be a four-year standard course in the United States without a period at a foreign preparatory school. Mr. Sebald requests my frank views on the matter.

My views are set forth in the accompanying letter. With reference to the last sentence of my letter, may I have your concurrence?
