JAN 23 1934
File

Secretary of State,
Washington.

10, January 20, 11 a.m.

The Consul at Kobe reports that from the local office of the P. and O. Steamship Company it has been learned that Japanese dockyards have been instructed by the naval authorities to accept no orders for repairs from foreign steamship companies more than 30 days in advance, presumably in order to leave the docks free for emergencies.

GREW

HPD

# PARAPHRASE

A telegram dated January 20, 1934, from the American Ambassador at Tokyo, reads substantially as follows:

The Consul at Kobe learns from the local office of the P. and O. Steamship Company that the Japanese naval authorities have instructed Japanese dockyards not to accept orders for repairs from foreign steamship companies more than thirty days in advance. It is assumed that the purpose of the order is to have the docks left free for emergencies.
