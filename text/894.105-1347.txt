October 24, 1944

In reply refer to
Ja

My dear Captain Winterburg:

Your letter of October 16, 1944 in which you requested that the Department of State send you available information on prisons and police departments in Japan has been received.

You have probably received by this time through the authorities of your Civil Affairs Training School a Civil Affairs handbook on Japanese legal affairs (M 354-3). So far as the Department is aware, this volume contains the latest available information in English on Japanese prisons and police departments.

Sincerely yours,

For the Secretary of State:

Erle R. Dickover
Chief
Division of Japanese Affairs
