FROM Tokyo (Cameron) DATED Nov.29,1938.
NAME 1-1127

REGARDING:

Entry of German Jews into Japan;

Reports possible understanding between Japanese and German Governments that no passports valid for travel to Japan will be issued by German Government to German Jews unless previous permission of Japanese Government has been obtained for entry of such German nationals.
