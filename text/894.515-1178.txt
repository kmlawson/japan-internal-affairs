DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
DEPARTMENT OF STATE
February 29, 1940.

PA/D:
Mr. Hornbeck.

Please note that we are sending to various offices the whole of the text of your memorandum of conversation with Mr. Officer on February 10 -- the latter part of the memorandum does not relate to the subject of credits, but it seems to me that the offices indicated will benefit by receipt of the further information set forth in that memorandum.

m.m.h.

FE:MMH:REK
