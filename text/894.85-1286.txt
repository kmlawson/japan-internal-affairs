251, July 17, noon.

Reports unconfirmed are current in the local press and in business circles to the effect that all Japanese merchant ships operating between Japan and Thailand have been ordered to suspend (*) by the Japanese Government. Merchant ships which had sailed prior to the order were recalled by wireless to return to Japanese ports it is stated. It is also reported in the local market that Japanese firms have recently refused to receive any new orders for Japanese goods and have cancelled old orders.

Shanghai please repeat to Tokyo.

Telegram no. 251 Dated: 7-17-41, noon.
From: Legation, Bangkok. Rec'd: 7-17-41, 9:35 p.m.

In the local press and in business circles unconfirmed reports are current to the effect that all Japanese merchant ships operating between Thailand and Japan have been ordered to suspend (*) by the Government of Japan. It is stated that merchant vessels which had departed prior to the issuance of the order were recalled by a wireless to return to ports of Japan. There is also a report in the local market that firms of Japan have recently declined to take any new orders for Japanese merchandise and old orders have been canceled.

GRANT

(*) apparent omission.

NA
