STANFORD UNIVERSITY
DEPARTMENT OF HISTORY
UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA
STANFORD, CALIFORNIA
July 15, 1949

Mr. John Allison
Assistant Chief
Office of Far Eastern Affairs
Department of State
Washington, D. C.

Dear John:

We are hoping at Stanford to have Kazuo Kawai with us as a Visiting Professor during the coming year. Everything is in order from this end, and I am told that they tell him in Japan that it is only an administrative lag in the State Department which is holding up his exit permit. He is scheduled to sail August 14, and we certainly do want him at that time in order to carry out our academic program for the Far East.

Will you do two things for me:
(1) Check to see if this administrative lag does exist, and (2) do anything you can to overcome it, if it does exist?

I am also asking Max Bishop to look into this matter for us.

Many thanks,
Claude A. Buss
Professor of History

July 20, 1949

Dear Claude:

I have checked into the case of Professor Kawai and find that a telegram went out to Japan on July 11 clearing him so he should be able to sail on time.

When are you coming to Washington again so that we can have a long talk about everything in general?

Sincerely yours,

John M. Allison
Deputy Director for Far Eastern Affairs

Dr. Claude A. Buss,
Professor of History,
Stanford University,
Stanford, California.
