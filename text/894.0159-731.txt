=== Page 2 === (confidence 0.96)
October 31 1951.

Miss Erma Barnhart,
450 First Avenue,
East Liverpool, Ohio.

Madam:

Your letter of October 22, 1951, in which you request that you be furnished with a copy of the "National Song of Japan", has been received. It is regretted that this Department has available no copies of Japan's national anthem.

It is suggested that the Japanese Embassy in Washington may be able to assist you in the matter.

Very truly yours,

For the Secretary of State:

M. M. H.
Maxwell M. Hamilton,
Assistant Chief,
Division of Far Eastern Affairs.

Oct. 31 1951.
