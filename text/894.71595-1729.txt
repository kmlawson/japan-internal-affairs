The Ambassador has the honor to report a visit to Tokyo of Mr. G. N. Miller, Postal Consultant, Economic Cooperation Administration, for the following purposes:

1. Conference with Japanese postal officials relative to the establishment of parcel post relations between Korea and Japan.

2. Establishment of rates, terminal charges and transit charges on parcel post matter and mail of other classes, handled by each country for the other.

3. Comparison of statistical data, obtained by both countries during the period May 1 through May 28, 1949, upon which international rates and other charges between the two countries will be determined.

4. Arrangement for steamship and air contracts for mail handling, with companies of Japanese registry.

5. Conferences with American postal officials of SCAP relative to prohibitions on mail matter sent from Japan or destined to Japan from Korea.

6. Conference with American and Japanese postal officials relative to the exchange of newspapers and periodicals between the two countries.

It is understood that Mr. Miller has been authorized to negotiate these matters for the Republic of Korea. Any agreements reached will be submitted to the Korean Government for approval.

The Korean Chief of the Postal Bureau and a Korean section chief of the international mails will accompany Mr. Miller on this trip, which is scheduled during the week of July 11.

This is a joint State-ECA despatch. It is requested that copies be furnished to the Economic Cooperation Administration.
