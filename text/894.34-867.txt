# DOCUMENT FILE

## NOTE

SEE 811.111 Yasuba,Takeo FOR Despatch # 7

FROM Nagoya (Stephan) DATED June 22,1935.
TO NAME 1-117

## REGARDING:

Granting of visas to Japanese for investigation of designs and manufacture of airplanes.

Advises of issuance of temporary visitor visas to Takeo Yasuba and Shigeru Kamiya, engineers of the Aichi Tokai Denki K.K., Aeronautical Engineers, Nagoya, Japan. This firm manufactures planes for the Japanese Army and Navy as well as for commercial purposes.
