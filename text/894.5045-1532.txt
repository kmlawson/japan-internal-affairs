A-566, September 4.
Izvestiya of August 25 picked up report attributed to Allied Labor News correspondent at Tokyo regarding so-called kamikaze strike breakers.
Izvestiya led off with statement that "Under gentle and curative influence of American occupational policy, Japanese reaction has almost completely recovered from unfeigned terror which seized it in first days of capitulation. Conscious of firm ground under its feet it is beginning to take heart, and has not only retained Draconian laws against workers, but is gradually reviving symbols of imperialistic struggle dear to its heart."
Allied Labor News despatch is then quoted, significant parts of which state the kamikaze strike breakers, with concealed support of Japanese Government and General MacArthur's headquarters, dispersed pickets of Japanese workers fighting for increased wages and for right of organization. Another interesting statement lifted from Allied Labor News was: From MacArthur's recent statement it has become sufficiently clear that Japan is being trained up into potential ally of United States in event of another war... If Japanese workers are permitted to develop in democratic direction and set up their own free trade unions, they will not support such a war.
Izvestiya concluded that "it would be good thing if air of this country were cleared not by a divine, but by an ordinary fresh wind of genuine democracy?"
