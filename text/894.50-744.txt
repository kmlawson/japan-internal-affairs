Secretary of State,
Washington.

107, April 6, 4 p. m.

SECTION F - COPPER:

Copper production in Japan in 1931 amounted to 74,800 metric tons; exports 26,600 metric tons; imports 2,000 tons. The exports were largely copper bars shipped to Russia.

The four leading copper producers have a paid up capital of 102,500,000 yen. Present prices are unsatisfactory but the industry is said to be in good financial condition.

Commercial Attache who supplied this information states that the gold and silver content of the copper industry has helped to keep the industry profitable. There is an import duty on copper of 7 yen per hundred kin.

There is a steady market for copper in Japan due to the development of the electric industry.

NEVILLE
