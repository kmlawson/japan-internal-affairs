F. A. FMS.

The Japanese Ambassador presents his compliments to the Honorable the Secretary of State and has the honor to inform him that the Government of Formosa has amended its regulations with regard to plant quarantine.

In this connection copies of the new Forms, Numbers 7 and 8, of the Certificate of Examination of Plants for Export are herewith enclosed. They went into effect on October 1, 1931.

The Ambassador begs leave to request that the Secretary be good enough to cause the competent authorities to be notified concerning this matter.

(Enclosures)
December 7, 1931.
