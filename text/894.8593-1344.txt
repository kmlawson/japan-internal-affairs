DIVISION OF
OCT 2 1941
THE AMERICAN REPUBLICS
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

MEMORANDUM

RA:

I believe that the local coal dealers should not be encouraged to supply Japanese steamers with coal. But I am not clear that this might not be resented in Buenos Aires.

Do you feel that you can draft a telegram? It would have to be cleared with FE and DCA.

A. A. Berle, Jr.

PS/LDP
