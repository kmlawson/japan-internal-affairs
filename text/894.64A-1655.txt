FROM: Nanking
TO: Secretary of State
NO: 1534, August 19, 1 a.m.

Presence of Stilwan's discussions with Armstrong is what what is needed in Taiwan is not personal advisor to Wei Tan-ming but services of competent firm which Armstrong willing to undertake (DEPTEL 102 August 1948). Under no circumstances is Armstrong willing to enter upon contract requiring him personally to give in Taiwan any definite period of time. Stilwan has discussed matter with C. K. Yen using these terms. Yen has promised endeavor locate qualified American willing to be Wei's personal advisor. He and some non-person turns up in connection with engineering projects under ECA; Stilwan not so guide we will succeed in finding such individuals. As he put it qualified persons just do not know enough about Taiwan to be willing. After contract requiring prolonged residence there.

Sent Taipei 71; repeated Department 1534.

STUART
