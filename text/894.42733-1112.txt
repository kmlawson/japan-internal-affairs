JAPANESE SCHOLARSHIP FOR URUGUAYAN PROFESSOR

From
Augustin W. Ferrin, American Consul.

Montevideo, Uruguay. Date of completion: July 13, 1939.
Date of mailing: July 13, 1939.

Approved
Dudley G. Dwyfe, American Consul General.

The President of the Republic of Uruguay by decree dated July 5, 1939 and published in the DIARIO OFICIAL of July 13, 1939, designates a jury to choose a Uruguayan professor for the scholarship recently offered to the Uruguayan Government by the Institute of Students of Tokio and accepted by Resolution of the Executive Power on June

14,

=== Page 2 === (confidence 0.99)

14, 1939.

According to the preamble of that Resolution the
Tokio Institute of Students is an international center
for the study of sociological problems, attended already
by scholars of many nations. Aspirants for the scholar-
ship granted to Uruguay were advised to present their
applications by June 30, 1939 and the jury named by
the Decree of July 5, consisting of the Inspectors of
Primary and Secondary Education, and the Government
delegates in the Councils of Primary and Secondary
Education will now select one of these applicants to
go to Tokio.
