MEMORANDUM for Mr. Jaffee, Room 113, State Department.
In re: Wages paid to crews on Japanese vessels.
Confirming request by telephone of this date, it will be very much appreciated if you will obtain by cable from a consular representative in Japan, the rate of wages paid to crews of Japanese merchant vessels, segregated according to the several ratings of such crews.
