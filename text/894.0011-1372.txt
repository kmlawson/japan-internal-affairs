TELEGRAM RECEIVED
AS Moscow
This telegram must be Dated November 18, 1942
closely paraphrased before being communicated to anyone. (SC) Rec'd 5:45 a.m., 19th
FROM
Secretary of State,
Washington.
472, November 18, 11 a.m.
STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL.
Moscow's 462, November 15, 11 a.m.
Dickerson reports that on November 16 a Chief of Mission of a neutral power who is friendly towards United States had a talk with the Japanese Ambassador in Kuibyshev. The friendly diplomat stated that the Ambassador, who previously has taken the line that the war between Japan and the United States was a regrettable mistake and on occasions has expressed the hope that "the Americans being practical people, some sort of a fifty-fifty deal might be worked out with them in the Far East", referred to American successes in North Africa and said, "But we can't stop now".
When it was hinted to the Ambassador that he might be expecting important visitors from Japan, Sato replied that so long as he was Japanese Ambassador in the Soviet Union there would be no super ambassadors.
HENDERSON
JT
