In reply refer to JA

My dear Mr. Landone:

I acknowledge the receipt of your letter dated February 21, 1944, addressed to Mr. William L. Schurz of the Department, in which you inquire as to the name of the Premier of Japan in August, 1914, and whether he made a statement at that time to the effect that the war then beginning would last thirty years and would end with the destruction of European civilization.

The Premier of Japan in August, 1914 was Count (later Marquis) Shigenobu Okuma. The Department has no knowledge of a statement such as that to which you refer, and a search in the indexes of the New York Times and The Times of London for August, 1914 fails to reveal any reference thereto. If you could furnish a more definite indication of the place and manner in which you believe Count Okuma made the statement, the Department will be glad to look further into the matter.

Sincerely yours,

Erle R. Dickover
Chief
Division of Japanese Affairs

Mr. Brown Landone,
Winter Park,
Florida.

2728, July 24, 2 p.m.
Translations follow of despatches datelined Tokyo July 22, in July 23 press.

One. "Domei reports that today at 1 p.m. General Koiso and Admiral Yonai presented to the Emperor the new Cabinet staff. The following have entered the new Cabinet: Prime Minister General Kuniyaki Koiso, Naval Minister Admiral Mitsumasa Yonai; War Minister Field Marshal General Sugiyama; Minister of Internal Affairs Shigeo Odatsi, Minister of Finances Sotaro Ishiwata; Minister of Munitions Gindziro Fujiwara; Minister of Foreign Affairs and Minister for East Asiatic Affairs Manoru Shigemitsu; Minister of Communications and Transport Yoned Zomaeda; Minister of Agriculture and Trade Toshio Shimada; Minister of Justice Hiromasa Matzaka; Minister of Education Harushige Ninomiya; Minister of Health Hisatada Hirose; Ministers without portfolio; Tsyudai Matshida, Hideo Kodama and Taketoro Ogata"
