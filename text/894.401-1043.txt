SUBJECT: Japanese Magazine Article Predicting Shift of World Leadership from White to Colored Races.

HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

I have the honor to enclose herewith a rough translation of an article by Prof. Tatsuma Takata, Litt. D., Kyoto University, published in THE CHOSEN AND MANCHU, Keijo, for August, 1938, the article attempting to show why world leadership is about to pass from the white race to the colored races under the direction of the Japanese. The article in itself should be interesting to any thoughtful reader, but particularly so to some of Japan's allies who in the mind of the author apparently are considered as tools in the hands of Japan to accomplish their own downfall.

# TRANSLATION

## The Chosen and Manchu

### August 1938

Japanese People as Viewed from Natural Law of Rise and Fall of Nations
Tatsuma Takata, Lit. D.,
Professor of Kyoto University.

(1) Among what we generally call nations of Europe, there are ups and downs. Take Spain, for example. Once a dictator of the world, she has now completely declined, due to internal strife. Taking the white peoples as whole, however, they have now been commanding the world for four hundred years. During those four hundred years, the peoples of the colored races have either completely declined or have lost independence; and while those who have been barely maintaining a semblance of independence have lost influence and fallen to the condition of colonies, Japan alone has been maintaining a glorious history of independence.

Is it possible, then, for the white peoples to maintain, as before, their position as commanders of the world, now that the Japanese people, of the yellow race, have been rising fast and are now wielding no less commanding power than the white peoples? The white peoples are already in fear of losing their power, because it may be remarked that, while the Japan-China emergency may be superficially regarded as a fight between Japan and China, it is really an action of Japan against certain powerful nations who, when you look carefully into the present

the present trouble, are backing up China in various ways, while China has been parcelled to their influence as colonies. There is a reason, therefore, to regard the present emergency as a change in the course of the world's history. Have the white peoples superior power to keep the colored peoples in subjugation to the end?

(2) I will make the white peoples answer the question. Up to the present time, many white peoples, including Egypt, Babylon, Greece and Rome, have built up their respective civilizations and then declined. There are many uncertain points to account for their decline, but as far as the present day knowledge shows, it appears that the decrease of population was the principal cause.

The reduction of birth rates is always an outcome of excessive individualism. How is the birth condition in Europe? From about 1880 birth rates began to decline in all countries in Europe without a single exception, and continued to fall even after the World War. Germany and Italy have adopted measures to increase the population, but the result is very poor, while even this poor result is considered to be a passing phenomenon. The reduction of birth rates is accounted for by the fact that people maintain high living by reducing the number of children and that they like to give their children, such as they beget, high education and high social standing thereby. This is what scholars call social capillary phenomenon, or in other words, reduction of births in order to gratify their personal cravings for high social standing for themselves and their children. As society is becoming individualistic this tendency is extending. At

the outset

the outset this tendency prevailed among affluent and comparatively high classes, but now it has become a fashion among all classes. France is outstanding among the European nations in this tendency. Even before the World War, births did not offset deaths. If this sad condition continues, it is thought that the European nations, which have built up splendid civilizations and are commanding the world, will surely go into a decline sooner or later.

Let us think. There is a reason why any people, whose birth rates decrease, will decline. The reduction of birth rates of a people precludes expansion of population, although they do not get prosperous only by increased population; and they cannot maintain their standing as such without having a certain volume of population, while on the other hand, as a people increase rapidly, so their struggles among themselves become strenuous. How much the stagnancy of population impairs the progress of a nation is shown by France. It is certain, therefore, that the reduced birth rate itself tends to enervate a people and make them retrogressive. There is another reason. Excessive individualism expedites the reduction of birth rates, and leads the people to sure decline. As long as a people are severally taken up with egoism, so long they forget the good of the whole, and while the people are forgetful of the good of the whole and craving after personal influence, it is natural that they are going to decline. If the peoples of Europe do not give up the individualistic life of today, they will slowly but surely meet the fate that is in store for them.

(3) There are many reasons why peoples become individualists, but I will discuss here the reasons of the European peoples. Along with the development of capitalism, individualistic claims become compulsive. There are two reasons why capitalism

capitalism has rendered the white peoples' civilization still more individualistic. In the first place, capitalism teaches calculation and utility, and it is easy to see that capitalism, of which machines and profits are principal considerations, should come to such a consequence. In the second place, the white peoples have conquered many colored peoples and made their lands colonies and semi-colonies, where they have been extorting enormous profits to enrich and raise the living of the people at home in various ways, the enhancement of the standard of living, so far as there is no change in other circumstances, tends to intensify individual efforts to improve standing, wealth and income, and such efforts naturally entail the danger of forgetting the good of the whole, which accounts for the fact that the reduction of birth rates, which is regarded as a sign of excessive individualism, appears at first among high classes, but not among the lowest classes who suffer the the most from the oppression of living. Viewing the question from these points, it may be remarked that the development of capitalism in Europe under various circumstances, has tended to build up modern excessive individualism and is leading it in the course of decline.

From the standpoint of the European peoples, whose material civilization has reached the highest point of development, it may be possible for them, if they are able to monopolize such civilization, to keep up their position of conquerors, however strong the esprit de corps of the belated peoples may be. The so-called capitalistic economy, however, necessitates that they come into contact with other peoples, particularly with the backward peoples, to find markets for their supplus products and secure profitable resources; and their contact

with the

with the backward peoples must necessarily tend to raise demand for such products among them and at the same time facilitate them, by selling machines to them, to equip themselves for production and learn the arts therefor, and thus they create competitors for themselves. In this manner, although some backward peoples have declined under oppression of the white peoples and under other circumstances, other peoples have fitted out themselves strong enough to compete with the white peoples, in armament, in politics, and in economy. In economy, they are technically on the same level with the white peoples, but as they are on a lower level in wealth, they have advantage over the white peoples in the cost of production. In armament, they are able to keep parity with the white peoples due to development of material civilization, while they are not so deeply affected, as the white peoples are, by individualistic action of capitalism. Traditions sway strongly, and the whole is living in individual lives. The white peoples must, in spite of themselves, give way to the best of the rising peoples. Is not the world's history, which has been taking the course of the white peoples' supremacy, changing direction now? When we think that it is Japan which leads the change, we feel that our historic mission is by no means trivial.

(4) It need not be mentioned that it is based on the national constitution that Japan has become what she is today. It must be mentioned, however, that the absorption of the European capitalistic civilization is one of basic factors by which Japan has made historic progress in so short a time since the Meiji Restoration. Capitalism is a demon, nevertheless; it has given things to the white peoples and impaired their minds. By absorption of capitalism Japan has got things, but

it must

-6-

it must not lose its mind in return. To retain the mind, spiritual movement is necessary, while, at the same time, in view of the consequences of the limitless rise of the standard of living of the white peoples by their becoming individualists, especially among capitalists and other high classes, strict caution should be taken against a rise in the standard of living. There are opinions which favor a limitless rise of the standard of living for the sake of human-like living and human dignity, but if the country declines and the people perish, what is the good of human dignity? The government has been conducting an economization movement, but such a movement is not necessary only in wartime, it being necessary at all times for Japan to accomplish its world's historic mission. It is thought at the same time that it is our duty to think of the hardships of our brethren at the front, and lead such a life that we can endure any hardship and want "behind the guns".
