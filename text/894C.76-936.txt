THE ORIENTAL RADIO ASSOCIATION
HEADQUARTERS:
P.O. BXO 150.
DAIREN, SOUTH-MANCHURIA

October 1st, 1936

Messrs. Broadcasting Station,

Gentlemen:

A general meeting of the members is going to be held in Dairen, South-Manchuria this winter. Availing the chance of a large congregation of Radio men and men connected in the radio, and exhibition of a wide of various helpful data is planned. We hope very much to make this exhibition a success with your sympathetic co-operation.

W'not you be good enough to send us with your:
QRA CARDS
We should like to have THREE of FIVE Copies of them at your earliest convenience.

We shall be very thankful, if you would kindly comply with our request. If you seek any thing about the countries in this part of the world, please commends us, we will cheerfully furnish you.

Thanking you in advance for your kind assistance and prompt attention to our wishes, we are,

Yours faithfully,

THE ORIENTAL RADIO ASSOCIATION
[Signature]
President

=== Page 3 === (confidence 0.95)
October 1936

Reply refer to

My dear Mr. Baldwin:

I have received your letter of October 22, 1936, in regard to the procurement of information respecting the Oriental Radio Association which has headquarters at Dairen, South-Manchuria.

A very careful inquiry has been made in this Department, and the Department of Commerce and the Federal Communications Commission have also been consulted and I regret to say that it has been impossible to ascertain anything regarding this organization.

I suspect that the Oriental Radio Association is an organization of amateurs, although I cannot be certain, and I suggest that you write Mr. Kenneth Warner of the American Radio Relay League, Hartford, Connecticut, with a view to ascertaining whether this is true.

Sincerely yours,

For the Secretary of State:

Assistant Secretary.

Mr. James W. Baldwin,
Managing Director, National Association
of Broadcasters,
National Press Building,
Washington, D. C.
