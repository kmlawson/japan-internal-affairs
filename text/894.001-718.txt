=== Page 4 === (confidence 0.97)
The Japanese Consul held a reception at the Japanese Consulate from 11.30 to 12.30 p.m. on April 29, 1901, to celebrate the birthday of the Japanese Emperor. The imperial toast was proposed at noon by the Japanese Consul, Mr. G. Uchida. After the usual three "bananis", General Han Tung, representing General Liu Shen Hien, also proposed the Chinese "1,000 autumns" for the Japanese Emperor. It was obvious that the latter toast lacked the usual cordiality. All the officials of the port, as well as the leaders in the local commercial and civic circles (both foreign and Chinese) were present.

=== Page 6 === (confidence 0.98)
Considerable interest was aroused locally by the rumor that the League of Nations Commission might come to Tsingtao to prepare its report on the Sino-Japanese difficulties. Local innkeepers are especially favorable to this move.

The Japanese Consul General invited the local Chinese officials, the foreign Consuls and the leading business men of all nationalities to a reception at his residence on April 29th, the birthday of the Emperor of Japan. The reception was fairly well attended by all nationalities.

The Consul General was unable to receive his guests, being indisposed, and Consul Hori made a short formal address, proposing a toast to the Emperor in Japanese, which address was interpreted in Chinese by one of the Japanese Vice Consuls who spoke with an excellent accent...

Tokyo, April 8, 1920.

No. 93

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

I have the honor to transmit under separate cover two albums containing photographs taken at the time of the coronation of His Majesty the Emperor. The Imperial Coronation Office has requested, through the Department of Foreign Affairs, that these albums be forwarded to former Ambassador Charles MacVeagh and Mrs. MacVeagh, together with the note of presentation also transmitted herewith with translation attached.

I have the honor to be, Sir,
Your obedient servant,

A true copy of the signed original. [signature]

WTT/ER
Accompaniment.
Enclosure- AS stated.

W.R. Castle, Jr.
