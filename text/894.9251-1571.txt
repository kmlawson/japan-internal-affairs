EQUIVALENTS OF UNITS
The Equivalents of Units Tables were calculated from the official Japanese definitions as indicated at the top of each table.
In general the figures in the table are accurate to four significant figures.
The quantities on the same line are equivalents. The notation 0.(3)6214 indicates that the (3) is to be replaced by 3 ciphers.
A great deal of pressure was brought to bear by various professional and trade groups to adopt the metric system as the official
Japanese measuring system. A section of the Office of Home Affairs started the work of conversion with completion scheduled
for the year 1950. The work was interrupted by the war and as a result although in many instances metric units are used,
Japanese units of measurement are still being used and are official.
CONVERSION TABLES
The Conversion Tables provide a quick and easy method of converting certain common American units to Japanese units
and vice versa. For example, to convert 39.37 feet to shaku, take two numbers from the table on page 17 and add them together,
thus 39 feet=39.2278shaku and 0.37 feet=0.3722 shaku;then 39.37 feet=39.2278+0.3722=39.6 shaku.

SHAKU
The Japanese word, shaku, is used to denote a fixed unit of measurement. The same word, expressed in Japanese writing by the
same or a different character, is used to denote other fixed units of measurements. The determination as to which unit of measurement
is meant can only be made by context of the subject.
In +he table below the words, linear shaku, are used to indicate the Japanese basic unit of linear measurement, i. e., 0.9942 feet.
This is done for explanation purposes only and is never translated as such into Japanese.
1 shaku (linear-ordinary measure)
=0.9942 feet.
1 shaku (linear-cloth measure only) = 1.25 × linear shaku
=1.24274 feet.
1 shaku (area measure) =a square, 0.6 linear shaku long by 0.6 linear shaku wide which is
equal to 0.36 heihō (square) shaku
=0.3558 sq. ft.
1 heihō shaku (square shaku) = a square,1 linear shaku long by 1 linear shaku wide.
=0.9884 sq. ft.
1 shaku (volume measure) =a cube, 0.086547 linear shaku long by 0.086547 linear shaku wide
by 0.086547 linear shaku high which is equal to 0.(3)64827 rippō (cubic)
shaku
=0.(3)6370 cu. ft.
1 rippō shaku (cubic shaku) =a cube, 1 linear shaku long by 1 linear shaku wide by 1 linear
shaku high
=0.9827 cu. ft.
Similarly other Japanese fixed units of measurement, such as the sun, bu, rin, mo, shi, tan, tsubo (bu), koku, kan (kwan), etc.,
have other fixed values when used in different measurement tables.
