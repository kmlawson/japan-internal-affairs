CONFIDENTIAL

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

For the past two weeks rumors have been prevalent
in San José financial circles to the effect that the
Japanese owned Yokohama Specie Bank contemplated the
opening of a branch bank in San José to facilitate
exports of Japanese merchandise to Costa Rica.

On yesterday afternoon, I have the honor to inform
the Department, I made inquiry of the Minister for
Foreign Affairs as to the accuracy of this report.

Mr. Gurdián declared that the report was not true,
in so far as he knew, and that if by chance the Yokohama
Bank

Bank was considering the establishment of a branch in San José, the necessary authority would not be given by the Government of Costa Rica. The Minister for Foreign Affairs added that his Government is now taking steps to decrease the value and volume of Japanese exports to Costa Rica. In this connection it is of interest to report that opposition to Japanese merchandise seems to be gaining ground among Costa Rican consumers, and merchants taking note of this public sentiment are beginning to volunteer statements to purchasers that "this is not Japanese". In many cases they are obviously lying, but nevertheless much of the Japanese merchandise sold in this country which heretofore carried a "Made in Japan" mark no longer carries any identification mark.

Respectfully yours,

LEO M. RACK.
