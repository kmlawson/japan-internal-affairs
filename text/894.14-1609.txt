DEPARTMENT OF STATE

DEPARTMENT OF STATE RECEIVED
MAR 1 1948
DIVISION OF BIOGRAPHIC INFORMATION
SUBJECT: Transmittal of a Report Concerning Certain National Welfare Agencies in Japan.

The Acting Political Adviser has the honor to enclose three copies of a report dated December, 1947, prepared by the Public Health and Welfare Section of this Headquarters containing information with regard to the organization and programs of a number of large national welfare agencies which operate throughout Japan. Although these agencies are under private management, they have a close relationship to the Japanese Government.

Enclosure:
Report, December, 1947, concerning national welfare agencies in Japan, prepared by the Public Health and Welfare Section, General Headquarters, SCAP (five copies).
