NO. 415

SUBJECT: Travel of Mr. KITAMURA Hiroshi from Stockholm, Sweden to Zurich, Switzerland.

The United States Political Adviser has the honor to enclose copy of a telegram from Mr. KITAMURA Hiroshi, Foreign Correspondent in Stockholm for Yomiuri Shimbun, to his head office in Tokyo requesting permission to proceed from Stockholm to Zurich, Switzerland. There is also enclosed copy of a memorandum dated April 19, 1946, from the Central Liaison Office of the Imperial Japanese Government, in which it is requested that Mr. KITAMURA and his family be given permission to proceed to Zurich.

This Mission is in receipt of a communication from the Assistant Chief of Staff, G-2, of this Headquarters, stating that it has no objection to the granting of the above request. SCAP Headquarters has also informed us orally that it is not concerned with the return of Japanese from Europe. It has further requested that the matter be referred to the Department of State. The Department's comments are respectfully requested.

Enclosures:
1. Telegram from Mr. KITAMURA.

IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
CENTRAL LIAISON OFFICE

TO : GENERAL HEADQUARTERS OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS.
FROM : Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT : Request for Permission for Yomiuri Correspondent to Proceed to Switzerland from Sweden.

C.L.O. No. 1807 (RG) 19 April 1946

1. An application has been received by this Office from the Yomiuri Shimbun for permission from the General Headquarters for its correspondent in Stockholm, Sweden, Mr. Kitamura and his family, to proceed to Zurich, Switzerland.

2. The Swiss authorities have already granted entry visas to Mr. Hiroshi Kitamura, born November, 1909, his wife, Marie Louise, born April 1913, his sons, Hikaru and Tadashi born March 1943 and February 1946 respectively.

3. It is understood that the U.S. authorities in Sweden have no objection to Mr. Kitamura and his family leaving Sweden and entering Switzerland providing that the General Headquarters grant permission.

4. Mr. Kitamura wishes to proceed to Zurich and continue to act as the Yomiuri correspondent.

5. The Japanese Government would be obliged if the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, grant Kitamura and family permission to proceed as the Yomiuri Shimbun requests.

6. 3 copies of the telegram from Mr. Kitamura to Mr. Kori Tanaka, Foreign Editor of the Yomiuri Shimbun are attached hereto for your reference.

FOR THE PRESIDENT:

s/ S. Iguchi
T/ (S. Iguchi)
Director of General Affairs,
Central Liaison Office.
