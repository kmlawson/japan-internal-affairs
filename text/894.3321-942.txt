The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

In a personal letter, Vice Consul Raymond Phelan tells me that he has been informed by the Governor of the Department of Atlanticco that the Japanese Government plans to send two naval vessels to visit the port of Barranquilla.

As the Department will recall, the city's new maritime port was inaugurated on December 22, the ceremonies being attended by Admiral Williams with the U.S.S. OMAHA and MANLEY. Since that time, the port has been visited by British, Swedish, German and French war vessels.
