MOSCOW
Dated April 10, 1941
Rec'd 2:25 p.m.

Secretary of State,
Washington.

727, April 10, 3 p.m.
My 446, March 7, 2 p.m.

The Iranian Ambassador told me last night that the Soviet Union had recently purchased "seven hundred thousand dollars worth" of cotton from Iran. He said that he had thus far received no reply to his request for wheat excepting that the Soviet Union would not sell the same for dollars or any other currency but desired merchandise in return including among other things hides and cotton and that he would be advised at a later date whether "any wheat is available."

STEINHARDT
WTC
