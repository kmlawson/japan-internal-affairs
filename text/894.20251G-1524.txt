MEMORANDUM

Re: Japanese Intelligence in French Indo-China: Summary

The following is a list of the officers and staff of the Japanese Military Police of the Southern Area Command (North of Latitude 16 degrees) in French Indo-China. The list is current as of 1 September 1945, and was received from a source regarded as very reliable. The rank, position, and name of each Gendarme is given. It will be noted that many individuals were detached from their original assignments in the Japanese Army for reassignment to the KEMPEI TAI.

KASUGA Kaoru, Col.		Now in Hanoi, was Chief of the Military Police in French Indo-China.

OSHIMA Chikamatsu, Maj.		Became head of the Gendarmerie in Hanoi and Tonkin Province in February 1944; was Chief of the Secret Police of the Gendarmerie Headquarters. (The Secret Police of the Gendarmerie were the members of the Japanese military police group stationed at the Surete.)

SAITO Sadamu, Maj.		Chief of the Gendarmerie at Hue
