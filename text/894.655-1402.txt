Berlin Deutscher Reichsanzeiger November 15 published report from Japanese press showing wide changes in Japanese textile industry which began after outbreak hostilities with China accentuated by enemy embargoes July 1941 and recent war developments. Changes caused partly by government control, partly by voluntary adaptation to war needs status Japanese textile mills May 1943, partly corrected to August 1943, compared to commencement Japanese China's hostilities as follows: 35% still in operation, eighteen sold or leased, eight converted to temporarily expanded industries merged with other industries, seven used by own branches or subsidiaries, seven new installations in parts of old buildings, three converted by owners to affiliated industries, two held in reserve, one consolidated in own companies, one used as warehouses, ten used for unspecified purposes.
