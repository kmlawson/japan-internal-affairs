Tokyo, Japan, February 10, 1931.

Analysis of Semi-Anthracite Coal
from Northern Saghalien.

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to refer to the Department's Memorandum
of December 16, 1930, (#2260), conveying comment made by
the Mineral Division of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic
Commerce on my report of June 4, 1930, on the Foreign
Market Value &amp; Cost of Production of Anthracite Coal in
Russia", prepared in accordance with the Department's
instruction of May 10, 1930.

The Minerals Division states that the Coal Section
would be glad to have representative analysis of the semi-
Anthracite Coal mined in Russian Saghalien as well as data
on the production. Information in regard to boat loading
facilities is also desired.

There is no Semi-Anthracite Coal mined in Southern
or Japanese Saghalien.

Product of the Due Mine (Northern Saghalien)

Operated by the Northern Saghalien
Mining Company, Ltd.

Analysis
Moisture...1%
Volatile matter...22%
Ash...3%
Fixed carbon...72%
The coal is called the highest power caking coal.

Production

### Product of the Regatoi Mine (Northern Baghalien)
Operated by the Russian Government.

### Analysis
Moisture...1%
Volatile matter...19%
Ash...12%
Fixed carbon...69%

This is called middle power caking coal.

## Production
Estimated at 50,000 to 60,000 long tons annually.
Consumed as bunker coal at Vladivostock and for
other purposes in Russia.

### Product of the Meashi Mine (Northern Baghalien)
Operated by the Russian Government.

### Analysis
Almost the same as the Regatoi Coal.

## Production
Insignificant.

## Port Loading Facilities
It has not been possible to obtain information in
regard to Port Loading Facilities at Northern Baghalien.

Respectfully yours,

Arthur Garrels
American Consul General
