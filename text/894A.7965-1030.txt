I have the honor to report that at 1:53 P.M. yesterday, June 24, the two motored passenger plane operated on the Taiwan East Coast run by the Japan Air Transport Company crashed at Giran, Taihoku Province, Taiwan, resulting in the deaths of the pilot and four passengers, all of whom were Japanese. Giran is about twenty-five miles from Taihoku.

The ship, which was reported by the local press as "the crack 6 passenger plane of the East Coast Service, the ENVOY, (DOAX)", was returning to Taihoku from the southern terminus, Taito in Taito Province, and was flying at a height of approximately forty meters, preparatory to landing at Giran, when it was seen to suddenly plunge to earth in the outskirts of that city. The plane was entirely demolished and the pilot and passengers were killed instantly. The cause of the disaster is not known but it is believed that it was due to a sudden gust of wind which caused the pilot to lose

lose control of the plane long enough to precipitate it to earth, a mere 120 feet below.

This is the worst air disaster in the annals of commercial aviation in Japan and the third airplane accident in which passengers were killed. The first occurred at Fukuoka, Kyushu in 1931 when one passenger met death when the plane in which he was riding crashed to earth. The second disaster, which claimed the lives of two passengers, took place last year, also at Fukuoka.
