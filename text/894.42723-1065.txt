The Peruvian Economic and Cultural Mission which came to Japan in September departed on November 2, 1938, after a visit of about seven weeks' duration. The mission, visiting the principal industrial and tourist centers in Japan, Korea, and Manchuria, was lavishly entertained in the usual Japanese manner.* The Mission apparently was not empowered to make any binding commitments, but it did hold discussions with Japanese financial and industrial leaders. While the Japanese on their part brought forward a proposal that there be a more liberal policy toward Japanese immigration into Peru, discussion of the subject was successfully avoided and the matter was dropped. The Embassy was informed that the Peruvian Mission made the principal theme of discussion a request for larger cotton imports into Japan from Peru. The Japanese Government, on its part, was principally concerned with the desirability of opening negotiations for the conclusion of a new treaty of commerce and navigation. The results of the mission's discussions with Japanese interests were drawn up in a set of resolutions agreed to in a conference on October 5. These resolutions will be submitted

mitted to the Peruvian Government on the mission's return to Lima. The Mission agrees to inform the Peruvian Government of the desire of the Japanese Government for a commercial treaty, to recommend greater cooperation between Peruvian and Japanese Chambers of Commerce, banking institutions, shipping and communication facilities, to recommend the cooperation of Peruvian and Japanese technicians and capitalists in the exploitation of minerals and in the development of the fishing industry in Peru, and to urge the increased purchase of Japanese cotton goods by Peru.

On November 29 Dr. José de la Riva Aguero, former Peruvian Prime Minister, arrived in Japan as a "cultural envoy to Japan" to take part in the formation of a Japan-Peruvian Cultural Society. It is suggested that a Japan-Peruvian cultural pact will be concluded in the near future. No date has as yet been set for the inauguration of the Society but it is said that it will take place during the stay of Dr. Aguero who is to be here little more than a month and is to give a series of lectures.
