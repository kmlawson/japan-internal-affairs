March 2, 1939.

Mr. Stanley K. Hornbeck,
State Department,
Washington, D. C.

Dear Sir:

Thank you ever so much for the courtesies extended to both Mr. Wattawa and myself when we visited your office.

I am preparing the memorandum which I discussed with you, and will forward it to you upon its completion.

I have no doubt that the enclosed clipping is already part of your official file. However, since it directly has reference to our discussion, I am taking the liberty of sending it to you.

Very truly yours,
M. Kleiman
