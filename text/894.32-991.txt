REGARDING:

Discipline in connection with PANAY incident:
Recall of Admiral Mitsunami as the officer responsible for the PANAY incident was extremely severe punishment because it implies disgrace and the fact that he is no longer competent to command "on the field of battle". List of eleven naval officers against whom measures have been taken in accordance with law given Mr. Grew by the Minister.
