ENTRY OF ALIENS (INTO TAIWAN).

Taiwan Government General Order No. 7. February, 1918.

Article 1. When a foreigner arriving in Taiwan is considered to come under one of the following items, the governor or the prefectural chief may prohibit landing of such a foreigner:

1. A person who has no passport or a certificate of nationality.
2. A person likely to act against the interests of the Empire, or to assist enemy.
3. A person likely to act against peace and order and public morals.
4. A person who has the habit of loafing or begging.
5. Persons infected with contagious diseases of various kinds; or a person suffering from such disease as is dangerous to public health.
6. A person of mental derangement; feeble-minded person; a pauper; and other person likely to become a public charge.

The passport or the certificate of nationality mentioned in item 1 of the preceding paragraph shall have affixed thereon a photograph of the bearer, the passport or the certificate of nationality being issued by the government of his country, and visaed by a Japanese ambassador, minister or consular officer abroad within one year prior to landing in this country.

Article 2. A subject or a citizen of the country which does not require production of a passport or a certificate of nationality of a Japanese seeking entry into that country may be specially exempted from application of the provisions of item 1 of paragraph 1 of the preceding article, and a subject or a citizen of the country which does not require visa of that government's authorities on the passport or the certificate of nationality of the said Japanese may be exempted from application of the provision relating to visa in paragraph 2 of the said article.

Article 3. A foreigner arriving in Taiwan shall produce a passport or a certificate of nationality in compliance with the demand of a competent police officer, and shall make a true statement in answer to questions concerning investigation of the matters mentioned in each item of paragraph 1 of Article 1 and of other necessary matters.

Article 4. A person who has violated the provisions of the preceding article, or a person who has used a passport or a certificate of nationality in which another person's

person's name is mentioned, or who has obtained visa of his passport or certificate of nationality through fraudulent means may be prohibited landing by the governor or prefectural chief and may be deported to a territory outside the Japanese territory.

**Article 5.** The provisions of this order shall not apply to foreign ambassadors, ministers, staffs of embassies and legations, foreign consular officers and consular staffs, stationed in the Empire, their families, and persons on official duty of foreign governments.

Crew of foreign vessels calling at a port or ports in the Empire shall also be exempted from the application of the provisions of this order.

**Supplementary provisions.**

This order shall take effect from the day of its promulgation, provided, however, that the matters concerning passports or certificates of nationality shall be in force from February 20, 1918.

A foreigner who is on way to Taiwan on the day mentioned in the proviso of the preceding paragraph and who is considered to have reasons for inability to obtain a passport or a certificate of nationality or a visa thereon mentioned in Article 1 of this order may be exempted from application of the provisions concerning item 1 of paragraph 1 of Article 1 of this order.

REGULATIONS CONCERNING TREATMENT OF ALIENS.

Taiwan Government General Order No. 71. July, 1899.

Article 1. Any person accommodating a foreigner with a lodging shall submit within 24 hours a report to the competent police station setting forth the following items: The name, title, and name of place in a foreign country shall be written both in Japanese and in the original language:

1. Name.
2. Nationality.
3. Title and occupation.
4. Age.
5. Place of residence; address in foreign country if no place of residence.
6. Place of departure.
7. Date and time of arrival.

When the foreigner covered by the report mentioned in the preceding paragraph has left the lodgings, the date and the time of his departure, the place to which he removed, or his destination shall be reported to the competent police station within 24 hours, provided, however, that in the case of a person coming under the provisions of Article 4 the provisions of the said article shall apply.

The time within which the report mentioned in paragraphs 1 and 2 shall be submitted may be shortened by provincial and prefectural orders in the case of proprietors of hotels or others accommodating foreigners with lodgings for purpose of business. The report mentioned in paragraphs 1 and 2 may be submitted to a police box or a police officer on beat, excepting cases where there are provisions in provincial or prefectural orders.

Article 2. When demanded by the proprietor or custodian of the house in which a foreigner lodges, the foreigner shall give information concerning himself and any foreigner accompanying him required in submitting the report prescribed in the preceding article, or he shall fill in the blank furnished by the proprietor or the custodian.

Article 2-2. The provisions of the two preceding articles shall not apply to Chinese.

Article 3. A foreigner residing in Taiwan shall submit a report, giving the following items, to the competent police station within ten days after his residence concerning himself, his family and attendants accompanying him:

1. Name.

When a foreigner does not keep an independent household, the person who has accommodated the foreigner with a lodging shall jointly affix his signature on the report.

**Article 4.** A police station shall be provided with a register of foreigners, and shall register therein the items reported in accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 3, Article 5, and Article 6. Even in the absence of any reports the registration required in this article shall be made whenever the required facts are available in accordance with Article 8.

**Article 5.** When a foreigner registered in the register of foreigners removes, the following person shall within ten days after the removal report to the competent police station the date of removal and the place to which the foreigner has removed, provided, however, that when the person who removes submits the report himself the report shall be submitted before removal:

1. In the case of removal of a foreigner lodging in a house, the person who has accommodated the foreigner with the lodging.
2. When the family of a foreigner keeping an independent household remove, the said foreigner.
3. When a foreigner keeping an independent household removes himself and his family still remain in the said house, the adult who shall be the chief of the remaining family. If there is no such adult who shall be the chief, the oldest person of the adults.

4. When not coming under the preceding three items, the owner (landlord) of the house or custodian thereof.

Article 6. When a fact mentioned in items 1 to 20 of Article 6 of the Census Regulations has occurred in the case of a foreigner registered in the Register of Foreigners, the fact shall be reported to the competent police station within ten days after the occurring of the fact. The form prescribed in the Census Regulations will be applied to the report provided in this regulation.

Article 7. Any person may, by paying the following fees, inspect the Register of Foreigners, or request a copy thereof:

1. Inspection - 5 sen per family per inspection.
2. Copy - 20 sen per copy.

Article 8. A foreigner shall answer to questions by a police officer concerning information required to be reported in accordance with Article 1 or required to be registered in the Register of Foreigners, and when demanded he shall produce his passport in his possession or other document certifying to his nationality.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall apply to cases where a police officer questions a Japanese who has accommodated a foreigner with a lodging.

Article 9. The Governor General of Taiwan may order deportation from Taiwan of a foreigner coming under the following:

1. A person who is unable to earn his livelihood.
2. A person who roams about without a fixed residence.
3. A beggar.
4. A person who is considered harmful to peace and order or public morals.
5. A person who has again entered Taiwan without permission after having been deported.

The order for deportation may be executed by force after punishment in accordance with Article 10, or without application of the Article 10.

Article 10. A person who has not left Taiwan within the prescribed period after receiving an order for deportation in accordance with Article 9, and a person who has again entered Taiwan without permission after deported, shall be punished by a major imprisonment not exceeding four months or a fine not exceeding Yen 50.00.

Article 11. A person who, in violation of Article 8, does not comply with the demand of a police officer, or does not answer questions by the police officer, or who makes false answers, shall be fined not exceeding Yen 20.00 excepting cases where the penal code is applied.

Article 12. A person who has failed to submit the reports mentioned in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 1, Article 2, paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 3, and Articles 5 and 6, shall be fined not exceeding Yen 1.95, and a person who has submitted false reports shall be fined not exceeding Yen 20.00 excepting cases where the penal code is applied.

A person who has violated paragraph 3 of Article 3 shall be fined not exceeding Yen 1.95.

Article 13. In the case of a foreigner actually residing in Taiwan at the time of the enforcement of this regulation the period within which the reports prescribed in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 3 shall be submitted shall be calculated from the date of the enforcement of this regulation.

A person who has actually a foreign lodger in his house at the time of the enforcement of this regulation shall submit the report mentioned in paragraph 1 of Article 1 on the day of the enforcement of this regulation.

Article 14. Chinese shall be governed by respective regulations wherever there are such separate regulations.
