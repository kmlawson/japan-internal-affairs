# CROSS-REFERENCE FILE

## NOTE

### SUBJECT
Japanese plans for Madagascar:

Reports the French Ministry of Collonies has instructed the Governor General of Madagascar to investigate possible Japanese subversive activity on the island.

For the original paper from which reference is taken

See Tel. #376 11am.
(Despatch, telegram, instruction, letter, etc.)

Dated March 12, 1942
From To France (Vichy) (Leahy)

PARAPHRASE

STRICTLY

The following information was received from the Commandant of the Third Naval District. (new York)

CONFIDENTIAL INFORMANT RELIABILITY BAKER PRESUMABLY A FREE FRENCHMAN REPORTS VICHY HAD ARRANGED FOR ARRIVAL IN MADAGASCAR OF THREE SEPARATE JAPANESE MISSIONS WHOSE PURPOSE WAS TO COMPLETE PLANS FOR CLOSING THE STRAIT BETWEEN MADAGASCAR AND MOZAMBIQUE TO ALLIED SHIPPING. TWO OF THESE MISSIONS ALREADY ARE REPORTED ON MADAGASCAR AND A THIRD IS EITHER ON THE MOZAMBIQUE MAINLAND OR ON AN ISLAND IN THE STRAIT KNOWN (PHONETICALLY) AS "LAWRENCE" OR "LAURENZE".

DECLASSIFIED
E.O. 11652, Sec. 3(E) and 5(D) or (E)
