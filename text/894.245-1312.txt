Summarization of Report
When Required

There have been persistent reports in the past year of Japanese activity in the Paracel Islands, with rumors of the establishment of naval bases and air bases.

In Comments on Current Events Enter Item Heading Here

1. The Paracel Islands and Reef are located in the South China Sea at approximately 16° 30' N, 112° 00' E.

2. The Crescent Group, see sketch herewith, could possibly afford shelter for submarines. The islands of Robert, Pattle, and Money might be used for supply dumps. The commercial undertakings of the Japanese in past years, such as in gunno and phosphates, were probably blinds for military and naval development work. The phosphates are known to be below the normal grade utilized by the Japanese. Cells of brackish water were formerly in existence in several of these islands.

3. The Amphitrite Group, see sketch, includes only one usable island. Woody Island, see detailed sketch, may be suitable for a landing field. The surface of this island is flat, about 3/4 by one mile. The approach to the island by sea is difficult because of the reefs, but blasting could make passages for small craft. There is a pier on the side of Woody Island and there were at one time three water reservoirs.

J. K. EVANS,
Lt. Colonel, General Staff Corps,
