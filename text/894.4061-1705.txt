We enclose herewith application for permission for the
Transpacific Film Corporation to export moving picture films to

We are sending a copy of this letter to the Secretary of
Commerce, Secretary of War, Secretary of the Treasury and to General
Macarthur. Our reason for this is that we have tried for sometime
to get a definite answer as to what could be done along this line
which has resulted in our being sent from one agency to another and
we are therefore respectfully requesting that the matter be handled
by the offices in the various Departments having jurisdiction and that
we be advised exactly what must be done in order to secure permission
to start an export business in films to Japan.
At the present time the so-called Big Eight are operating
in Japan through a central company called the Motion Picture Export
Corporation and operate a Japanese office under the name of the
Central Film Exchange. The Transpacific Film Corporation distri-
butes only films of Independent companies such as Producers Releasing
Corporation, Eagle-Lion, Walter Wanger and many others. Under the
present arrangement the Big Eight have a monoply on the sale and
distribution of films in Japan and we are sure that it is not the
desire of the Government to create or foster monoply, and that the
situation is unhealthy from the standpoint of normal competition.
If any additional information is necessary in order to have
the matter passed on, it will be furnished promptly on advice as to
just what is needed.
We shall also be available at any time to see the proper
officials who may wish to have any questions which occur to them
answered. Our reason for presenting the matter to each of you
gentlemen is, as stated above, that it is practically impossible to
secure information as to the proper persons to see the proper Depart-
ments with which to file the application, and the necessary infor-

did and do not have the organization or the representation to adequately compete
