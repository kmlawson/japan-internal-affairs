## NOTE

### SUBJECT

Lt. Col. Eitoro Yosioka: First resident Japanese military attache in Bolivia, arrived at La Paz by rail from Peru. Bennett was one of Japanese officers to arrive in Lima in December. Utida and Kaneko are at present military attache and assistant military attache to Peru. Col. Hauro Teishann, Japanese military attache to Chile, was formerly accredited to Bolivia and Peru.
