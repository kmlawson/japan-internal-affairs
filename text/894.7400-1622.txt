Tokyo, November 26, 1947.

No. 1420

UNCLASSIFIED

SUBJECT: Payment for Press or Governmental Messages
Transmitted Over Direct Circuits Between
Japan and Certain European Nations.

The Acting Political Adviser has the honor to
enclose five copies of a directive from this Head-
quarters (SCAPIN 1823) of November 19, 1947 in-
structing the Japanese Ministry of Communications to
accept messages from press and governmental agencies
transmitted over direct circuits to Japan from Sweden,
Switzerland and Russia, only when charges have been
prepaid and to transmit such messages to those countries
collect. On the reverse of the directive there is
printed a memorandum prepared by this Headquarters in
which it is pointed out that this method of payment will
result in the accrual of foreign exchange credits for
Japan in the amount of the Japanese proportion of fees
collected in those countries. In addition it is noted
that similar arrangements have been in effect with re-
gard to messages transmitted over other circuits from
Japan for which Japanese currency is accepted in payment
of telegraphic charges.

In a letter of November 19, 1947 from this Head-
quarters, the Soviet Member of the Allied Council for
Japan and all diplomatic missions in Japan were re-
quested to advise their respective governments of the
contents of the above mentioned directive.

MEMORANDUM FOR: JAPANESE GOVERNMENT

THROUGH: Central Liaison Office, Tokyo

SUBJECT: Provision of Payment for Press and Government Messages Circulating between Japan and Europe

1. Effective 1 December 1947 the following provisions will apply to press and government messages circulating over the direct circuits between Japan and Sweden, Japan and Switzerland and Japan and Russia:

a. Press and government messages sent from Japan must be forwarded collect.

b. Press and government messages to Japan received over the subject circuits must be prepaid.

2. The Ministry of Communications will not accept press or government messages not in conformity with the provisions contained in 1a and 1b above.

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1823), file AG 311.23 (19 Nov 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 19 Nov 47, subject: Provision of Payment for Press and Government Messages Circulating between Japan and Europe

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Radiotelegraph circuits between Japan and Sweden and Japan and Switzerland were uninterrupted during the war. The radiotelegraph circuit between Japan and Russia was interrupted for only a short period and was found to be operating when the Occupation Forces entered Japan. These circuits were ordered to be continued in operation by the provisions of paragraph 15b of SCAPIN 2 dated 3 September 1945.

3. Present arrangements permit press and government messages to be sent prepaid from Japan and collect into Japan over the subject circuits. This condition, when applied, is unfavorable to Japan's economy and foreign exchange position since it results in payment for such messages being made in Japanese yen currency.

4. The sending of press and government messages collect from Japan and prepaid into Japan over the subject circuits will result in foreign exchange credits for Japan in the amount of the Japanese proportion of the charges collected for such messages in the countries where the foreign terminals are located. Such arrangements have already been effected on other circuits from Japan where Japanese yen currency is employed as the medium for payment of telegraph charges.

5. The subject memorandum requires all press and government messages to be sent collect from Japan and prepaid into Japan over the circuits with Sweden, Switzerland, and Russia.

MEMORANDUM FOR: JAPANESE GOVERNMENT

THROUGH: Central Liaison Office, Tokyo

SUBJECT: Revised Plan for Receiving Payments Due American Carriers for Messages and Radiotelephone Calls Filed by United States Military and Accredited Personnel for Transmission To or Through the United States, Its Territories and Possessions, Prepaid in Japan

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 311.3 (24 Sep 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1228), dated 24 Sep 1946, subject as above, as amended.

b. File AG 676.3 (27 May 47)CCS (SCAPIN 1707), dated 27 May 1947, subject: Establishment and Operation of Direct Telecommunications Circuits Between Tokyo and Manila.

2. So much of paragraphs 11, 12 and 13 of SCAPIN 1228 and paragraph 15 of SCAPIN 1707 pertaining to the rendering of statements of accounts on a semimonthly basis and making deposits in accordance therewith, is amended as follows:

a. Effective 1 February 1943, the Ministry of Communications will render statements of accounts covering telecommunications operations between Japan and the United States, and between Japan and Manila, and make the deposits in accordance therewith on a monthly, instead of a semimonthly basis as heretofore, the period being the first to the last day of the month inclusive.

b. The Ministry of Communications will submit to the Transfer Agent within thirty days after the close of each monthly period, five copies of a preliminary statement of accounts, accompanied by the deposits in accordance therewith, and the final traffic accounting statements, showing payments made, and adjustments, if any.

c. In addition, the Ministry of Communications will render a monthly report in triplicate, not later than five days after the close of each monthly period to the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, showing the exact amount in Military Payment Certificates and Foreign Trade Payment Certificates collected by each of its public offices during the period, as well as the exact amount withheld by each public office for change making purposes.

MEMORANDUM FOR: JAPANESE GOVERNMENT

SUBJECT: Japanese Overseas Radiotelephone Service

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government file AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47) CCS SCAPIN 1830 dated 6 December 1947, subject: Japanese Overseas Radiotelephone Service, as amended.

2. Radiotelephone calls between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1 attached hereto, via the United States, are authorized, effective 1 March 1948, subject to the following terms and conditions:

a. The provisions of paragraph 2 of SCAPIN 1830/1 applicable to calls from Japan to Canada, Cuba, and Mexico, will apply equally to calls from Japan to the points listed in inclosure 1 of this memorandum, effective 1 March 1948.

b. The provisions of paragraphs 3c and 3d of SCAPIN 1830/1, applicable to calls from the United States, Hawaii, Canada, Cuba, and Mexico to Japan, will apply equally to calls to Japan from the points listed in inclosure 1 of this memorandum, effective 1 March 1948.

3. Rates and division of revenue for the service herein authorized are shown in inclosure 1.

4. Collect calls from any person or organization in Japan to Hawaii are authorized effective 15 March 1948.

5. Routine operating practices, including rules and procedures governing placing, acceptance, and routing of calls incoming to Japan will be as directed by the Commanding General, Eighth Army.

6. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section or the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding operating and accounting procedures within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.
