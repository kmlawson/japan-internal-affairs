DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
July 12, 1940

Tokyo's despatch no. 4767, June 11, 1940,
"Proposed Changes in the Japanese Civil Service".

The Embassy submits an outline of press reports of suggested changes in Japan's civil service system which are now under consideration by the Cabinet. Changes suggested:

(1) Consolidation of administrative and diplomatic higher civil service examinations. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has not opposed the consolidation but has objected to the elimination of a foreign language as a required subject for the entrance examination into the diplomatic service.

(2) Abolition of the ordinance guaranteeing the personal status of civil service employees. This is a measure to curb the power of the bureaucracy which, due to its protected status and the increase in governmental functions, has grown rapidly since 1932.

(3) Increase of salaries of minor government officials; a measure to meet needs arising from higher living costs.

(4) Adoption of a policy which will permit the employment from outside the ranks of the civil service of "private experts and men of ability".
