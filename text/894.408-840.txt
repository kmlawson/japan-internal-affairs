# YOUNG MEN'S ASSOCIATION
(Seinendan)

1. Purpose.
To give physical, mental, moral, and civic training to young men no longer in school.

2. Membership.
a. Membership is open to young men between the ages of 13 and 25. 56% of the members are under 20.
b. Approximately 65% of the members are employed in agriculture, 12% in commerce, and 10% in industry, and 5% in the fishing industry.

4. Leaders.
a. Home Minister Fumio Goto is president of the Federation of Young Men's Associations. Mr. Yoshisuke Tazawa is one of the principal directors. It is reported that most of the important leaders of the association are members of the Kokuihai, a society organized principally by bureaucrats for the purpose of bringing about a moral renaissance of the nation.
b. 56% of the leaders are school teachers, 13.5% are farmers (chiefly landlords), and 13% are local officials.

5. Activities include an annual track and field meet at the Meiji Shimao Aikbukio Field in Tokyo; an annual conference; the publication of a pamphlet, a newspaper, and a magazine; the encouragement of awareness to historical places; assistance to the police and fisankuikan time of earthquake or other emergency; and the concept of a number of courses of training for students. The Japan branch of the Young Men's Association.
