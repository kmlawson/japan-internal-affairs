CROSS-REFERENCE FILE
NOTE

SUBJECT: "Referring to my despatch no 1070 of October 10, 1960, enclosing a report entitled 'Paradish, Nazi, Communist and Japanese activities in Haiti' and to the discussion therein of the activities of the Haitian branch of the Data Show Company, I have the honor to report that Frantisek Votava who the name of Frank Votava and states he is the manager of the show factory of Chausseeus Data Haiti, S.A., contemplates visiting the U.S. for three to six months from November, 1960, on company business, i.e., to purchase materials.

"The Consular Section of this Mission has no information on which to base a refusal of a temporary visitor's visa, but is is inclined to view with suspicion all persons connected with Chausseeus Data, Haiti, S.A. Since Mr. Votava spent one year in the United States, according to the notations appearing on the application for non-immigrant visa (enclosed) executed by Frantisek Votava at the American Consulate General, Prague, Czechoslovakia on July 28, 1960, the Department may have some information concerning him. Mr. Votava intends to apply for a visa on Wednesday, October 28, and I should appreciate receiving telegraphic instructions prior to that date.

"I should also appreciate being informed whether anyone has a right to retain in his possession an expired visa such as that presented by Mr. Votava and transmitted herewith."
