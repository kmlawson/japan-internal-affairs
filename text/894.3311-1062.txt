December 8 1938.

In reply refer to
PR 894.3311/501

The Acting Secretary of State presents his compliments to the Honorable the Secretary of the Interior and refers to the letter of October 29, 1938, from the Department of State concerning the visit of the Japanese transport Shiriya to Los Angeles from November 13 to November 18, and Honolulu from November 29 to December 3, 1938.

The Department of State is now in receipt of a note from the Japanese Ambassador at Washington stating that the Japanese transport Shiriya was compelled to change her schedule for visiting Honolulu and arrived there on December 4, 1938. Having arranged to obtain material for repair of the Shiriya by the motorship Chichibu Maru, which is due at Honolulu on December 16, the transport is planning to stay at that port until the repair work shall have been finished.

Similar communications have been addressed to the Secretary of War and the Secretary of the Navy.

A true copy of the signed original.

P

=== Page 3 === (confidence 0.94)
The Acting Secretary of State presents his compliments to His Excellency the Japanese Ambassador and has the honor to acknowledge the receipt of his note no. 219, dated December 5, 1938, stating that the Japanese transport Shiriya was compelled to change her schedule for visiting Honolulu, arriving there on December 4, 1938 and plans to stay at that port until certain repair work shall have been completed.

The Acting Secretary of State has communicated with the interested Federal Departments with a view to the extension to the vessel of the courtesies and facilities usual on such occasions.

F/A

Department of State,
December 8 1938.
