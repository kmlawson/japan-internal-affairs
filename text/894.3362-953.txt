Courtesies and privileges extended to naval officers of the Japanese Cruiser ASHIGARA during visit to Kiel.

894.3362/1

Inclosure: (A) Newspaper Photograph.

1. As mentioned in the report submitted as reference (a), special consideration and attention have been shown by the German Government to the officers of the Japanese Cruiser ASHIGARA during its recent visit to Kiel, not only in Kiel but in Berlin.

2. Considered quite unusual and as a mark of special consideration was the audience given to officers from this Cruiser by Der Führer — Herr Hitler.

3. Special opera parties attended by Admiral Raeder, other high officials of the Nazi Government and Japanese officers were given in Berlin.

4. Japanese sailors under arms participated in the "Skagerrak Day" Celebration in Berlin and Kiel, Unter dem Linden being lined with German school children bearing Japanese flags.

5. The Japanese flag was displayed at half-mast on the Japanese Embassy conforming to the German orders of the day (31 May) requiring all German flags to be at half-mast in respect for those killed on the DEUTSCHLAND. As far as is known, the Japanese Embassy was the only Embassy displaying its own national colors.

6. Japanese officers have had unusual privileges extended them both in Berlin and in Kiel, in that they have been permitted to visit plants and activities not customarily shown to foreigners — particularly officers of the foreign Military and Naval Services.

7. These incidents are being reported as having some bearing on the German-Japanese Agreement announced in 1936 and the possibility of an understanding for the collection and exchange of information under this agreement between the two countries.
