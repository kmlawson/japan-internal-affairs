NOV 9 - 1942
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

To the Holders of the following bonds:

JAPANESE GOVERNMENT 6½% of 1924, due Feb. 1, 1954
JAPANESE GOVERNMENT 5½% of 1930, due May 1, 1965
CITY OF TOKIO 5½% of 1927, due Apr. 1, 1961
CITY OF YOKOHAMA 6% of 1926, due Dec. 1, 1961
TAIWAN ELECTRIC POWER CO. 5½% of 1931, due July 1, 1971

The New York branch of the Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd. was the Paying
Agent for the above named Japanese bonds. After Japan made war on the United
States last December, that Agency was taken over by the Superintendent of Banks
of the State of New York and the Bank is now being liquidated by him. The Super-
intendent of Banks of the State of New York has issued a form to be filled in
by those wishing to file a claim against funds of the Bank. In case the matter
may not have come to your attention, there is enclosed herewith a copy of this
Proof of Claim and a Notice to Creditors of the Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd. for
such action, if any, as you may care to take in the matter. The Council is not
in a position to give any legal advice to the bondholders.

FOREIGN FUNDS CONTROL DIVISION
NOV 6 - 1942
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

14057 La Secretaría de Estado de Relaciones Exteriores presenta sus cumplimientos a la Embajada de los Estados Unidos de América, en ocasión de rogarle se sirva informar a esta Cancillería si el Superintendente de Bancos en Posesión de los Negocios y Propiedades en la ciudad de New York del Yokohama Specie Bank, Limited, es un funcionario del Gobierno de los Estados Unidos de América, y si tiene capacidad legal para recibir los efectos de comercio librados por Sanko &amp; Co., Limited, de Osaka, contra firmas comerciales establecidas en este país.

14057 The Department of State for Foreign Affairs presents its compliments to the Embassy of the United States of America and has the honor to request that office to inform it whether the Superintendent of Banks in charge of Business and Properties in the City of New York of the Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd., is an authority of the Government of the United States of America and whether he has legal capacity to receive commercial documents drawn by Sanko &amp; Co., Ltd., of Osaka, against commercial firms located in this country.

August 10, 1943

It appears that the questions raised in despatch no. 958, July 15, 1943, from Ciudad Trujillo involves legal considerations which FF does not feel competent to answer and which appear to fall appropriately within the jurisdiction of Le.

It would be appreciated if such reply as Le may make to the underlying despatch were routed through FF.

As Le is undoubtedly aware, for some months now the Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd., New York branch, has been in the process of liquidation by the Superintendent of Banks of the State of New York in connection with the Alien Property Custodian, and the Treasury Department has for some time past been referring inquiries in regard to this bank directly to the Superintendent of Banks.

My dear Mr. Murray:

There has recently been received a despatch from the American Embassy at Buenos Aires, with reference to a letter which you addressed to the Banco de la Provincia de Buenos Aires at Buenos Aires, Argentina, dated May 26, 1943. The Embassy was requested by the Banco de la Provincia de Buenos Aires to transmit the reply to you in a confidential manner as the Banco de la Provincia de Buenos Aires is a semi-official institution and the firm through whom the payment was made is the Banco de Brasil, a government institution. The information which the bank asked the Embassy to convey to you is set forth below:

"The Banco de la Provincia de Buenos Aires states that the payment of Yokohama Draft 1392 for $230.00 was made on July 7, 1941 by postal transfer in favor of the account of the Banco de Brasil at the Guaranty Trust Company, New York."

Sincerely yours,

Dear Mr. Hackworth:

In the absence of the Superintendent, I acknowledge receipt of your letter of August 10, 1943, inquiring on behalf of the Dominican Foreign Office about the powers of the Superintendent of Banks in the liquidation of the business of the New York Agency of Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd.

Under authority conferred upon him by Section 606 of the Banking Law of the State of New York, the Superintendent took possession of the New York Agency of Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd. in December, 1941. Authorization to liquidate the Agency was issued by the Treasury Department to the Superintendent of Banks on January 14, 1942. This license was replaced by a supervisory order of the Alien Property Custodian on September 28, 1942.

Very truly yours,

W. A. Lyon
Assistant to the Superintendent
