Subject: List of Organizations in Japan Handling
Economic, Cultural, Religious, Propaganda,
Educational, Press, Colonization, Tourist,
and Other Endeavors in the Greater East
Asia Ophers.

STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

I have the honor to enclose a comprehensive list
of organizations as published in the Nippon Times
Weekly for April 29, 1943, which are said to handle
Japanese interests in East Asia covering a wide field
of endeavor as listed in the subject of this despatch.

Although this list is some ten months old, it is
believed that it will prove of value for reference
purposes, in view of the comparative non-availability
of Japanese reference works since the start of the war.

This issue of the Nippon Times Weekly was loaned
to the Embassy by former Argentine Consul Ferdinand
Bidabehere of Yokohama, as mentioned in despatch No.
13965 of February 24, 1944.

Respectfully yours,

For the Ambassador:

Hugh Gillard
First Secretary of Embassy

Enclosure:
List of Japanese Organizations, as stated.
