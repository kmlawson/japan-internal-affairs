CONSULATE GENERAL OF JAPAN
500 FIFTH AVENUE
NEW YORK, N.Y.

February 22, 1935

Mr. Eugene Dooman
Division of Far Eastern Affairs
Department of State
Washington, D. C.

Dear Mr. Dooman:

I am writing to introduce to you Mrs. William M. Hosken of the Hosken Trading Company of New York.

Mrs. Hosken says that she and Mr. Hajime Hoshi, of the Hoshi Pharmaceutical Company, have been negotiating for a plan to promote American-Japanese trade in pharmaceutical products on a considerably large scale. She wishes to know the opinions of the official circles in Washington on this plan for cooperation between the two countries for the promotion of pharmaceutical trade.

Interested as I am in exhausting every possible means of promoting American-Japanese trade which will be of mutual benefit, I shall indeed be happy to act as an intermediary between Mrs. Hosken and Mr. Hoshi in so far as the Japanese side is concerned, in case the plan should materialize.

I am introducing Mrs. Hosken to you, as I think that you may know Mr. Hajime Hoshi yourself. I shall appreciate it if you will kindly meet Mrs. Hosken and then introduce her to some official who may be interested in pharmaceutical trade. If you could be good enough to grant her an interview, will you kindly let me know the time and date when it will be convenient for you to have her call on you at your office?

Very truly yours,

Renzo Sawada.

RS:SM

Renzo Sawada, Esquire,

Dear Mr. Sawada:

I acknowledge the receipt of your letter of February 22, 1935, making known to me Mrs. William M. Hosken of the Hosken Trading Company of New York.

You may be perfectly sure that, should Mrs. Hosken call on me, I shall be pleased to render her all appropriate assistance.

With all best wishes, believe me,
Sincerely yours,
Eugene H. Dooman
