SUBJECT: New Port in Taiwan Named Niitaka.
THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE
WASHINGTON

SIR:

I have the honor to refer to voluntary reports submitted by this office on March 18 and September 27, 1938 regarding a new port to be established at Gosei, Taichu Province, Taiwan, and to the voluntary report of September 16, 1939 "The Industrialization of Taiwan" and to state that the port has been officially named "Niitaka Ko" or "Port of Niitaka".

Ceremonies were held at the site of the new port on September 25 when Governor General Kobayashi officially christened the port "Niitaka". More than 800 persons, including officials of the Government General, local governors, and prominent business men, attended the ceremonies.

Respectfully yours,

John K. Emmerson
American Vice Consul.
