May 31st., '48

Bureau of Foreign Affairs,
Washington D.C.

Dear Sir;-

If consistent would like to have the information, as just who I should contact, as to making a contract, with some one or the Japanese Government, whereby I can ship, Lily of The Valley Bulbs and Croft Lily Bulbs to Japan, and in due course have the increase of bulbs returned to me. I have heard that this is being done, and if you can send me particulars will be most grateful.

Yours truly,

H.W. Croft

In reply refer to
CE

My dear Mr. Croft:

This is in reply to your letter of May 31, 1948, requesting information concerning the export and import of lily bulbs to and from Japan.

I suggest that you write to the Japanese Board of Trade and request that you be put in touch with a dealer familiar with your proposed practice. You may address your letter as follows:

Becki-Che
Kasumigaseki,
Chiyoda-Ku,
Tokyo, Japan.

I hope that the Board of Trade will be able to direct you to a satisfactory correspondent. It might be well to check with the U.S. Department of Agriculture concerning your ability to reimport the bulbs.

Sincerely yours,

A P
