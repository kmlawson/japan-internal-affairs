# LIST OF PAPERS

Subject: Cultural relations between Japan and Mexico.
Exchange of professors and students. Attendance
of Mexican students in schools of Japan.

1939

|  R No. | FROM | DATE | TO — | SUB No. | PERPORT  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Mexico (Daniels) | June 19 |  | NOTE | Efforts of Japanese to establish themselves in Mexican fisheries. Discussion of past -, in providing experts to assist Mexicans, and furnishing of scholarships for three Mexican students, two of whom have been in employ of Mex. Fisheries Dept. Offer pending before Mexican Govt, to take at least one Mexican student to Japan from Mexican Fisheries, without cost to Mexico. Japanese efforts likely to increase with more critical conditions in the Far East. SEE 812.628/540.  |
