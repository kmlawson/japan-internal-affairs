Do you wish to send a copy of this despatch from Dairen and its enclosures to Commerce? The same subject matter, namely the promulgation of Ordinance No. 324 by Japan, applicable to industrial property in the Kwantung Leased Territory is discussed in the despatch from the Embassy at Tokyo, also attached hereto. The Embassy's despatch discusses the political phase of the question and if it is considered desirable to inform Commerce of the promulgation of the ordinance it would seem to be sufficient to send a copy of the consular despatch and its enclosures.
