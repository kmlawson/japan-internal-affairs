Sir:

I have the honor to transmit two tabulations compiled from the returns of the census of October 1, 1930, as published by the Kwantung Leased Territory government in its official gazette, issue of December 18, 1930. Statistics for both the Kwantung Leased Territory and the South Manchuria Railway Zone are included as released by the Territorial government. Although the Zone lies within the Mukden consular district and has no doubt already been reported on in this matter by the Mukden Consulate General, the census returns for the Zone are herewith set forth for ease of comparison.

The population of the Kwantung Leased Territory has increased one-fourth (24.8%) in the period 1925 to 1930, having more than doubled the rate of increase of the preceding five years; and now it numbers almost one million persons. Seven-eighths of these are Chinese, which is the same proportion as in 1925. Among the Chinese the excess of males over females has become slightly aggravated (57.9% to 58.5%), but among the Japanese the excess has been slightly diminished

(53.3% to 52.5%). In area the Territory equals the state of Rhode Island, but the population exceeds Rhode Island's by almost half. The population of no state in the United States has so high an average density.

During the same period 1925 to 1930 the population of the South Manchuria Railway Zone has increased at a slightly more rapid rate (29.1%). Its population now approaches four hundred thousand persons. In 1925 64.9% of the population of the Zone were Chinese; in 1930 this percentage has risen to 66.4%. Males formed 80% of the Chinese population of the Zone in 1925; in 1930 this excess has fallen to 75.9%. In 1925 55% of the Japanese in the Zone were males, and this proportion has remained practically constant.

Respectfully yours,

Cabot Coville,

According to figures appearing in the issue for February 24, 1938, of the Official Gazette of the Kwantung Government, the total population of the Kwantung Leased Territory, at the end of December 1937, numbered 1,190,003. This figure represents an increase over the population figure for 1936 by 41,969. Males outnumber females by 172,393, indicating that a considerable portion of the population may be regarded as unstable.

The population may be divided by nationality or races as follows: Chinese 1,009,870; Japanese 174,587; Koreans 3,917; foreigners 1,629. A considerable portion of the total population is concentrated into the five principal cities and their suburbs: Dairen 503,778; Port Arthur 175,227; Pulantien 187,189; Pitzuwo 166,221; Chinchow 157,588.

Kwantung Government have been consulted in connection with this matter and they confirm that the population figure for Dairen as of December 1937, 503,778, given in the Consulate's despatch above-mentioned, is correct.

In regard to the population figures for other cities of the Kwantung Leased Territory, it is regretted that the figures for the police supervisory areas centering in those cities were mistakenly inserted under the headings indicated. The correct figures are: Port Arthur 31,439; Chinchow 21,473; Pulantien 21,320 and Pitzuwo 19,780.

Respectfully yours,

William T. Turner
American Consul
