February 17, 1933.

MRS:
TED:
ATT:
MCH:

Dairen's despatch of January 16, 1933, to the Embassy reports on the budget of the Kwantung Government as submitted to the Diet. This budget balances at yen 25,864,792, which is yen 3,128,776 over the budget of the previous year. Increased ordinary revenue is expected to be raised by increased taxation. The estimate of revenue does not include revenue from the opium monopoly because this revenue is remitted directly to the National Treasury.

Under the heading of "Extraordinary Revenue" is a grant of 5,000,000 yen from the National Treasury as compared with 4,000,000 the year before, and yen 3,226,643 from a loan. The latter amount appears in the list of extraordinary expenditures as "expenses for the Manchuria situation". The practice of borrowing money is new for the Kwantung Government.

An increase of yen 464,645 was noted by the Consul in the expenditure provided for the operation of the Opium Monopoly Bureau. His suggestion that this increase might
