DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS

April 19, 1941.

Tokyo's despatch 5461, March 24, 1941.

Well covered by telegrams and separate despatches.

Also notes: Budgets passed by the Diet for the fiscal year 1941-42 were
General account ¥ 6,863,000,000
Temporary war expenditures account 4,880,000,000
Supplementary account 1,132,000,000
Total ¥ 12,875,000,000

an increase of ¥1,241,000,000 over last year.

To meet these expenditures national bonds totaling ¥7,555,000,000 will be issued during the fiscal year, an increase of ¥550,000,000 over the previous year. The Finance Minister stated that the Government would not resort to compulsory bond sales to the public.

Strenuous efforts to increase industrial production have met with little success since the first of the year. Wholesale and retail prices increased .5 percent during the month. A decision was reached to reduce silk basins by 36 percent and reeling by 15 percent. Silk prices increased somewhat. Indications were given of some improvement in Japan's foreign trade. Restrictions on electric power were withdrawn.
