77, November 17, 5 p. m.

The Japanese cruiser MYOKO with Vice Admiral Shiozawa
commanding officer fifth cruiser squadron paid a courtesy
call at Hong Kong today arriving at 9:00 o'clock A. M.
and departing at half past two P. M. This visit is con-
sidered of special significance because it is the first
time in more than two years that a Japanese warship has
called at Hong Kong and the purpose of the visit is
understood to be an opening move towards closer relations
between Japanese and British naval forces in South China
waters. There is an unconfirmed report from official
sources that the British Admiral in command here will
shortly return the courtesy by a visit in Japan in his
flagship. Japanese Admiral and officers were entertained
at luncheon by the British Admiral here. Many people
visited the Japanese cruiser including Italian Consul
General whose reception by the Admiral, according to a
responsible American newspaper correspondent with whom I
talked,
