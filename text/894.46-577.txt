3 State Dept. Sun (Dunn) Jun.28
M.M.D.

Special broadcast planned for July 4th: Informs of -.Japanese Broadcasting Co., has arranged a special program which will include a message by Japanese Foreign Minister and Mr. Grew. N.B.C. has arranged a musical program immediately preceding the Japanese broadcast, and would like to have some one from State Dept. give a brief talk, appropriate to the occasion. News: 111.018

Special July 4th Broadcast, and request of National Broadcasting Company that the Secretary make a short talk. Discusses subject matter of message and whether the Secretary would talk in person, whether a message should be read by someone else, or whether another official should make the talk. Has asked Mr. Berkeley to inform him definitely whether Hirota will speak in person July 4th Broadcast: Mr. Berkeley of National Broadcasting Company telephoned that Hirota would not talk on this occasion, but Prince Tokugawa had been substituted on the program. In view of this arrangement, the Company is trying to get Mr. Taft, President of the Japan Society of New York, or Mr. Castle, or someone not having an official position, but having some connection with Japanese affairs to speak on this occasion. Mr. Dunn stated this arrangement would be appropriate.
