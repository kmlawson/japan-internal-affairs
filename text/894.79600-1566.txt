Dear Bunce:

The Department is presently engaged in drawing a policy paper covering the entrance and operation of civil airlines to and within Japan. The policy will apply only to international lines since it is contemplated that there will be no domestic lines in the near future. The international lines may, however, be permitted to render domestic service.

One of the lines (Northwest) soon to enter Japan will also enter and serve Seoul, Korea. The military Government has already approved the flights and scheduled service will probably begin in late spring or early summer. We are undecided as to the need for a policy paper covering civil aviation to and within southern Korea, since the basic conditions of the occupation differ considerably from those in the case of Japan. We should like to draw upon your familiarity with political developments and situations there; would you regard it advisable for us to prepare and issue a civil aviation policy paper for southern Korea?

Your comments will be greatly appreciated.

Sincerely yours,

Edwin M. Martin
Chief
Division of Japanese and Korean
Economic Affairs

Mr. Arthur C. Bunce,
Economic Adviser,
Staff XXIV Corps,
Seoul, Korea.
