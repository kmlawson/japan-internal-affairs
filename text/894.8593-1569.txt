STANDARD-VACUUM OIL COMPANY
26 BROADWAY
NEW YORK 4.

April 17th, 1947.

YOKOHAMA
Bunkering of Commercial Vessels

Mr. Edwin M. Martin, Chief,
Division of Japanese &amp; Korean Economic Affairs,
State Department,
Washington, D. C.

Dear Mr. Martin:

We have been informed by the ANPB that they would be agreeable to turning over to oil companies the bunkering of American and other foreign flag vessels in Japan. The quantities, we understand, will run between 60,000 and 100,000 barrels monthly. It is further understood that payments for bunkers would be made in US$ or in sterling, and thus the question of an exchange rate for Yen would not enter into the transactions. The bunkering of non-Japanese vessels at Yokohama would, we believe, be of assistance to and relieve somewhat the burden on the Occupation Forces.

If the above suggestion meets with the approval of the interested departments, it is suggested that one of our employees now in Japan be authorized to investigate and report to us the feasibility of using present equipment at our Tsurumi (Yokohama) Installation and if additional equipment is needed to properly supply bunkers. At the present time we understand that at least three large tanks at this terminal are being used or can be used for the storage of petroleum products.

A copy of this letter is being sent as information to the ANPB and the Petroleum Division.
