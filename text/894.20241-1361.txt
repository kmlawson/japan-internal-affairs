Washington.

868, October 3, noon.

STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL
The informant referred to in my 867, October 3, 11 a.m., tells me that the Japanese diplomatic and consular representation in London is providing the Germans with very valuable information; further, that useful information concerning England is continually reaching Germany by way of Sweden.

My informant went on to say that a Japanese by the name of Genji Miura who was formerly connected with the Kokusia Kisen Kaisha Holland House London is the head of a spy organization working for Germany. There is also a Swede named Svenson either a minor employee of the Swedish Legation or else a newspaperman who is also rendering Germany excellent services.

GUNTHER

NPL
