Division of Far Eastern Affairs
MAR 12 1932
3/15/32
Dept. of State

D P:

The only thing to be dealt with in ack. this letter from McCune seems to be the "audience" of Mr Hirayama with the President, which, presumably, you should answer.

I see no need of referring to anything else.

If you concur, will you please draft, and let us see reply for information only.

I would suggest that reply go through our Consul General in Seoul, Chosen, unless you have some other system which you prefer to follow in regard to such matters.

J. K. Caldwell

JKC
