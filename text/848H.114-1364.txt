September 12, 1942

In reply refer to
FE 848H.114 Narcotics/11

The Secretary of State presents his compliments to the Honorable the Secretary of the Treasury, and encloses herewith, for the records of the Treasury Department, a copy of the annual report of the Government of Bechuanaland Protectorate on the traffic in opium and other dangerous drugs for the year 1941 submitted under the narcotics Limitation Convention of 1931 and transmitted, under the terms of that Convention, by the Secretary General of the League of Nations.

Two additional copies are also enclosed for the information of the Commissioner of Narcotics and the Customs Agency Service, respectively.

Enclosure:
League document No. O.C./A.R.1941/10, June 19, 1942.

FE:GAM
