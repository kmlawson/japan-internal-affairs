Hong Kong's no. 176, May 10, 1938,
"Domei News Agency Opens Office in Hong
Kong".

On May 1, 1938 the Domei news
agency opened a regular office in Hong
Kong. Mr. Robert Y. Horiguchi, an
American-trained newspaperman, has been
placed in charge. Consul General
Southard is of the opinion that the
agency was established solely to dis-
seminate Japanese propaganda. While
this news agency circularized the
twenty-odd Chinese newspapers in Hong
Kong, not one subscribed to its ser-
vices. Its clients are confined to
the Hong Kong Telegraph, the Hong Kong
Press, and a Japanese afternoon daily.
It is rumored that the Hong Kong Press
is subsidized by the German and Japa-
nese Governments.
