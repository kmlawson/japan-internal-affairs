DEPARTMENT OF STATE
Memorandum of Conversation
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
DATE: November 29, 1940

SUBJECT: Increase in telephone rates at Shanghai.

PARTICIPANTS: Mr. Frank C. Page, International Telephone and Telegraph Corporation
Mr. Mackay

COPIES TO:

Mr. Page, following discussion of another subject (see separate memorandum), said that he had just received information to the effect that the authorities of both the Shanghai Municipality and the French Concession had approved an increase in telephone rates in Shanghai which action, when implemented about the middle of December, would partially offset the losses of the Corporation resulting from the sharp drop in the exchange value of the Chinese dollar.

Mr. Mackay thanked Mr. Page for his information and said that the matter would be brought to the attention of the appropriate officers of the Department.

FE:Mackay:HNS
