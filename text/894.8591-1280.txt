JUL 10 1941
Department of State

Hon. Cordell H. Hull
Department of State
Washington, D.C.

Sir:

We felt ourselves duty bound to convey to you that several
importers from Japan have telephoned us within the past two weeks
to the extent that the shippers in Japan do not propose to have
their vessels come to New York and that the respective shipments
will be discharged only at San Francisco, Seattle or Los Angeles.

We don't know what has motivated this particular condition
but nevertheless feel that there may be something in the air
and that the relative data may be of some value.

Very truly yours,
H.S. DORF &amp; CO INC
President

950, July 8, 10 a. m.
