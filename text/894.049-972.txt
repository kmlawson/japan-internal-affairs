## NOTE

SEE 103.9992/5848 FOR #95

FROM Osaka (Krentz) DATED Oct. 25, 1937
TO NAME 1-1127 GPO

REGARDING:

Credence accorded official acts of Japanese Government Officials; Believes that requests to Japanese officials to swear to correctness of their records before American consular officers may seriously prejudice the standing of the Consulate and the American Government, and in the absence of instructions from the Department, is not inclined to take such affidavits.
