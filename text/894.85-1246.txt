BRITISH EMBASSY,
WASHINGTON, D.C.

February 14th 1941.

Dear Dr. Hornbeck,
AMMUNICATIONS
AND RECORDS

I think you will be interested
to know, with reference to our recent conversation
about the situation in the Far East, that we
received a telegram yesterday from the Foreign
Office saying that the Governor of New Caledonia
had reported to General de Gaulle that he had
positive information, originating from Officers
of Japanese merchant vessels, that all Japanese
ships had received orders to return to Japan
by March 1st.

In sending us this information the
Foreign Office added that they had as yet no
confirmation of the report from other sources.

Yours sincerely
Ursule Butler

Dr. Stanley K. Hornbeck,
United States Department of State,
Washington, D.C.
