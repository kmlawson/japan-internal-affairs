The Railway Department is being severely criticized because of the number of accidents that have occurred recently on Japanese railways. The most serious accident for some years occurred on the Sanyo line, with the death of nine persons and the injury of fourteen. The cause of the crash is believed to have been due to the carelessness of the engineer in driving the train at too great speed around a curve in the narrow-gauge road.

Dr. Egi, Minister of Railways, replying to questions put to him by a member of the Teiyukai in the Diet, declared that in spite of the general

He admitted, however, that the revenue of the Railway Department has been greatly reduced since last year on account of the general business depression. The Railway Department was further criticized for decreasing the estimates for the improvement of the railways. The Railway Minister declared that the money for the improvement of the railway had been used mostly in increasing the capacity for carrying passengers and freight.
