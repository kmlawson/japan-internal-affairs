Sirs:

I have for reply, by reference from the Treasury Department, your letter of November 8, 1937, asking for information in regard to the political status in 1935 of the city of Dairen.

The city of Dairen is located on the Liaotung Peninsula within the area which was leased by China to Russia by a convention concluded in 1898, which area (including some 1300 square miles of territory) is known as the Kwantung Leased Territory. The term of this leasehold was for twenty-five years, and during this period "the entire military command of the land and naval forces and equally of the supreme civil administration will be entirely given over to the Russian authorities" (Article 4). The convention specifically stated, however, that "This act of lease in no way violates the sovereign rights of H. M. the Emperor of China to the above-mentioned territory" (Article 1).

By the Treaty of Portsmouth, concluded between Russia and Japan on September 5, 1905, the Russian Government transferred and assigned to Japan, with the consent of the Government of China, the lease of Port Arthur, Dairen and adjacent territory and territorial waters and all rights, privileges and concessions connected with or forming part of such lease (Article 5). This transfer was confirmed by China in a treaty with Japan, signed on December 22, 1905. On May 25, 1915, an agreement was signed in Article I of which there appears the provision:

"The two High Contracting Parties agree that the term of lease of Port Arthur and Dalny and the terms of the South Manchuria Railway and the Antung-Mukden Railway, shall be extended to 99 years."

For the texts of the various agreements, reference may be made to MACMURRAY'S TREATIES AND AGREEMENTS WITH AND CONCERNING CHINA (Oxford University Press, New York, 1921).
