Tokyo, Iran, July 21, 1949
No. 190
Subject: Request of Imperial Ministry of Foreign Affairs for copy of National Flags of Japan and Korea.

The American Ambassador has the honor to transmit the request of the Imperial Ministry of Foreign Affairs that it be furnished with a copy of the national flags of Japan and Korea.

Inquiry here has disclosed that neither the Office of the Military Attaché nor the USIE Office have available flags or photographs of the flags desired.

UNCLASSIFIED

No. 65

To the
Officer in Charge of the American Mission, Tehran.

The Secretary of State refers to the Embassy's Despatch No. 190 of July 21 requesting a copy of the national flags of Japan and Korea on behalf of the Imperial Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Iran.

The Officer in Charge is informed that the national flag of Japan has not been changed since the War and it is presumed that the Embassy has a picture of the old flag. A sketch of the new national flag of Korea is forwarded herewith for transmittal to the Imperial Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Enclosure:
Sketch of National Flag of Korea

PRIME Minister Regarding National Public Service Law",
and to ARMYTEL to SCAP No. WCL 87563, August 13,
subject: "Revision of the National Public Service Law"

ARMYTEL, as you are undoubtedly aware, requests
SCAP's views and comments relative to above subject,
as well as pertinent information soonest as basis for
expected further discussions in Washington between
State, Labor and Army on issues involved. Department
would like POLAD's independent views and comments on
all aspects of this matter, including the procedures
used by the Cabinet, the text of the Cabinet's Orders
(including statement issued with Ordinance), and all
subsequent official orders and statements relative to
this subject. Reactions by interested Japanese police
and economic parties and organizations to actions that
also a subject of interest to us.

In view of widespread interest in U.S. and above
Dept. would appreciate the prompt despatch of copying
airgrams covering significant future developments relative
to this issue as well.
