JUN 18 1947
DIRECTOR
Department of State

In reply to question regarding present whereabouts and status of General Okamura, director of Chinese Government Information Office at weekly press conference June 18 said that nothing can be added at this time to statement of April 30 to effect that General Okamura has been chief liaison officer in connection with Japanese surrender and repatriation of Japanese troops and that it will take several months to complete this work.

Sent Dept; repeated Tokyo as 35.
