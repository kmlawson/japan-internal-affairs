REGARDING:
Reduction of freight rates.

It is possible that the--, might be made, the purpose being principally, to counteract the declining railway revenues, and to help the people by lessening the cost of production. ## Freight rates:

894.773

In this connection mention of discussion of reduction of freight rates might be made. The aim of the suggested reduction is principally, it would seem, intended to counteract the declining railway revenues, but incidentally to help the people by lessening the cost of production. No concrete plan was drawn up, but two were said to be under consideration: one for a twenty per cent. reduction of all rates, another for a sliding scale of from ten to twenty per cent. on producing materials. (These rates discussed do not apply to Japanese export products, for a reduction in railway freight rates of twenty per cent. for all Japanese goods being exported was effected during the spring. There had formerly been a reduction for certain export products, such as tea, but by this new action reduction was made equally for all products being exported.)
