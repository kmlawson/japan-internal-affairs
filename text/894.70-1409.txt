![img-0.jpeg](img-0.jpeg)

Japanese Government advises that effective November 1st, 1943 Ministry of Transport and Communications was created by combining Ministries of Communications and Railroads, Administration Council of Posts and Telegraphs instituted to administer Posts Telegraph Communications postal money orders and savings accounts services. Telegraph Communications and Posts services under Direction General Director Posts and Telegraphs (Tyumukyoki), His Excellency Mr. Yosiaki Hatta named Minister of Transport and Communications. Mr. Sigeru Komatu named President Administration Council Posts and Telegraphs and Mr. Juniti Nakamura named Director General Posts and Telegraphs.
