DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
November 11, 1932.

MRH:

Victoria's confidential despatch No. 1246 of November 4, 1932, encloses two newspaper clippings from the Victoria DAILY COLONIST, one a presentation of the Japanese viewpoint by the Japanese Consul and the other an announcement that Victoria's Little China is bringing films of the Shanghai fighting. The Consul states that in all such propaganda in Victoria the Chinese seem friendly to the United States and the Japanese unfriendly.
