The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

I have the honor to inquire whether the Department desires a copy of the Résumé Statistique de l'Empire du Japon, 1930 Edition (44e Année), published by the Bureau of Statistics of the Japanese Government.

In past years the annual issues of this publication have been received gratis and two copies have generally been sent to the Department. Due to economies of the Japanese government bureaus, copies are no longer obtainable without charge. The Embassy obtained a copy for its own

=== Page 2 === (confidence 0.99)
-2-

own use by purchase in May and understood at the time that the edition was exhausted, but it has now been learned that further copies are available for purchase and they may possibly still be obtainable when a reply from the Department is received by the Embassy.

This publication contains much the same range of information as is provided in the Japan Year Book and a wider range than the Financial and Economic Annual of Japan. While these other publications offer some interpretation and analysis of the statistics which they contain, it has the advantage of serving as a compact manual covering a wide range of statistics in considerable detail. The price of the 1930 edition is Yen 3.80 per copy.

I have the honor to be, Sir,

Your obedient servant,

Edwin L. Neville
Edwin L. Neville,
Chargé d'Affaires ad interim.
