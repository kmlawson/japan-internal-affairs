12/22/38

Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington, D. C.

Sir:

I have the honor to inform the Department that according to reports published in the newspapers of this capital on December 7, 1938, the Japanese Government, through the medium of its Charge d'Affaires accredited to the Central American Republics, has announced its intention of granting ten scholarships in Japanese universities to two students from each of the five Central American Republics. The reports state that the selection of the students for these scholarships will be made on a competitive basis.

Insofar as Guatemala is concerned, the notice has received scant attention and little comment in the local papers.

Respectfully yours,

Walter H. McKinney
Charge d'Affaires ad interim.
