DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
September 18, 1933.

SJF:

The Consul at Taiwan reports, in his despatch to the Embassy dated August 25th, on the change in the command of the Japanese forces. He quotes a statement made by the new commanding general which indicates that he is in sympathy with the view of the ruling element in the Army. The despatch is not of particular interest.
