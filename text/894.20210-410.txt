|  2 | State Dept. Latin American Division (Wilson) | Dec.11 ME LA |  | DITTO: Conversation between Mr. Wilson and Gov. Schley on Dec.10th. Gov. Schley stated Panama Canal authorities were receiving no reports from War or Navy Depts. He suggested the advisability of a coordinating board in Washington to handle the various reports concerning Japanese activities in this area. In telephone conversation with Gen. Smith he said he intended to discuss this matter with Gen. Drum, and also with Governor Schley, and would send an officer of M.I.D. to Dept. tomorrow.  |
