COMMUNICATIONS AND RECORDS

Stanley K. Hornbeck Esq.,
Department of State,
Washington, D. C.

Dear Mr. Hornbeck:

Since your letter to me last fall very much has happened and is still happening in China. Sec. Hull's fine speech on bombing civilians should have resulted in something different from what is going on in Canton today.

Calling Japan an aggressor, outlaw and treaty breaker, and then giving her the same respect and rights we do to any other nation besides supplying her with gasoline, scrap iron and munitions, shrouds our whole international attitude in a veil of hypocrisy.

Lincoln once said "Stand with anybody that stands right. Stand with him while he is right, and part with him when he goes wrong." Isn't it time for our country to at least take this attitude toward Japan?

Yours truly,

E. I. M. Dickson
