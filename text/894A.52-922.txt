I have the honor to report concerning two measures now being taken by the Taiwan Government General with the avowed object of assisting and supervising Japanese activities, principally economic, in South China, the Philippine Islands, and islands in the South Pacific.

The first of these measures is the proposed formation of a government sponsored and semi-official corporation to be known as the Taiwan Colonization Company. Unfortunately, details concerning this Company are not yet available, but newspaper comment is to the effect that the Company will be capitalized at one hundred million yen. It appears that the Taiwan Government will invest in the Company all unpatented lands in the Island as well as funds as yet to be appropriated. Although it is not known how much money, in addition to the public lands, the Government will invest in the Company, it is nonetheless virtually certain

=== Page 2 === (confidence 1.00)
-2-

certain that the Government will exercise complete control of the Company.

The reported object of the Company is to encourage and assist colonization of its lands in Taiwan by Japanese immigrants and Formosan savage aborigines, and to take over outstanding loans of local commercial banks on Japanese enterprises in South China, the Philippine Islands, and islands of the South Pacific as well as to engage in further activities of that nature.

Director General Hiratsuka is now reported to be drawing concrete plans for the formation of the colonization company, and revising the budget of Taiwan to make provisions for its inauguration. He is supposed to return to Tokyo in the near future with the plans, the revised budget, and a draft of a law to be enacted by the Imperial Diet authorizing the Company.

The other measure is the re-establishment of the Foreign Affairs Section of the Taiwan Government General after a lapse of ten years. Mr. Ryuki Sakamoto, now in the Foreign Office in Tokyo, and lately Japanese Consul General in Amoy, will be the new chief of the Foreign Section.

According to press reports, the Formosan Development Company is being created with the twofold idea of (1) colonizing Taiwan with Japanese immigrants and (2) to further expansion into the "South Seas", a loose geographical designation embracing South China, Indo-China, Siam, the Philippine Islands, Malaya, the Netherlands East Indies, and adjacent islands. ## Law No. 43.

Law of the Formosa Development Company Limited.

**Article 1.** The Formosa Development Co., Ltd., shall be a joint stock company, whose purpose shall be to operate development enterprises and to provide funds for development. Its head office shall be located at Taihoku.

The Formosa Development Co., Ltd., may handle fixed deposits of money in accordance with orders.

**Article 2.** The capital of the Formosa Development Co., Ltd., shall be Yen 30,000,000, but it may be increased by permission of the Government.

Article 4. The Government may contribute as capital any of its property, other than money, that is under the control of the Government General of Formosa.

In the cases specified in the foregoing paragraph, the registration tax in respect of the acquisition by the Formosa Development Co., Ltd., of real property from the Government shall be 3/1000 of the value of the said property, payable at the time that the registration is received.

Article 5. The Formosa Development Co., Ltd., may increase its capital even before the total amount of the shares has been paid up.

Article 6. The Formosa Development Co., Ltd., shall have one President, one Vice President, not less than three directors and not less than two auditors, whose duties, powers and terms of office shall be fixed by Imperial Ordinance.

The President, Vice President and Directors shall be appointed by the Governor General of Formosa, by permission of the competent Minister of State, in accordance with the Imperial Ordinance.
