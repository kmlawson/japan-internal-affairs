LABOR DISPUTES IN JAPAN.
According to the Social Bureau there were 2456
cases of labor disputes during the year lo31, which
exceeded the previous record figure of 2289 cases
which occurred in 1930, and doubled the figure for
1926.
The number of workers involved, nowever, was
154,528, or a decrease of 37,277 compared with the
number for 1930.
The number of disputes resulting in general strikes,
sabotage and lockout totalled 993, involving 64,536
workers. Compared with 1930, this represented an
increase of 92 in number of d isputes, and an increase
of more than 6o0 workers resorting to drastic action.
Regarding duration of disputes, the Social Bureau
statistics show that 148 continued for over a month,
which was an increase of 40 compared with 1930.
As to the demands of workers causing the disputes,
it is noted that most of them were defensive in char-
acter, reflecting the intense financial depression
prevalent throughout 1931.
