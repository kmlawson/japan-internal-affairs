V 16.6. GOVERNMENT PRINTING OFFICE 1-207

|  SUB No. | FROM— | 1944 | TO — | SUB No. | PURPOSE  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  1 | Japan Tel. #10-11am. (Grew) | Jan. 20 | FE |  | Ban on repairs to foreign steamships for more than 30 days in advance: Consul at Kobe reports that from local P. and O. Steamship Company it has been learned that Japanese dockyards have been instructed by naval authorities to accept no orders for repairs from foreign steamships companies more than 30 days in advance. This is resumably, in order to leave docks free for emergencies. Note: 894.30  |
|  2 | Japan Tel 260 Grew | Apr. 18 |  | NOTE | WEST CUSSETA has lost rudder and is being towed to Yokohama; F.O. states that dry dock facilities are fully occupied but authorities will endeavor to vouch temporary release of dry dock when precise information concerning necessary repairs is available. SEE 195.7 WEST CUSSETA  |
