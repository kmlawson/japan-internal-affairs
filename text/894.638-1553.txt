Tokyo, December 18, 1946

SUBJECT: Limestone, Lime and Gypsum Resources in Japan

The United States Political Adviser has the honor to
enclose four copies of Report No. 60, dated November 15,
1946, entitled "Limestone, Lime, and Gypsum Resources
in Japan," which was prepared by the Natural Resources
Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the
Allied Powers. The report is prefaced by an adequate
summary.

Enclosures:
