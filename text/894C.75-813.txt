May 26, 1930.

To the American Consul,
Bairam, Manchuria.

The Secretary of State refers to the Department's instruction of July 23, 1929 (File No. 893.75/56) requesting statistical information regarding government and private telephone systems in the Huanhung Leased Territory and, at the request of the American Telephone and Telegraph Company, 195 Broadway, New York City, in a letter to the Department dated April 21, 1930, enclosed forms to be used in bringing the data procured last year up to January 1, 1930.

There is also enclosed, for the files of the Consulate, a copy of the Company's publication entitled TELEPHONE AND TELEGRAPH STATISTICS OF THE WORLD, as of January 1, 1929.

The desired statistics should be submitted in triplicate under a covering despatch, which should cite the date and file number of this instruction and the date of the Company's letter given above.
