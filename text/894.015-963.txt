# DOCUMENT FILE

## NOTE

SEE 793.94/9091 FOR Tel#337, 6pm

FROM China (Lockhart) DATED July 30, 1937
TO NAME 1-1127 6P6

REGARDING: Sino-Japanese crisis in North China resulting from incident of July 7, 1937.

Chinese have been ordered to make and fly Japanese flags; many such flags are flying from Peiping to Yenching, according to Leighton Stuart.

FRG.
