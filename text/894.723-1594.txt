Dear Mr. deWolf:

This refers to your letter addressed to Mr. Norfleet dated February 21, 1947 which concerns a suggestion by SCAP that the rates for telegraphic messages between Japan and the United States be changed. As pointed out in the Commission's letter of March 17, 1947, action was deferred on SCAP's request pending determination of the issues involved in the Commission's investigation in the matter of charges for communications service between the United States and overseas and foreign points.

Pursuant to the Commission's decision in this case, the several United States telegraph carriers have filed increased rates along the lines of the "Bermuda" pattern from all parts of the United States to all parts of Japan of 30 cents per full rate word and 20 cents per CDE word. In view of this decision, SCAP may consider it desirable to discuss with the United States telegraph carriers the application of the "Bermuda" pattern of rates and settlements in both directions between the United States and Japan.

Very truly yours,

T. J. Stowie,
Secretary
