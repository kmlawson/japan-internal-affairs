=== Page 2 === (confidence 0.99)
(7) Hungary.

On November 28 it was reported in the press that a Japan-Hungarian cultural pact was to be concluded within a short time and that the Japan-Hungarian Cultural Society in Japan, under the Chairmanship of Baron Takaharu Mitsui, had announced that it will institute the Japan-Cultural Prize in commemoration of the event. This award is to be given annually, beginning next year, and is to take the form of medals and monetary rewards to Hungarians "who render service in the study and introduction of Japan to Hungary". This article further stated that Baron Mitsui was carrying out a five-year program, which is to end next year, in which he has been donating Yen 30,000 each year for the purpose of promoting Japanese-Hungarian relations. Baron Mitsui was reported to have said "the pro-Japanese sentiment prevailing among Hungarians is almost enthusiastic. I think we should have countries in Central Europe surrounding Soviet Russia understand the true cultural aspect of Japan and of the Japanese spirit..."

DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
August 27, 1934.

Referring to the statement in the attached letter of August 6 from Mr. Herbert S. Houston that a Pan-American Library was established in New York last January, Mr. McGurk of LA tells me that they know of no such library but that it is possible that the Pan-American Society of New York has set up such a library. Mr. McGurk said that any such library would be private and unofficial in character.

M.M.H.
