My dear Mr. Castle:
394.91
I have your letter of November 28 and I keenly regret that I am
unable to do anything further to aid Mr. B. W. Fleisher in the reestablishment
of the Japan Advertiser. I appreciate the value of the publication to the
relations of the two countries and I have great sympathy for Mr. Fleisher and
the others who must suffer, indeed the whole matter is distressing to me.
Because of your friendly interest I think you should know the fact
about the note. Mr. Lamont was never a backer of Mr. Fleisher or of his enter-
prise, and so far as I know essrs. Rockefeller and Crane were not. Mr. Lamont
Ci
had known Mr. Fleisher in a friendly way and after the property was destroyed
6
by the earthquake and fire joined in guaranteeing the loan under discussion, in
order to assist Mr. Fleisher in rehabilit ing the plant and paper. That was
his sole interest and it was entirely an act of friendship.
It was a bank loan and as you know banking capital is not loaned
for long periods. It was the understanding that the loan would be reduced and
finally paid off or absorbed by permanent financing. The loan has run well
into its seventh year, none of the principal of it has been paid and the interest
is at present in arrears. I am confidentially informed that there have been
repeated examinations of the loan by the bank examiners who have marked it as
a slow.
Added to this unsatisfactory picture is the fact that Mr. Fleisher's
management of the property has been extravagant. I repeatedly discussed that
subject with him but apparently he could not see it or was unwilling to economize.
The second crisis came to him in the midst of the worst condition that New York
has experienced in many years. So between the story of the loan and the manage-
ment of the property and the conditions prevailing it was not possible to do
anything for him.
Please treat this information as confidential as I do not want to
retard the efforts Mir. Fleisher is making to reorganize the property.
Thanking you for your letter, I am,
DEC5
Sincerely yours,
