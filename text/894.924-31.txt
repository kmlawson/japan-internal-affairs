December 23, 1943

FE

My dear Mr. Walker:

The book entitled "Geology of the Enviroms of Tokyo"
which you were so kind as to send to the Secretary of State
has been referred to this Division for acknowledgment.

The spirit which prompted you to make this useful
contribution to the Department's reference materials is
greatly appreciated.

Sincerely yours,
For the Secretary of State:

LEE
Lawrence E. Salisbury
Assistant Chief
Division of Far Eastern Affairs

The Reverend
E. Walker,
Rou Iemond, California.
