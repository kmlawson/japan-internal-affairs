My dear Mr. Draper:

Thank you for your letter of April 17, 1947, outlining your plans to reestablish bunkering service in Japan.

As you probably know, final and formal approval from the Army-Navy Petroleum Board is required before the plan becomes operable. When that approval is given, you may instruct your employee in Japan to make his investigations as to the status of your bunkering services in Tsurumi, with the knowledge and acquiescence of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

Sincerely yours,

Edwin M. Martin,
Chief
Division of Japanese and Korean
Economic Affairs

Mr. T. J. Draper,
Standard-Vacuum Oil Company,
26 Broadway,
New York 4, New York.
