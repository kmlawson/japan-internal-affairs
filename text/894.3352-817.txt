SIR:

I have the honor to inform the Department of the visit to Barcelona of the Japanese training cruisers "IVATE" and "ASAKA", from May 14th to the morning of the 18th last.

The Spanish battleship "JAIME I" was ordered to Barcelona for the period of the visit, and was used as headquarters by the Spanish Minister of Marine who apparently came to Barcelona for the special purpose of officially receiving the visitors.

The Japanese Minister in Madrid was also present. The usual official ceremonies, including banquets, were held; and in addition the ships brought, and presented to the City of Barcelona, cherry trees which were formally planted on Calle de Tokio under the auspices of the Japanese Commander and local authorities. The same day, as part of the festivities, a detachment of Japanese marines also paraded on the principal Avenue of Barcelona in union with a detachment of troops from the local garrison.

These unusual features of a visit of foreign war vessels were announced to be due, respectively, to Japanese appreciation
