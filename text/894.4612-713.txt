Madam:

In reply to your letter inquiring as to the type
of dress worn by American ladies when they are presented
at the Japanese Court, the Department understands that,
if the presentation is at a private audience, foreign
ladies, including American ladies, wear the usual after-
noon dress, avoiding black dress however. If the pre-
sentation is at a formal reception, such as the New Years
Reception, foreign ladies, including American ladies,
wear evening dress with a train failing from the shoulders.
The proper length of this train up to about two years ago
was four yards. Presumably there has been little if any
change since then.

Very truly yours,
For the Secretary of State:
Richard Southgate
Assistant Chief,
Division of International
Conferences and Protocol.
