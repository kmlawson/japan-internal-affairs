Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

TO: JA - Mr. Lory
FROM: FC - Mr. Amshey
SUBJECT: Nazi Party Membership Records, Japan

DATE: July 19, 1946
DIVISION OF JAPANESE AFFAIRS
JUL 22 1946
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

I am enclosing for your information a mimeographed copy of "Nazi Party Membership Records, Japan". This copy was prepared by a representative of the Department in Berlin from original NSDAP card indices captured in Germany.

The list of names in the enclosure is to the best of our knowledge an accurate record of Nazi Party members in Japan. It is understood that the list of names is not complete and we have been assured that supplemental lists will be forwarded when the cards have been properly sorted. As soon as additional lists are received they will be promptly transmitted to you.

Two copies of the enclosure were sent to Tokyo on July 13, 1946, under cover of DS-4 Forms.

Enclosure:
As stated.

This Document Must Be Returned To
