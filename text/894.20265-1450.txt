CONFIDENTIAL
22 September 1944

TRANSLATION

SUBJECT: ROMA NIPPON KURABU, Rome, Italy -- Activities of.

(The following is a literal English translation of a sheet of ROMA NIPPON KURABU stationary, forwarded to ONI by ComEighthFleet, N.I.U. in September, 1944. It is interesting in that it shows the close relationship between Japanese Associations (NIPPON JIN KAI) and Japanese diplomatic and Consular authorities.)

"Nippon Kurabu
Casa del Giappone
Roma - Via Antonio Bosio, 26
Telef. 862413

3 May 1943

(To) Japanese Embassy in Rome

(From) Nippon Club

Greetings: We cordially enquire after your health, etc.

Passport Check of Resident Japanese

With your permission we are forwarding herewith an investigative report (on the above subject) among the civilians (original and two copies each), together with a list. Please handle them in any way you see fit."

CONFIDENTIAL
