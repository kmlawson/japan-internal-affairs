Hon. Fletcher Warren
Executive Assistant to Assistant Secretary,
Department of State,
Washington, D. C.

My dear Mr. Warren:

I enclose a letter that I received from my good friend, William C. Gloeckner, a Florist at Albany, New York, concerning the importation of Japanese lily bulbs.

Mr. Gloeckner informs me that these lily bulbs are already harvested and packed for shipment to this country, and unless they are allowed to enter it will mean that the florists will have no lilies to sell during the next Easter period.

I would very much like to be of every assistance to Mr. Gloeckner and his fellow florists, and I shall be thankful if you can inform me as to whether or not any steps can be taken that will permit the entrance into the United States of those bulbs that are now ready for shipment from Japan.

I am

Sincerely yours,

W. T. Bayne
