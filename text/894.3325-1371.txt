# CROSS-REFERENCE FILE

## NOTE

### SUBJECT

The Japanese Secret Service Agent, Sozan Miyoshi, is reported to have told Chilean officials he could arrange to have four or five Japanese warships visit Chile if fuel oil could be supplied for the return trip.

Informs that - probably to keep Chile neutral.

For the original paper from which reference is taken

See Memo.
