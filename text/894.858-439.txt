Far Eastern Division (Dooman) Japanese Embassy | Apr. 13 12:10. |  | Murder committed on the Japanese Steamship RAGON MARU while on the high seas: Conv. ration between Mr. Dooman and Mr. Shun-ichiro Kawahara, Third Secretary, Japanese Embassy, in regard to Mr. Kawahara asked whether Dept. of Justice had sent instructions to U.S. District Atty. at Honolulu. Mr. Dooman talked with Mr. Ramsay of D. of J., who stated there appears to be no violation of any Federal law; violation of territorial laws of Hawaii should be determined by territorial authorities; and that Master of the vessel could take custody of the person accused.
