Washington.

107, April 7, 9 a.m.
SECTION G - CEMENT.

Commercial Attache reports that the cement industry in Japan has a capital investment of some 200,000,000 yen. Production during the past two years has been some 3,700,000 tons per year; exports were 450,000 tons and imports 10,000 tons. Present conditions are said to be unsatisfactory but the industry is supposed to be in good condition.

NEVILLE
