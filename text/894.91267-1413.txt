FROM Dated November 17, 1942
Rec'd 3:43 a.m., 18th

Secretary of State,
Washington.

1167, November 7, 3 p.m.

Director General of press informed member of Embassy last night that he had expelled Istanbul correspondent of NICHI NICHI to Bulgaria on ten hours notice. When asked reason he stated facetiously that expelled Japanese correspondent had tried to surpass in sensationalism most sensational of American correspondents.

KELLEY

FROM: American Consulate General, Istanbul, Turkey.
TO: Secretary of State, Washington.
DATE: November 19, 1942, 4 p.m.
NO.: 313.

Eti Moto, Japanese correspondent of the Niche Niche Shim Bun, was very recently expelled from Turkey for allegedly taking photographs in military zones. Secretary of State,

148, January 29, 7 p.m.

The Turkish Press Bureau informs me that it is expelling the Japanese Domci agency correspondent in Istanbul named Oda for abuse of journalistic ethics and misuse of facilities granted him by the Turkish Government.
