Tokyo, November 12, 1932.

No. 191.

Subject: Address presented by Japanese Boy Scouts.

The Honorable
The Secretary of State,
Washington.

Sir:

On Armistice Day the Embassy was visited by a band of fourteen young boy scouts, representing the boy scouts of Tokyo. They presented a short address, of which a copy is enclosed at their request.

The incident is reported not because of its importance, but as an indication that the youth of Japan are not completely

Respectfully yours,

Although we were not old enough to participate in the first joys of Armistice Day in 1918, we, the Seinan Boy Scouts, would like to share in the preservation of the peace which was brought to the world at that time. In the interests of good fellowship we are remembering Armistice Day this year with our brothers of all countries and of many different races both here in Japan and throughout the world. We should like to offer our sincere good-will to the youth of your country through you.

Kiyota Hashimoto,
105 Rokucho-me, Toyama,
Tokyo.
