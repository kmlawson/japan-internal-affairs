Respectfully yours,

Glen Bruner

American Consulate, Taihoku, Taiwan, Japan,
August 10, 1941

Robert B. Orr, Esquire,
Honorary Secretary and Treasurer,
North Formosa Peace Memorial Association,
Taihoku.

Dear Mr. Orr:

The receipt is acknowledged of your communication of even date advising that the North Formosa Peace Memorial Association had, at a meeting held last year, placed the proceeds from the sale of its Taihoku property in the hands of a Board of Trustees, and that the resident American Consul was named as one of five members of that Board. You indicate that an Annual Meeting of the Board of Trustees is to be held annually in August, and suggest that you hope that there is nothing to prevent my taking a seat on the Board to which you kindly welcome me.

I have to thank you for your welcome, and to express my willingness to serve on the Board in any non-executive capacity. I wish, however, to abide by the time-honored unwritten rule of the American Foreign Service, namely: that American consular officers do not accept executive or quasi-executive positions in organizations without the Service, but that they are free to accept such advisory positions as may be open to them because of their official status.

Very truly yours,

Glen Bruner

A true copy of the signed original. (J.K.B.)

Enclosure No. 3 to Despatch, dated August 29, 1941, from
Glen Bruner, American Vice Consul, at Taihoku, Taiwan,
Japan, to the Embassy, Tokyo, on subject of North Formosa
Peace Memorial Association.

Minutes of a Meeting of the Board of Trustees of
the North Formosa Peace Memorial Association held at
The Twatutia Foreign Club at 6.30 p.m. on Tuesday
26th. August 1941

Members present:- The Resident Acting British Consul,
Mr. W. W. McVittie; The Resident American Vice Consul,
Mr. Glen Bruner; Certer, Macy Co., Inc. representative:
Mr. R. B. Orr; Tait &amp; Co., Ltd. representative: Mr. F.
H. Berger.

The question before the Meeting was who was to receive
the money in the event of no Foreign Representative
of the Board of Trustees being resident in Formosa.

Mr. McVittie proposed and Mr. Glen Bruner seconded the
motion that

The U.S.A. and British Embassies at Tokyo in
consultation with the Head Office of Trustee
Firms should decide how to dispose of the Fund
in the event of their being no Foreigner in
Taihoku able to act as Trustee of this Fund.

Carried unanimously

(Signed) R. B. ORR
R. B. Orr
Hon Secretary and Treasurer
