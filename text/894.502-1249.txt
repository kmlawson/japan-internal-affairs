MAY 15 1941
PS/HH

Found a very quiet scene here but living conditions, generally speaking, have not deteriorated much in the year that I was absent. Certain items of food are lacking in the shops but they concern the foreigner chiefly and most of us foreigners have stores of our own. Frequently there's difficulty getting bread and potatoes and often there's a shortage of lamb and game. Nevertheless one can always make out pretty well. Mild grumbling is heard from those who spend time in the "bread line" but it's of no significance--just the normal reaction. The populace is amazingly well garbed and shod when considering that rigid restrictions have been enforced on wool, cotton, leather, rubber etc for roughly three years.
