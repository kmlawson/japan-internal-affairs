Despatch from Dairen, November 1, 1934:
This despatch, which was sent directly
to the Embassy at Tokyo, contains interest-
ing information on the trend of Chinese
immigration to and emigration from Manchuria.
The number of Chinese immigrating to
Manchuria compared with those leaving
apparently has greatly increased. The
number of incoming Chinese during the first
nine months of this year practically doubled
the number coming in 1933 during the same
period, whereas the number outgoing increasec
only about 13%.
The excess of incoming over outgoing
Chinese amounted to 170,140 persons in
the January to September period, 1934, as
compared with only 17,792 for the same
period of 1933.
COMMENT:
It is obvious from the state of
immigration and emigration to and from
Manchuria that the word has gone around
through nearby provinces, probably Shantung
in particular, that Manchuria again offers
advantages and attractions to Chinese
laborers and peasants.
