DEPARTMENT OF STATE
DIVISION OF FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
December 5, 1932.

The sinking of the Japanese destroyer SAWARABI.

In a conversation over the telephone this afternoon Captain Ogan of the Office of Naval Intelligence stated that, according to information received yesterday from the Japanese Naval Attache, the Japanese destroyer SAWARABI had sunk while at the north end of Formosa on its way with two other destroyers to the Pescadores. Apparently high seas were the cause of the disaster, which resulted in the loss of approximately 90 lives. Captain Ogan stated that the Secretary of the Navy had despatched a telegram of condolence to the Japanese Navy Minister and that the Navy Department here had expressed its condolences to the Japanese Embassy.

Mr. Cooke of the Division of Protocol, when questioned by an officer of this Division as to whether any further action should be taken in this regard, stated that in his opinion nothing more should be done, as anything further would be beyond the usual procedure in such a case.
