SUBJECT: Tour of Japan by German Pianist Sponsored by Government.
Division of FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
JUL 24 1936
Department of State

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to inform the Department that Professor Wilhelm Kempff, a prominent German pianist, was recently in Singapore en route to Germany after giving a series of concerts in Japan. While in Singapore he gave a concert at the home of the German Consul General at which the Japanese Consul General and I were present among an attendance of about one hundred and fifty. Professor Kempff in newspaper interviews spoke enthusiastically of the Japanese as great music lovers.

I should consider the above as inconsequential were I not informed by the Japanese Consul General at Singapore in a conversation commenting on the concert that Professor Kempff was sent to Japan by the German Government, presumably at the expense of the
