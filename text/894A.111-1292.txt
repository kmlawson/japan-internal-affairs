Taihoku, Taiwan, Japan, August 27, 1941
1941 OCT 28 AM 11 37

The Honorable
Joseph C. Grew, American Ambassador,
Tokyo, Japan.

Sir:

I have the honor to refer to my despatch of August 22, 1941 (File No. 811.11 GB:tkh) to the Embassy with regard to the revision, on August 21, 1941, of the Ordinance of the Government General of Taiwan under which that Government General exercises control of the entry, sojourn, and departure of aliens.

There was addressed to the American Consul and received yesterday through the local post a carbon copy of a typed Japanese-language communication in which I am informed that the provisions of the new regulations are not be applicable to the staff members of foreign consulates, their families, and employees. As of possible interest, there is transmitted herewith a copy of a translation of the communication received, together with a copy of my reply of even date.

Respectfully yours,

Glen Bruner
American Vice Consul

Translation

1939 Nov. 1, 254

Taiwan Government General,
August 25, 1941

The American Consul.

Regarding Revision of Government
General Ordinance with Regard to
the Entry, Sojourn, and Departure
of Aliens

Sir:

In as much as this Government General has, as
of August 21, revised Government General Ordinance
No. 50 of 1939 with regard to the entry, sojourn,
and Departure of aliens by promulgating Government
General Ordinance No. 153 as published in the Official
Gazette, which became effective as of that date,
I have to request you please to understand that the
details thereof are as set forth therein.

I have further to advise that, as for foreign
consular staff members, their families, and employees,
the provisions of this Ordinance are, as heretofore,
not to be applicable.

Respectfully yours,

(SEAL) Teruo HACHIYA
Chief of the Department of Foreign
Affairs

AMERICAN CONSULATE,
Taiho, Taiwan, Japan, August, 27, 1941.

His Excellency
Peru. Pachy.,
Chief, Department of Foreign Affairs,
The Government General of Taiwan,
Taiho, Taiwan.

Excellency:

I have the honor to acknowledge the receipt of your Excellency's communication of August 25, 1941 (Gai Ryo No. 1,764) in which I am informed with regard to the revision of the Government General Ordinance pertaining to the control of the entry, sojourn, and departure of aliens, and am advised that, in accordance with precedent, the provisions of the revised ordinance are not to be applied to consular staff members, their families, and employees. Please accept my sincere thanks for this kind communication.

I avail myself of this opportunity to renew the assurances of my highest consideration and esteem.

I have the honor to be, Excellency,
Your obedient servant,

Glen Bruner
American Vice Consul
