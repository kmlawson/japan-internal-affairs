The Alien Land Law of Japan (Law No. 42 of 1925), which went into force on November 10, 1926, provides that aliens may own land in Japan on a footing of equality with Japanese subjects, but with a qualification that the acquirement of land by nationals of a country (or subdivision thereof) which prohibits or restricts land ownership by Japanese nationals may be prohibited or restricted by Imperial Ordinance. At the time when the law was put into effect the Japanese Government announced that it did not intend for the time being to apply this qualifying provision of the law, and, so far as is known, no such prohibitions or restrictions have been as yet imposed by Imperial Ordinance.
