Prior to Japan's recent conquests, Japan's aluminum
industry was largely dependent on imported materials. Although
efforts were made to develop the small bauxite deposits in the

It is widely believed that one of the principal limita-
tions on Japanese produotion of aircraft and of armaments gener-
ally'consists in the smallness and mediocre quality of her machine
tool production. This belief is based on the general impressions
of observers rather than on readily verifiable data, and there is
a possibility that it has been exaggerated. Nevertheless. limita-
tions of machine tool capacity and of skilled labor are probably
more important than raw material shortages in restraining the rate
of expansion of Japanese plane produotion, but these limitations
probably are not sufficiently severe to halt further expansion of
