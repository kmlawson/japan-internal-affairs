Formosa: Memorandum in two sections<br/>
The first section constitutes primarily a historical sketch. The second section contains comment in regard to Japanese administration practice in Formosa and character and attitudes of the people of Formosa. Dated 894A.01, 711.94A, 740.0011 P.W., 740.00119 P.W. Chronological outline in regard to Formosa prepared by Mr. Kerr of MIS. Attaches -.

3a Ascertain current usage in English the name Taiwan or Formosa in Chinese official circles. Formosa is English name preferred by the Chinese.

Memo: THE SOVEREIGNITY OF FORMOSA.
