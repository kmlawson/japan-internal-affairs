Notes contained in book found on prisoner indicate that Japanese gas grenade is effective for 30 seconds after detention. These grenades consist of a glass flask one pint capacity filled with liquid HCl. Apparently they are designed for use on enclosed spaces such as tanks. If leakage occurs they are easily ignited. Specimens captured in the test were packed in sewer in a tin canister.

For information to Chemical Service a price that is being
continued. It is received test conducted by the British on entire
time was set before by breaking a joint bottle of HCl by a detonator.
The sediment in the grenade container is to be phosphoric
acid by the British who believe it is used as a stabilizer. The
sear test, however, think it probably phosphorous which acts as
incendiary agent.
