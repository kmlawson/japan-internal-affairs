DEPARTMENT OF STATE
MAY 27 1943
May 12, 1943.
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

I do not see why, after stating as we do in the second paragraph of this letter that there is no treaty in force between the United States and Japan concerning the ownership of property, we proceed to a discussion in the third paragraph of the treaty that has been terminated and enclose a copy of it, particularly in view of the fact that the treaty even when in force did not give Japanese subjects the right to own land. Certainly the State Land Board of Oregon is not interested in the details with respect to the termination of the 1911 treaty and they could hardly be interested in having a copy of it, especially when we have told them that there is no treaty in force. Personally, I should prefer also to omit the fourth paragraph. I see no reason why the Department should state what it ordinarily says in reply to inquiries regarding the rights of aliens with respect to real property. These people are interested in knowing what our views are concerning their proposed action in this case. Those views are stated in the fifth, sixth and seventh paragraphs.

Green H. Hackworth

DEPARTMENT OF STATE
TREATY DIVISION

May 5, 1943.

Mr. Barnes:

SD considers that we should avoid stating that the Department is not in a position to inform Mr. Griffith in regard to specific Japanese laws governing rights of aliens to own real property in Japan.

You will recall that in drafting the reply to Mr. Griffith there was some question as to whether any mention need be made of Japanese laws, since it appears that such laws could not affect, as a legal proposition, the Oregon law under consideration with respect to escheat.

However, we may, in order to inform Mr. Griffith as fully as possible, delete the penultimate paragraph of the letter, as previously drafted, change the period after "modified" at the end of the next preceding paragraph to a comma, and add at the end and as a part of that paragraph something along the lines of the underlying draft.

W. V. W.

# DEPARTMENT OF STATE
## SPECIAL DIVISION

April 28, 1943.

[Stamp: RECORD DIVISION MAY 1943 DEPARTMENT OF STATE
EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT TO ASSISTANT SECRETARY JAN 29 1943
MR. BRANDT]

[Handwritten: A - L]

SD feels that it is unfortunate that the Department should have to make the admission in the penultimate paragraph of the underlying letter that the Department has no information regarding Japanese laws governing the rights of aliens to own real property in Japan. If this information is not now available in the Department, SD would be pleased to request it through the Swiss Government in charge of American interests in Japan.

Please note Mr. Smith's underlying memorandum.

[Stamp: TREATY DIVISION MAY 5 - 1943 DEPARTMENT OF STATE
EDwin A. Plitt
