AMERICAN CONSULATE,
CONFIDENTIAL
Amey, China, May 17, 1932.

Subject: Proposed Flight to Taiwan.

The Honorable
Nelson Trusler Johnson,
American Minister,
Peiping, China.

Sir:

I have the honor to refer to the Legation's instructions dated February 15, 1932, regarding the proposed flight of Messrs. Stevens and Halliburton over Taiwan, and to enclose a copy of a map which the Japanese Consul of Amey gave me for the use of the above persons. He informs me that this map is of the military drill ground which has been prepared as a temporary landing field on the island of Taiwan especially for these fliers.

A few days ago, the Japanese Consul told me that he had received information to the effect that these persons had advised that this proposed flight would not take place.

A copy of this map was given by me to the Commander of the U.S.S. STEWART, who I understand forwarded it to the Commander-in-Chief of the U.S. Asiatic Fleet.

Respectfully yours,

Lynn W. Franklin,
American Consul.

Notice No. 131.

A notice is hereby given that the following aviation ground will be established on September 25, 1935:

September 24, 1935
Kenzo Nakagawa,
Governor General of Taiwan.

1. Purpose of establishment: For public use.
2. Name, title and address of operator: State.
3. Name, title, and address of owner of the ground: State.
4. Name and location of the aviation ground:
Taihoku Aviation Ground at Matsuyama-sho,
Shichisei-gun, Taihoku-shu. 121° 3"
Longitude East; 25° 4" Latitude North.
5. Kind of aviation ground (whether land, sea, or both): Land Aviation Ground.
6. Area and topography: Total area 469,421 square meters.
Landing field: Approximately 900 meters east to west; approximately 700 meters south to north.
The surface of the field is level.
7. Usual direction of wind: Generally easterly.
8. Outline of equipment: Signal post.
(Official Gazette of the Taiwan Government General dated September 24, 1935)

NOTE:
The above aviation field is located in the northeastern part of Taihoku about three miles from the Taihoku Railway Station.

SUBJECT: New Aviation Field at Giran, Taiwan.

1-1055 G P O

THE HONORABLE
TIME SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON

SIR:

I have the honor to enclose herewith a translation of a Notice from the Official Gazette of the Taiwan Government General, dated July 14, 1936, covering the inauguration of a public aviation field at Giran, located on the northeast coast of Taiwan.

Since Giran is not a port of entry for foreign trade, aeroplanes coming directly from abroad would not be permitted to use this field.

Notice No. 111.

A notice is hereby given that the following aviation ground was established as of July 13, 1936.

KENZO NAKAGAWA
Governor General of Taiwan.

July 14, 1936.

1. Purpose of establishment: For public use.

2. Name or title and address of the person operating the ground: Giran-gai (Town of Giran).

3. Name or title and address of the owner of the ground: State; Town of Giran; Giran Irrigation Association, Giran-gai, Giran-gun, Taihoku-shu; Taihoku Provincial Agricultural Association care of the Taihoku Provincial Government.

4. Name and location of the aviation ground: Giran Hikojo (Giran Aviation Ground), Kin Rokketsu, Giran-gai, Giran-gun, Taihoku-shu.

5. Kind of the ground (whether for land or sea-planes or for both): Land Aviation Ground.

6. Area and topography:

Total area: 74,672.3538 tsubos

Runway area: 800 meters east to west
800 meters south to north in "T" character type.

The surface of the runway area is flat, and on the surface of the ground other than the runway grass grow thickly.

7. Direction of prevailing wind: North-easterly.

8. Outline of facilities: Signal post and a sign of the Aviation Ground.

9. Period of the establishment: From July 1, 1936 to June 30, 1946.

(Taiwan Government General Official Gazette No. 2,734, dated July 14, 1936)
