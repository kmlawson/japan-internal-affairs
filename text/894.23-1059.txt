December 5, 2 p.m.
The following telegram has been sent to the Legation:
"December 5, 3 p.m. This morning at nine o'clock
the Japanese sent an armored car and a machine gun,
with fifteen or twenty men, to Nankai University which
is much over a mile from the Japanese concession and an
area heretofore entirely free of any disturbance. The
above named detachment stayed in vicinity (?) one hotel
and then returned to concession. The reason for the
move is not known but it shows a complete lack of con-
ciliation, or at least of appreciation of the present
strained relations. Repeated to the Department and
Nanking".
