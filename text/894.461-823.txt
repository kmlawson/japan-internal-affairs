Mr. Grew reports in his despatch
No. 826 of June 11, 1934, that he was
asked by Mr  Block, a journalist who writes
under the name of Bruno Lessing, to re-
quest that Mr. Block be received in

Foreign Office, on the ground that Mr.
Block is not of outstanding prominence
in the field of journalism.
The position taken by Mr. Grew is
entirely correct. He did not describe
the regulations of the Imperial Household
covering audiences, but these regulations
provide that foreign representatives
should apply for audiences in behalf of
only those of their nationals who are
high officials in their own government,
or who have high social position, or
who have world wide reputation in certain
