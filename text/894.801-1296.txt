DIVISION OF
FAR EASTERN AFFAIRS
AUG 28 1941
Department of State

Columnist Emuto in article tonight's HOCHI states Japan should close Tsugaru Soya straits especially as United States has closed Panama Canal to Japanese ships. Japanese should defend their territories and waters to the death and the passage through them of ships belonging to nations hostile to Japanese should never be permitted. The Japanese people should be awake that munitions airplanes and hightest gasoline are being shipped in quantities to Vladivostok. Bokkado and Karafuto are becoming increasingly important to Japanese national defense. Sent Department via Shanghai.

GREW

DJD
