Sir:

I have the honor to report the following political developments in Taiwan since the last report submitted on the 9th of this month. Most of these are connected with the China Incident.

Inspection trip of Prince Higashi Kuni

Prince Higashi Kuni, Commander in Chief of the Imperial Air Forces having completed his ten day inspection trip in Taiwan, took off from Matsuyama Airport, Taihoku on April 11 for Japan Proper. Two days after he had departed notice of his visit appeared for the first time in the press, and numerous articles appeared for days thereafter with accompanying photographs of the Prince arriving at the Governor General's residence, visiting the military hospital in Taihoku, proceeding through the streets, etc.

Visit of part of Fleet

Conference of Local Governors

From [A]pril 21st for three days the governors of the seven provinces of Taiwan met in conference in Taihoku, the group, including Governor Utsumi of Takao [P]rovince who was badly burned in the motorboat accident of [A]pril 12 when inspecting the heavy industry zone at Soga, Takao harbor. This is the second gubernatorial conference since Governor General Kobayashi's assumption of office on September 2, 1936, and was attended by the Governor General, Director General Morioka, all eight (including Pescadores) local governors, all seven bureau chiefs, and other interested officials. The main purpose of the conference was to consider measures to cope with the current situation on the basis of the special position and requirements of Taiwan. No Patriotic Bonds Yet sold in Pescadores

On April 20th an article appeared in the Taiwan Nichi Nichi Newspaper with front page headlines "FORMOSAN. [FORMOSANS?] TIGHTFISTED, THOUGH THEY ALL HAVE PLENTY OF MONEY, NOT ONE HAS BOUGHT PATRIOTIC BONDS". The short article following with a Mako dateline stated that although Patriotic Bonds are selling elsewhere like hotcakes, being bought by those supporting the men at the front, one finds in looking over the record that not a Formosan in the Pescadores has yet bought a bond. Since it is understood that the Formosans there are fairly prosperous the reason why is wondered at. "Can it be", asks a certain quarter (the Army) making a wry face, "that the officials and others have been so absorbed in the assimilation movement that they have forgotten about selling bonds?"

Departure of the Farm Volunteer Corps

On April 25, after a last word of advice from the Governor General including "be healthy", "be faithful" and "don't harm the fair name of the Corps", the 1000 youths (average age of 22), composing the Farm Volunteer Corps recently formed of young farmers from Taihoku, Shinchiku, Taichu, Tainan and Takao Provinces, sailed from Keelung on an undisclosed date (April 25) on an undisclosed ship(either the Osaka Shosen Kaisha's Chosa Maru or a naval vessel). The group arrived in Shanghai on the 27th at 11 A.M., disembarked at 4 P.M. and arrived at their undisclosed destination (in the environs of Shanghai) at 7 P.M.
