REVOLUTION BY USE OF FORCE AND RETURN TO CHINA PLOTTED IN TAIWAN—TAKING ADVANTAGE OF THE CRITICAL SITUATION.
WHOLE PICTURE OF THE SECRET SOCIETY “SHUYU KAI” (MASS FRIENDS SOCIETY) INCIDENT.

In September, 1934 a plot to return Taiwan to China by use of force under assistance of China was unearthed in Taiko-gun, Taichu-shu and Takao before execution of the plot. The leader of the plot was one Sai Shuku Kai of Kiyomizu-gai, Taiko-gun, Taichu-shu, who was graduated from the Peking University, was a member of the Chinese Nationalists, and Governor of Tukuka Prefecture, China. The plot was spread and instigated by the secret society called “Shuyu Kai” whose leader was Sai Shuku Kai. This society was disguised as a religious society. Concrete preparations were going on for obtaining funds for revolution, secret manufacture of rifles and bullets, etc. This was a very serious case, and press ban was enforced. Arrests were made most confidentially, and after two years and a month the case was released to the press by the Procurators' Office on the 18th at 10:00 P.M. The press release was as follows, which clarifies the whole picture of the incident.

Several hundred fellow members acquired under guise of religious organization.

One Bo So So (alias So Cho So) who was a fortune teller in the compound of the Shiunji Temple in the Kannon Byo (Kannon Temple) in Kiyomizu, Taiko-gun, Taichu-shu (who died on October 14, 1934) had enthusiasm in racial awakening and was opposed to the administration of the Governor General of Taiwan. He plotted disturbance in Taiwan under assistance of China, overthrow of the Taiwan Government General by use of force, exclusion of Japanese rule from Taiwan, and restoration of Taiwan to Chinese administration. Since 1925 or 1926 he had been seeking co-workers. In November, 1927, he became acquainted with defendants, Chin Hatsu Shin and Chin So Kai, Chinese citizens, who stayed in the said temple and were engaged in sculpture of buddha images. They had common interest in the plot, and in the middle of December of that year they organized the secret society “Shuyu Kai”. They organized in various villages of Taiwan religious organizations called “Fubo Kai” (Parents Society). They planned to gradually convert members of this society into co-workers. From March or April of 1928, Bo So So caused the following named defendants establishment of the Parents Society in the following places:

Defendants Oh Bun and Oh Sui To established Tanshi “Jifubo Kai” in Tanshikan, Sharoku-sho, Taiko-gun.

Defendants So Gi, etc. established “Chikurin Fubokai” in Chikurin, Sharoku-sho, Taiko-gun.

Accidentally defendant Sai Shuku Kai, who is a native of Kiyomizu, Kiyomizu-gai, graduated from the Economic Department of the Peiping University, and who has the career of a member of the Chinese Nationalists and the Governor of Toku Ka Prefecture (Tek Kwa Prefecture) returned to Taiwan in the latter part of November, 1929 and met Bo So So and found the plot of the above mentioned “Shuyu Kai”. Sai Shuku Kai was enthusiastic in racial awakening, and regarding the suffering of the life of five million population of Taiwan from exploitation and oppression under the administration of the Japanese Empire intolerable, he cherished restoration of Taiwan to China, fatherland, by armed revolution under assistance of China. Sai Shuku Kai and Bo So So found a congenial friends in each other. Subsequently, Sai Shuku Kai acted as the leader of the Shuku Kai, and directed Bo So So and other influential co-workers regarding the method of instigating racial awakening, method of acquiring co-workers, necessity of carrying out disturbance, and opportunity and method of such disturbance. On the other hand he informed important men of China of the above plot, and requested assistance in funds, arms, etc. when opportunity arrives. He regarded the so-called critical situation of 1935 and 1936 as the best chance for carrying out disturbance, and concentrated efforts in acquisition of co-workers and revolutionary fund. He obtained promise from some co-workers for a large fund, and already received several hundred yen. Especially Bo So So, together with defendants Kyo No Oh, Ri Bai, Bo Ko Sai, etc., influential co-workers, was busily engaged in preparations for plans for secret manufacture of rifles and bullets and other preparations for carrying out of disturbance, as well as concrete works such as organizations, etc. at the time of carrying out of the disturbance. During these preparations the case was unearthed.

Over 400 persons were arrested. 25 were decided guilty.

The Procurators' Office and police authorities had arrested several times since September, 1934, all suspects in the case, and subsequently made

Entered Taichu High School in 1919; left the school in 1922 and went to China; studied in high school and Preparatory Department in the Peiping University; failed to pass physical examination in attempt to enter Koho Military and Political College in November, 1925 (had interest in Chinese revolution under Three People Principles); returned to Peiping University and graduated therefrom (Economics Department) in July, 1927; joined Chinese Nationalists while in Peiping University; after graduation became one of the leaders of the Organizing Committee of the Nationalists in Fukien; teacher in a commercial school in Fukien; Governor of Tek Kwa Prefecture, Fukien, in December, 1928; resigned in October, 1929 due to illness and returned to Taiwan.

The motive for his plot of the revolution was that while studying the Peiping University he entered the Nationalist, and responded to one of the platform of the Nationalists “To plan independence of own country, and at the same time to help weak and small races and to plan their independence and emancipation”. He met So So with whom he was acquainted from childhood. When their conversations incidentally touched the question of Taiwan revolution they found congenial friend in each other. They swore and promised that they would work for promotion of campaign for revolution of Taiwan. Following the example of the late Sun Yat Sen who utilized the influence of “Ka Ro Kai” in revolutionary movements of China, he decided to enlarge the “Shuyu Kai” planned by So So as an actual influence in the revolutionary movement. In order to attain his object on the basis of this society he became the leader of the “Shuyu Kai”.
