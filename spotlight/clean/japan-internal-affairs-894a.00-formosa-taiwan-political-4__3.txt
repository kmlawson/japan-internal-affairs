SUBJECT: Reported Uprising of Savages in Taiwan.

THE HONORABLE
THE SECRETARY OF STATE,
WASHINGTON.

SIR:

I have the honor to inform the Department that another uprising of savages in Taiwan has been reported, although on the day following the outbreak the Taiwan Government General [illegible] to deny the rumor.

According to the press, certain savages of [illegible] village near Heito in Takao Prefecture, under the impression that their land was to be taken from them, rebelled and attacked various police patrols in and near this village with [illegible] loss of life. Though no details were made available, it was further reported that a number of high police officials were ordered to the scene of the alleged rebellion and that the Government General was consulted as to the steps to be taken in smoothing out the trouble.

The various official centers, the Taiwan Government General, the Takao Government, the Heito County Office, issued catagorical denials of trouble, stating that some person had repeated the history of a former uprising as a present occurance. Nevertheless it is learned that preparations against attack were made.
