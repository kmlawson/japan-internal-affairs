According to a statement issued by the Government General of Formosa, the major cause of the discontent which led to the revolt at Musha last November was the policy of the government in requisitioning aborigines in the work of transporting timber for the new school buildings at that place. The natives, being very proud of their ferocity, have a natural horror of manual labor, and resented being made to carry timber on their backs. Another of the causes was the personal resentment of two of the leaders, members of whose families had been executed by the authorities for head hunting.

On January [21st?] Governor-General Ishidzuka of Formosa called upon Premier Hamaguchi and Foreign Minister Baron Shidehara and signified his intention of resigning, due to his responsibility for the uprising. It is reported that Mr. Hitomi, Vice Governor-General of Formosa, has also resigned.
